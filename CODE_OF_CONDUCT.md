# Code of conduct

Parallax is a small project with one simple rule: be the collaborator you would want to work with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Criticise the work, never the person.
- Stay on topic: the kit, its pipelines, its docs and the games made with it.
- Give context in issues and pull requests: what you ran, what you expected and what happened. The kit's
  `summary:` and `next_action:` lines are the quickest way to show it.
- Only contribute what you have the right to share. Third-party material is vetted and recorded in
  [VETLOG.md](VETLOG.md) and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); new material needs the same.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated projects.
- Content that breaks Roblox's Community Standards, or that you do not have the rights to.

## Reporting

To report a conduct problem, open an issue that asks for a private contact, and leave the details out of it.
Security problems go through the repository's **Security** tab, using **Report a vulnerability**
([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block anyone who
keeps breaking it.
