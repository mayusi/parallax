The effect is ONE Dance strike (~2 s in game); these are the scene's beats it is built from.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

What a build from memory would get wrong: a soft white/yellow glow blob (the scene is SATURATED orange-red with a thin
white edge), a thin flat slash (the arc is THICK with a ragged burning rim), and a symmetric clean arc (the rim tears into
tongues). The fire is drawn anime-style: hard-edged flat flame shapes, not volumetric smoke.
