# orig - a selftest original: a brief that passes every section check, so only the REAL-frames lock can fail it

## Concept
A test bloom of light that opens on the ground, pulses once and lets go of its seeds into the night air.

## Shape language
A point opening into a disc, one pulse outward, many small points lifting and curling away.

## Palette
- petal: [240, 236, 255]
- seed: [230, 170, 70]

## Beats
| Beat | Time | What happens | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| open | 0.0-1.0 s | the disc opens on the ground | a flat growth sprite |
| pulse | 1.0-1.2 s | one pulse of light runs through it | a light, a kick |
| release | 1.2-3.0 s | seeds lift off and drift away | a swarm with curl |

## Techniques
- a flat growth sprite, a light pulse, a curling swarm

## Impact style
No impact frames: the pulse is the strike, drawn by the light alone.

## Dissipation
The seeds die into glints one by one while the disc drains from its rim inward.

## It is NOT
Not a magic circle, not an explosion, not a re-skin of moonflower.
