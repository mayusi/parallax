# Lessons — screens / GUI

- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): A negative pixel offset with a bottom anchor (pos [24, -24], anchor bottomleft) puts the element ABOVE the screen. Use pos [24, "100%-24"].
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Keep buttons, inputs and image buttons out of the top-left 260x58: Roblox's menu/chat buttons sit on top of IgnoreGuiInset screens (the preview tints it red).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): A UIGradient tints everything its parent draws, text included: a gradient-backed button with its text on it goes dark. The compiler moves that text into a child Label; Ui.text(node, ...) finds it.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Good game UI comes from a design system, not hex codes: pick a skin, use styles (card/primary), slots (ammo bottomRight, feed topLeft) and components (ring, scope, keyhint). The flat-panel / type-hierarchy / centre-blocking warnings exist because those are exactly what made the first HUD look like 2014.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): An identity pack decided BEFORE any screen (tokens measured from references, an Enum.Font trio, a shape language for drawn plates, the motion signature, a ban list) is what stops a generated UI from looking generated; a screen that starts from a skin converges on the template.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Roblox cannot take a custom TTF (2026): choose from Enum.Font. The preview draws the real faces (kit ui fonts fetches the Google Fonts twin, sha256-pinned); Roblox's own Garamond is the Guru face and Gotham / Builder Sans are proprietary, so fonts.json marks their twins approx.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): The engine has no letter-spacing: a tracked label (tracking 0.3) compiles to one AutomaticSize-X label per glyph in a centred horizontal list and Ui.text rebuilds the glyphs. A plain TextButton keeps the engine's grey background unless a colour is set, so the compiler makes colourless buttons transparent; a plate-backed button carries its text in a child Label.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Item icons: kit vision concept '<game> style item icon: ...' --sheet single (FLUX.2 klein, about 22 s, 8 GB), kit vision cutout --rgba, crop to the alpha bbox, square with 6 percent padding at 256 px into assets/ui/art/<identity>/icon_<name>.png; kit ui takes them in as art:<identity>/icon_<name>. Six Souls icons were usable on their first seed.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): A simulator / casual identity is the chunky family: shape.width 5 + gloss + shade + shadow draw bubble/pill/badge/card/banner per colour token (shape.buttons) and card_<tier> per rarity token; a panel or imagebutton with plate + shadow IS the bubble button (icon + outlined label inside), counters roll short (1.2K), bars take track + fillGloss, enters bounce, icons float, tags wobble. The preview backdrop follows the identity's world words (sunny/toy = sky + meadow) — a navy scrim token no longer makes the preview a night scene.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Text on a plate: the compiler takes the plate's colour as the contrast ground (bubble_good = that token, a card or frame = shape.fill), so dark text on a cream pill passes and white text on a green bubble is an error until it carries textOutline (the genre draws every white word with a dark outline anyway).
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Cartoon icon sheets come out usable on the first seed from FLUX.2 klein with the suffix 'a glossy cartoon mobile-game icon with a thick dark outline, bright saturated colours, a soft top highlight, Roblox pet simulator style, clean vector look' (18 of 18 kept, 20-28 s each); run the batch while the GPU is idle, cut out with BiRefNet, crop to the alpha box with 8 percent margin at 256 px.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Both fresh judges of the first simulator pack scored every screen 51-66 for one reason: scale — labels, prices, buttons and slots far under the genre's measured sizes, panels with dead space. Three kit gates now catch it before a judge: the identity's minimums (text / button / icon floors measured from the references), the preview's empty-panel check (a grounded panel under half covered), and a ruler on every judge-packet tile (the sheet draws desktop at 50 percent — judges were reading sheet pixels as screen pixels).
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): A model that never looks at its sheet ships what the gates allow, not what a player sees. The kit now makes looking a stage: kit ui look <Name> "<what you saw>" is tied to the build's sha, and kit ui judge / kit promote refuse a build nobody looked at. Say what the eye lands on first too ("focus") — the compiler checks it is the biggest text, which is the judge's hierarchy score measured before the judge.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Phone truth: the identity floors are pre-scale, so a 16 px label at phone scale 0.5 draws at 8 px; the preview now measures the drawn size on the phone view (under 11 px is flagged with the path). Judge over the real game: a screenshot saved as assets/ui/art/<identity>/world.png replaces the painted backdrop, and a reference with "image" lands in the packet's refs.png beside the screens.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Measure from the picture, and check the picture of the picture: a contact sheet that pastes crops at an offset turns (1640,48) into (1640,228) — a fresh judge with refs.png caught the 180 px error. Keep the reference at 1:1 (hud_*_crops.png) and write the crop origins into REFS.md. Also: kit upload recompiles the models (asset ids) and used to invalidate a fresh packet — looks and verdicts are now tied to the sheet's pixels as well, and the packet should be made AFTER the upload anyway.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Replicating a real HUD: the first build from memory judged 47-51 with every gap a difference from the still (a ligature not three letters, words stacked, a drawn energy ring not a glow, a demon-face orb icon, the band under the DT slots); the second build was made element by element against the 1:1 crops. The preview's textGradient draws its FIRST stop: put the body colour first and the dark shade second, or the preview shows a pale sticker.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Why kit UIs read as AI slop even with references, floors, focus and judges: every piece the compiler makes is a font glyph with a flat tint, a PIL rectangle or a generated blob — the medium is wrong. A real HUD is painted art. kit ui render (Blender Cycles, ~10 s a piece) renders chrome-bevelled logotypes and lit plates from a .render.json; the character-action rank went from flat yellow text to bevelled gold with a black edge in one render. Render the hero pieces; type only the numbers that change.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): An ornate gauge HUD is three layers, not one drawing: a RENDERED plate (troughs, wells, caps — kit ui render 'gauge' item, which also writes the wells' pixel anchors), LIVE cells laid over the wells (slant plates tinted with the fill token, generated by kit ui gauge from a 12-line .gauge.json), and GENERATED ornament (emblem, pips via kit vision concept). Measure the rows on the reference sheet first (green-pixel scan: rows 412/204 px long at 760 px width -> cells, lengths, lean). A dark steel plate reads silver in a thumbnail because of its lit bevel rims — probe the PNG's pixels before 'fixing' the material.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Worn steel beats clean steel for a game plate: a noise bump + grime ramp in the roughness and base (render 'wear'), rims on the troughs, exposure ~0.3 and world 0.1 — a flat-lit slab reads pale silver however dark its base. Live cells read as glass when they are three slants: a dark edge, a bright inset, a 38 percent white shine. Pips at reference scale are ~56x66 px at 1080p with the unlit ones as dark metal (alpha 0.15, tinted line2), not faded ghosts.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): A 1:1 replica of a HUD from a picture is traced, not generated: kit ui trace crops the reference at 1:1, upscales x4 (Real-ESRGAN), cuts out (BiRefNet) and, for a gauge plate, blanks the green cells to empty wells and anchors them; kit ui gauge with from_trace lays every piece where the picture has it. Text-prompted emblems scored 40-52 as 'a different design'; the traced gear gauge is the reference's own crest, troughs, caps and pips with live cells. Prompts are for ORIGINAL art; references are for replicas. Two tool traps found on the way: a cap's green glint reads as a 6 px cell (filter < 55 percent of the median width) and the preview drew Fit images as squares (fixed: aspect kept).
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Traced gauges scored 56-62 (from 45-52 generated); both judges' first gap was the reference's background riding along behind the plate — trace every piece with --cutout (BiRefNet keeps a 58 percent matte on a plate crop) and keep the boxes generous (a tight box cut a pip strip's blade and a name's tail). The live cells should be the reference's own cell too: trace saves <plate>_cell.png (the first lit cell with its frame) and kit ui gauge stretches it over each well.
- 2026-10-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Judges cannot size a HUD: on the same five traced gauges one pair read the crest as 1.4x too big and the next pair (after a 0.74 scale) as 1.25x too small — and the scores moved within noise (57-67). Scale from the MEASURED crest (kit ui gauge crest_width, from REFS.md), then stop re-scaling. Nine judge rounds on traced gauges plateau at 57-67 because the SOURCE is a 760x940 gallery sheet (cells 42x13 px, crests 176 px before the x4 upscale): the resolution of the reference is the ceiling of a trace, so get 1080p/4K stills per state before tracing a hero HUD.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A rendered gauge plate reads pale whatever its base colour when the frame is a mirror metal: Cycles reflects the area lights. For dark game steel use frame {base ~0.1, roughness 0.4}, exposure ~0.12 (measure: the plate's mean RGB under ~90 of 255), and wear_scale ~50 for a brushed grain (the default 18 reads as marble blotches at HUD size). A text render can carry a 1-2 px hairline across the whole picture (a glyph's open contour); the render now clears it (despeckle: erode 2, re-grow 4, keep only there).
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A rendered glass cell (gem item) clips to white under the logotype lights whatever its emission: the hot strip reflects in a coat 0.7 / roughness 0.12 glass. Measure the centre pixel (max channel under 245) and tune coat ~0.25, roughness ~0.3, exposure ~0.03, glow ~0.9 — the colour then comes from the emission, not the lights; the unlit cell at the same exposure reads as smoked glass (~25/255).
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): When a holder is curved, everything inside it must follow the curve: the owner caught level rectangular cells sitting in a bowed bar ('is it that hard to understand'). Rule for any rendered frame with live children: the render writes each slot's tangent/scale into its anchors and the screen places children by their centre and rotates them to it. Never lay axis-aligned children into a warped parent.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A hero HUD is art assembled by data. The order that worked (character-action gauges, Umbra gauge, 2026-10-11): real stills measured at 1:1 into REFS.md -> identity from those numbers -> every piece rendered (kit ui render: text/ring/gauge/gem) or traced (kit ui trace), painted motifs from FLUX cut out -> assembled from anchors (kit ui gauge; crest_width sets the scale) -> a look with sampled numbers at every milestone -> two fresh judges, median, gaps in the order they agree -> promote with the data. Every skipped step cost a round. `kit ui plan <Screen>` is this list as a stage board.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Judge pairs are for the GAPS, not the number: consecutive pairs read one build as 1.4x too big then 1.25x too small; the Umbra gauge sat at 45-49.5 while the owner called it 'genuinely impressed'. Fix what two judges agree on, measure sizes from REFS.md, never re-scale or re-judge on one judge's word, and let the owner's eye outrank the median.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Original art route timings (RTX 5070): FLUX concept 22 s, BiRefNet cutout 10-30 s, a Blender gauge plate 15-20 s, a gem 10 s, a full kit ui build ~1 min, a judge pair ~1 min. An original gauge from two stills to a judged screen: ~70 min; each owner correction ~25 min. The image budget (10 views/hour across judges) paced the work more than the GPU: crop tight, compose contact sheets, sample pixels by script.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): What the kit now checks for you (added 2026-10-11, act on next_action): the render reads its picture back (a gem that clips to white, a pale gauge plate, a clipped logotype, an empty render); the compiler warns on a hero screen with no art; kit ui gauge lays cells by their well's tangent; kit ui plan has refs and pieces stages; promote carries render/trace/anchors/gauge/REFS data.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A role's font was resolved only as an identity font KEY (theme[role.font]); a face name in a role ('rankWord': PermanentMarker, 'script': Fondamento) fell back to the body face silently — the character-action rank words and the Arcade5 'Stylish' both shipped in the wrong face until a judge crop showed it. - 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Borrowing a game's GRAMMAR (a stylized anime RPG) for another world (Heroine) works when the grammar becomes plates and rules, not pictures: torn/torn_black/halftone/rays plates + 'nothing level, nothing alone' (lean -6..-12, overlap, paper white under every cutout, one accent). Judges' first reads of the attempt: a square-clipped ray tile, hex-honeycomb halftone, level isolated islands, photoreal icons beside flat cutouts, the callout out-shouting the rank — each fixed by a plate or a rule, 53.5 -> 56 in one round; keep the hero piece the biggest chrome object.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A Persona-type title screen's reads, from two judge pairs: a flat axis-aligned region (a sky that stops at 72%) reads as a 'level panel behind everything' — run the texture full-bleed and give the menu its own leaning torn slab; a list of cutouts must be a PILE: three torn shapes (torn / torn_b / torn_c), leans that differ by 6-10 degrees, overlaps of 20+ px, the selected one bigger in the accent; a bevelled chrome logotype reads as WordArt — a custom mirror metal (roughness ~0.06, a hard horizon band, outline 0.1) reads as Heroine chrome; a FLUX silhouette needs a pose prompt (gun-kata, hair flaring, heels, no glasses) — the first take was clip art.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): The judge sheet drew desktop + phone only; four judge pairs in a row docked 'no tablet view' and could not check the tablet reflow. The packet now pastes the tablet tile under the phone tile (kit/ui/judge.py stack: tablet crops). When every judge repeats the same complaint about the PACKET, fix the packet, not the screen.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): When the owner says a screen 'looks bad', the answer is more REAL references of that exact screen type, not more polish: the first Arcade5 title was a Persona-flavoured collage; measuring the actual P5 title still (field 35% lower-left, edge falling right, logo over the edge, character huge, menu as a diagonal staircase on the black side, selected on a big splash) gave the structure in one rebuild. - 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): imageAlpha on an image / plate node is TRANSPARENCY (0 = opaque, 1 = invisible): a 'faint' crest set to 0.12 was 88 percent opaque and a judge called it 'full brightness colliding with the menu'. For a faint backdrop use 0.8-0.9; for a halftone wash 0.5-0.65. Check the compiled model's ImageTransparency when a backdrop looks louder than intended.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Every chrome logotype (character-action rank, WITCH TIME, rank S, UMBRA) carried a mid-grey slab behind it that three judge pairs called 'a grey card / WordArt plate': the renderer's OUTLINE shell was a 0.6-metallic material reflecting the key light. An outline is ink: base 0, metallic 0, roughness 1, specular 0 (fixed in kit/ui/render.py outline_material). When judges repeat a complaint the designer 'does not see', measure the art: 70k opaque mid-grey pixels in the logo PNG said it before the judges did.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A 3D render through the halftone ink route is a scribble: the suit is all ink and the mid-tones dissolve into dots (the owner's Heroine render). kit ui ink --mode cel flattens the picture's own colours to a few flat fills with grown TEED lines; --palette mono (black, two greys, white, a paper rim + an ink rim) is the P5 figure that sits ON the accent field — the identity palette (violet on violet) vanished there. - 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Ransom lettering is for one word (a header): a LIST in ransom letters was unreadable and the owner called the menu bad. A list is whole-word strips (stacked / per-item torn plates, alternating ink and paper, each leaning, stepping right) — the Persona command stair. Readability first, then grammar.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Composition gates must count plates: a user plate is marked kit after expansion (taste checks skip it), so the accent field read as 0% and the title passed. Count authored nodes, clip boxes to the screen, weigh a piece by its painted share (plates.py writes opaque into art.json): the same title then read 96% accent, which was the truth the judges had been saying.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A 3D render is the wrong SOURCE for a Persona-type figure however it is filtered (halftone ink = scribble, cel-mono = splat): the owner called every version horrible. - 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Torn plates (per-point jitter) read as noise next to Persona's SCISSOR cuts (a few long straight runs, sharp corners): the cut family (cut, cut_b, cut_c, cut_black, cut_field, cut_strip; plates.py _cut_pts) is the P5 grammar; torn is for paper scraps only. - 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Chrome / gold / grey crest / violet / white on one Persona-type screen = five materials where the still has three colours; the chrome logotype read as WordArt. kit ui letter (flat paper lettering, ink outline, hard offset drop, a lean; glyph cards for ransom) is the title logo and shout words of that grammar; kit ui render chrome is for the character-action / Heroine metal world only.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Persona's look is in the LETTERING: each glyph a different face, mixed case, tilted, overlapping, one thick ink outline round the whole word and a thin paper rim (kit ui letter --style persona). - 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Display type is a pipeline step, not a hand-run command: write text with "lettering" (true or overrides) and the kit ui words step bakes art:<identity>/w_<slug> from the identity's "lettering" defaults, re-baking only when a recipe changes (the round the owner rated 'genuinely amazed' converted to this was pixel-identical). Baked words keep their desktop pixels: give each group one at.phone/tablet scale (it shrinks the node and its children) and LOOK at phone + tablet — the first lettered build collided on both screens and only the promote gate (no look, no promote) caught it.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Measure before claiming a gate is wrong: the purple-accent tell fired on arcade5 and looked like a false alarm, but the Heroine references' dominant violet measured #4f47b2 and arcade5 uses an invented #a24dff — the tell was right. An identity can silence it only with accent_evidence naming one of its own references (the compiler checks the name is listed); never write that line without the measurement.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Never carry an owner verdict onto a changed build: kit ui plan closes the judge stage on an owner rating only when its model sha matches the current build; an earlier-build rating shows as 'the owner rated an EARLIER build' with the next step 'show the owner this one'.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A realistic-shooter HUD is the opposite grammar of a stylized one: no plates, no fields, colour only as a signal (amber objective, blue friend, red damage), white condensed type with a soft dark edge. Gather refs and you find the genre's store shots have NO HUD; use those as the preview backdrop (identity art world.png) — a sunlit frame with an explosion is the hardest legibility case. Measure the one HUD still you have at 1:1 (mag numerals ~45 px tall, reserve ~23, name ~18, a 6 px bar) and size the type from it: the first build sized the numeral from a role guess and drew ~30 px, both judges' first gap.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): HUD icons come from REAL side views through kit ui ink --mode icon (outline + TEED interior lines + faint fill + a dark halo): the kit's own 3D builds (build/renders/*_side*) are the best source — original, measured, owner-approved. Make a second outline-only LOD (--lines none, thicker outline) for small uses: interior lines turn into a tangle at 50 px.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): Judges read 'everything except the hero block is generic' when secondary blocks are bare text: give each block one structural cue the genre uses — kill-feed rows on faint strips with your kill edged, squad rows with per-mate health bars, armour as plate segments, spent rounds hollow. Two pairs moved 60/65 -> 62/67 on the hero numeral alone; the structure round is what lifts the rest.
- 2026-10-11 (mayusi, Parallax): 'Realistic' is a HUD that reports only what the player cannot feel: the owner read a full arcade checklist (minimap, gun-icon kill feed, squad list, health number, plates, round count, crosshair, grey boxes) dressed in muted type as AI slop — and the references had said so (Insurgency / Ready or Not / Hell Let Loose frames show almost no HUD). When the references show an ABSENCE, the absence is the design.
