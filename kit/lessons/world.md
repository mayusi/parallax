# Lessons — levels / maps

- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Ring generator: gaps must be fewer than segments; openings are spread evenly. Check build/worlds/<Name>.png from above before playtesting.
- 2026-10-13 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world check models R6 movement as a graph on 1-stud voxels: a gap jump is not 'half the jump height at any distance' — use the real arc (v = sqrt(2 g J), g = 196.2, 16 st/s across: full J up to ~4.3 studs out, ~8.7 studs flat). Unreachable warnings must ignore thin strips (wall tops under 5 studs wide) and name each platform with bounds + height, or they cry wolf on every ring wall.
- 2026-10-13 (mayusi, Parallax): Web shaders on Windows (ANGLE -> HLSL): a loop around an expensive function, or texture2D (derivatives) inside a loop, is UNROLLED - kit world web's terrain shader took 42 s to compile. Fix: textureLod(...,0.) in loops, ONE call site for the expensive function (weights in a cheap loop, then surface() twice for the two strongest), fewer noise octaves, renderer.compileAsync. Time programs alone with gl.compileShader on a blank page before guessing.
- 2026-10-13 (mayusi, Parallax): Rojo 7.7 model JSON: a Content property (ParticleEmitter.Texture) must be the IMPLICIT string "rbxasset://..." - the explicit {"Content": ...} form fails the whole build with 'Invalid JSON structure'. Bisect a property with a one-part test project.
- 2026-10-13 (mayusi, Parallax): A backdrop mountain range built from long ridges reads as a WALL from the map (a 2 km dead-straight crest under haze looks like a flat-topped block). Peaks = two SHORT ridges crossed at right angles, the crossing one lower and off-centre, plus far land under them (horizon floor) - and keep them lower than the hero landmark (Erdtree) so they frame instead of compete.
- 2026-10-14 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world shots: never judge 'the browser died' by msedge.exe's process (Popen.poll) - the launcher hands the page to another process and exits at once. And a far-terrain ring's middle ends near 0 (the designer shifts its minimum to 0): sink the op with its center y or it covers the map's lakes. Water lit by coloured light turns muddy (teal x desert orange): light its body by the light's strength, keep its own hue.
- 2026-10-14 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world shots stalls (1 view in 480 s on an idle GPU) are headless Edge on Windows: msedge hands the page to a child and exits, so killing the launcher leaves the renderer drawing the 3D page forever (GPU contention), and a hidden headless page can stop getting requestAnimationFrame. Fixed in shots.py/web_template: kill by profile dir, timers instead of rAF in ?shots=1. Playwright's Chromium measures the page truth (240 fps) when in doubt.
- 2026-10-14 (mayusi, Parallax): Terrain that reads as real comes from RIVERS, not noise: thermal erosion alone makes talus-angle cones; stream-power incision (two scales) gives branching valleys + sharp ridges; the painter colours from flow/deposit/curvature. Erosion moves the min height -> pin a design anchor (the lake bed) and keep basin/plateau(+edge)/ramp(+shoulders), or lakes go dry and castles become traps (kit world check catches the trap).
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Blender cameras clip at 100 units by default: a horizon landmark 2000 studs away silently never renders (looked like 'haze ate it'). Set cam.clip_end >= 20000 for any vista frame; kit/world/frame_blender.py does.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Organic meshes (roots, bark, ropes) must not use the castle atlas's per-face random tile windows: every face picks its own window and the surface reads as a checkerboard. Use ONE tiling texture with continuous UVs (u around, v along, wrapping) - castle_blender.bark_texture + Mesh.quad_uv.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Direction frames: a backlit (sun behind the subject) shot leaves the face black at mean ~0.1; add a camera-side bounce light (fill, 2-5x the key on a dusk sky) and measure the face's mean luminance (aim 0.2-0.4) before looking - it saves image budget.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Image-similarity numbers (edge correlation, colour distance) did NOT separate a rejected set of direction frames from a good one; make divergence a THINKING check instead (each direction's Axes differ on >= 2: kit direction).
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Direction frames: seat far pieces on the terrain you actually built (rock heights are not their size parameter: decimation and displacement change them) - towers floated above cliffs until moved down by eye; check the far plateau in a crop.
- 2026-10-27 (mayusi, Parallax): Runnable walls must be FLUSH: a 1.5 recess between piers snagged a wall-hugging R6 on the next pier edge, and any shelf (a dado top) ends a wall run as a landing. Do relief as non-colliding inlays >= 8 tall (the wall-run ray accepts them) over one collision plane.
- 2026-10-28 (mayusi, Parallax): Roblox smooth terrain draws a flat surface 2 studs ABOVE the top of its full voxels (FillBlock 100..104 reads 106; WriteVoxels: surface = partial voxel's bottom + 2 + 4*occupancy). Write heightmaps 2 lower, or a terrain 'ground at 0' covers part floors at 1 (it hid both SampleGame arena floors).
- 2026-10-28 (mayusi, Parallax): Decor on a run wall must be out of raycasts, not just non-colliding: a ray-based wall run (side + diagonal rays, normal check) hits a thin inlay's SIDE and reads a sideways wall - SampleGame's run broke into 0.14-1.0 s pieces; with CanQuery off one 2.22 s run. Use "query": false on collide:false parts.
- 2026-10-28 (mayusi, Parallax): Scanned rock-face meshes are open shells: aim the scan's surface (its mean normal, +Z for Poly Haven rock_face_01 after kit import) DOWNHILL; aimed into the hill you see the open back as pale slivers. Pale granite scans under moonlight read as snow: wear a dark copy of their own texture (luminance kept, hue dropped).
- 2026-10-28 (mayusi, Parallax): Pushing big build data into a place that has HTTP off and no kit runtime: carry it in through Rojo as StringValue chunks (180k chars) + the builder as a ModuleScript inside a synced model.json, run phases one MCP call each (a 250-high terrain clear over 10 km times the call out but finishes), then sync the clean model back.
- 2026-10-28 (mayusi, Parallax): Roblox Future lighting drops shadow-casting lights past a budget: a district with 121 shadowed lamp PointLights rendered pitch dark (lamps glowing, nothing lit); the same lamps with Shadows off lit the street at once. Many lamps = Shadows false; keep shadows for a handful of hero lights.
- 2026-10-28 (mayusi, Parallax): Procedural Blender meshes for Roblox: give every face UVs projected on ITS OWN plane (studs / tile), bake the in-game placement scale into them, and pick the tile for the body scale (stone 8 = 2-stud courses; 16 gave 4-stud 'giant blocks' beside a 5-stud R6). Dominant-axis world UVs smear every moulding, roof and pinnacle into stripes - the owner's 'texture issues'. Audit: UV-area / face-area = (scale/tile)^2 per face.
- 2026-10-31 (mayusi, Parallax): A long Studio build run from execute_luau in a task.spawn keeps going after the call returns, so its changes land in whichever later MCP call's undo waypoint ('Assistant N') is open: two Ctrl+Z in Edit silently removed the City of Ruins' last 242 meshes + all 326 parts (2026-10-31). Wrap clear + build in ONE ChangeHistoryService recording and end with ResetWaypoints(), then count every folder against the compile
- 2026-11-10 (mayusi, Parallax): A world's R6 fit cannot be checked from hand-written claims alone: the kit's own generators built below the owner's rig and nothing saw it - building floors of 12 (11 clear: 'a room a head too low', his District 6/10), bridge rails of 2.2 (the knee), lamps every 24 studs. Each generator now CLAIMS what it builds (every floor's clear ceiling, the door as an opening, the stair step, the bridge rail, a roofed room's ceiling and doorways) and a new world names its rig sheet ("scale": "r6", the world lock asks), so kit world check holds generated geometry to the sheet. Give generator claims MIN-only kinds (ceiling h >= 12, opening 4.5 x 7.5): a great hall or a castle gate is legitimate and a max flags every one. Do not claim window sills (a high sill is a choice) or a roofless yard's ceiling, and claim the top floor's whole height under a flat roof (none under a gable). Bad example: kit/selftest/world/_selftest_vaelmouth.json (5 errors).
- 2026-11-10 (mayusi, Parallax): 'A lamp everywhere' (the owner on the District, 2026-10-28) is crowding, not a count or a spacing: measure the median lamp's neighbours within 80 studs, with lamp/lantern parts and meshes merged within 4 studs so a post, its head and its light count once; 3 or more is the fault (kit world check design:). A street lit on both sides at 90 studs has 1 (its opposite) and passes; the row generator's old 24-stud default had 11 and SampleCity has 10. A plain count fails big lit cities and a spacing rule fails two-sided streets. The row generator's lamp default is now 90.
- 2026-11-10 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world look must show the meshes where Roblox puts them: fit the FBX template's OWN imported box (every object of it) to the part's Size, centre it on the CFrame, and undo export_fbx's 180-degree Z pre-turn (Roblox's importer turns the FBX back). The registry's min is the un-turned authoring box: placing by it put every off-centre mesh twice its offset away (SampleRegion's Erdtree root ~152 studs, the Stormwall 24) while the centred trees looked right - so a picture of trees on the ground proved nothing. look_blender.py now reads every placed mesh back against its part's box and fails the run over 0.1 studs (SampleRegion: 1180 meshes to 0.000).
- 2026-11-10 (mayusi, Parallax): Show the owner a world in HIS goal and say what to score: SampleRegion at eye level (a sunny meadow of trees and rocks, a far castle) got no score, 2026-11-11: 'I don't know what I'm supposed to be rating here ... if you did take a hint ... from what I was making in Country Yard, you'd understand what my goal is' (no 'Country Yard' exists; he means City of Ruins / the District's courtyard). Pick a place that shows those at eye level, and name what to score: the theme, whole detailed buildings up close, scale against his rig, placement.
- 2026-11-11 (mayusi, Parallax): A direction frame must read to the owner at a glance. The GothicCourtyard test frame (2026-11-11) - a wide-angle block-out looking UP at a lit flank, unlabelled - got REDO: 'I don't know what I'm exactly looking at ... I'm just gonna give it, a bad'. Show a direction the way he plays: from where a player stands (Roblox's default third-person camera behind his R6 rig, about 12 studs back and 7.5 up, vertical FOV 70), with the rig in the shot for scale, plain-word labels on what he is looking at (the cathedral ahead, the houses you can walk into, the stair, each lamp), and the source's real frames beside ours, each pair labelled 'the source: ...' / 'ours: ...'. Then ask ONE plain question: is this the look you want (yes, or what to change)?
- 2026-11-11 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world look at night (kit/world/look_blender.py): a lamp's PointLight sits INSIDE its Neon head part, and in Blender's EEVEE that closed box shadows its own light completely - the first night render's lamps glowed but lit nothing. Roblox never lets a light's own part block it, so the look turns shadow casting off for every part that holds a light (object.visible_shadow = False) while the light keeps its shadows for everything else. Proof on a probe street: the wall beside each lamp 35 vs 23 between them. Any renderer of compiled worlds (frames, sheets) needs the same rule.
- 2026-11-11 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world look's moon is an approximation, not Roblox's: at a night clockTime it shines from the opposite azimuth to the look's fixed day sun (Blender 215 deg - from the north-west, travelling toward Roblox +z) at a height from the clock ((clock + 12) mod 24 on the sun's curve: clockTime 2.5 = about 55 deg). A facade meant to be moonlit in the look must face NORTH (-z): GothicCourtyard's cathedral was moved to the court's south side for this. Roblox's real moon (Lighting:GetMoonDirection) follows ClockTime and GeographicLatitude; check the facade in Studio before trusting the look's shadows.
- 2026-11-11 (mayusi, Parallax): What turned GothicCourtyard's morning REDO ('I don't know what I'm exactly looking at', an unlabelled block-out looking up a wall) into a KEEP the same day ('Keep it'): (1) the look source's REAL frames beside ours, each pair labelled in plain words ('the source: ...' / 'ours: ...') - he said yes to the look from that sheet, then 'Yes, build it'; (2) every view from where a player stands - Roblox's default camera behind his R6 rig, level, plain-word labels on what he sees (the cathedral ahead, the stair, a house you can walk into, each lamp, 'you'), plus a view inside a house; (3) real meshes over the blockout - gothic house skins with walk-in rooms, traceried bays, lampposts - made in the kit (kit world meshes) BEFORE the piece, so the piece itself took about 10 minutes of its hero hour. He kept it although told it read dark and plain beside Bloodborne: what he could read as his kind of place came before polish.
- 2026-11-12 (mayusi, Parallax): Blender's light is not Roblox's, and the look's Blender pane hid it: GothicCourtyard (night preset, exposure 0.5) read navy with every wall visible in kit world look, and in Roblox Studio (kit world studio, 2026-11-12) the same stations are near black outside three lamp pools and the brazier, while the candle-lit room is cold and dim in Blender and hot orange in Studio. Judge a world's LIGHT only in the Studio pane. And with nothing uploaded Studio has no mesh: kit world studio shows the blockout hidden under each mesh skin so the place has walls, but anything set INSIDE that blockout (the cathedral's lit lancets sit in its wall) cannot be seen until the meshes are uploaded - that pane judges layout and light, never carving or windows.
- 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): kit world check does not print the compiler's own warnings (z-fighting, part budget): a generated town passed it with 0 errors while World.compile warned of 240 faces in one plane. That warning also cries wolf on every building of two floors (a slab edge inside a wall, a lintel top under the floor above) and skips every part that is not axis-aligned (a wall run toward -X is one). So a generator's test reads World.compile's issues itself and compares the count with the same houses stood 1,000 studs apart in one compile: what the generator ADDS is the difference.
- 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): The building generator centres its walls on the footprint's edge. Two buildings whose footprints touch are ONE party wall built twice (faces in one plane inside both houses), and a street between them is a wall narrower than typed. A neighbour's footprint is its lot less half a wall all round: wall faces then stand on the lot's lines, party walls back to back, and kit world check measures the street at exactly its planned width. With trim keep a wall and a stud between footprints on EVERY side: the ridge beam reaches a wall and a half past the footprint, posts and bands less. A house floor a hair (0.1) over the pavement it stands on, never level with it (card my7f's flicker).
- 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): A building skin is checked by NUMBERS (footprint, storeys, door size, window run, window sides): nothing in the kit sees WHERE the mesh cut its door. The house mesh counted its bays from the west end of the south wall and the building generator from the east: with an even bay count (a 24-stud front has two) the mesh's door stood in the bay where the building keeps a window - solid stone in front of the walkable gap, a doorway onto an invisible wall. Odd counts hid it for weeks. To see it: one building with a bright glass material and its skin, rendered from the front (kit world look with a view) - a pane inside the mesh's doorway is the fault. The rule now lives in kit/world/housebays.py for both sides; a new mesh kind that takes openings reads it from there.
- 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): A width or clearance measured by SAMPLING a few heights misses whatever lies between them: my slanted-lane measure read three heights of the body band and walked through a 1-stud bench at y 1.6-2.6, so a lane failed on an axis and passed at 45 degrees. Meet each thing by its own shape (an upright box: where the line meets its footprint, if its height overlaps the band at all), and test one obstacle at several angles for ONE verdict
- 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): A town bent as rigid blocks: SETTLE the blocks pair by pair (each two across a street moved until their nearest points are the planned width apart), never spread them all from the centre by one factor. The spread kept every street at least as wide as planned and made a 12-stud lane 20.8 studs at its narrowest (bend 8, 24 towns); settled it is 12.5. Measure the widths a generator really makes before writing 'no street is narrower' as its only promise
- 2026-11-18 (mayusi, Parallax): A drainage sink on a slope that is only made base level (its cells pass no water on) CUTS LESS, not more: it stops the water already flowing through it and holds the land above at its height. An interior sink must also be LOW (laid at the land's base level) before the rivers run, and on the two-scale solver a sink smaller than a coarse cell must stay off the coarse pass (laid as a whole coarse cell it cut the slope above it less than no sink at all).
- 2026-11-18 (mayusi, Parallax): A z-fighting check that drops an overlap as 'buried' by probing a few points (the middle, near the corners) hides seen faces: a part over most of it leaves a visible rim, two parts with a strip between them cover every probe, and glass buries nothing. Probe a grid over the WHOLE overlap with its edges, count only opaque parts, and search neighbouring normal buckets (two faces either side of a rounding edge are one plane). Run it over every selftest fixture before wiring it as an error: the sloped town had 220 real pairs nobody had seen.
- 2026-11-18 (mayusi, Parallax): Every *.json directly in assets/worlds is read as a world blueprint - by kit world (it compiles each) and by the lane locks (LaneLocks pieces). A world's side file (its bible, a plan) goes in a subfolder (assets/worlds/bible/<Name>.json), never <Name>.bible.json beside it; build/worlds/ holds compiled worlds and nothing lists it, so a scratch copy for a render (<Name>.corner.json) may sit there - the look renderer finds the world's meshes from the file's own folder, so a copy elsewhere renders every mesh as missing.
- 2026-11-18 (mayusi, Parallax): A new check over the BUILT worlds is measured before it is made an error: z-fight, fixtures, footpath and steep land were each run over every built world first (SampleRegion, SamplePeak, the selftest terrains...). Where older worlds fail, the error holds NEW worlds and the old ones warn ('improve:'), by the same grandfathered names the design gate uses - never a new red on a world nobody is building.
