# Lessons — 3D models (Blender pipeline)

- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Boolean cuts: make the cutter overlap the part clearly (half outside the surface). A cutter that only grazes a face leaves zero-area slivers (degenerate_faces warning).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Arches and curved lids: compute the outline points with cos/sin. A fillet radius is capped at 45% of the shorter neighbouring edge, so a short wall + big radius stays boxy.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Asset(size=...) is the WHOLE silhouette including handles, guards and pommels. Set it from the real design, not just the main body, or size_vs_target fails.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Build big shapes first and run with --fast; read facts.tiers and vs_r6_character before adding any detail. Detail on wrong proportions is wasted work.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Nested mirrors (X then Z) make 3 copies; only odd-mirror copies need their normals flipped. The inside_out gate catches any part whose faces point inward.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Write a.expect(...) for every relationship the design depends on (guard touches blade, scope in front of grip). The build then measures it — far more reliable than judging a picture.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, sample_game): a.expect in_front_of/behind/above/below demand DISJOINT bounds, not just touching: a barrel seated into a receiver fails in_front_of. Use touches= for intentionally overlapping joints and save directional expects for truly separated parts (scope above body).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, sample_game): A model uploaded through Open Cloud as a Mesh and shown via SpecialMesh faced +Z (backwards) in Studio — measure barrel-vs-camera direction in a probe and fix with Tool.Grip * CFrame.Angles(0, pi, 0); first-person tools: lock the grip to the camera each frame (R6 arms do not pitch).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Game scripts CANNOT set SurfaceAppearance maps (Plugin capability) and nothing can set MeshPart.MeshId (measured). In game use Models.spawn/Models.tool: Rojo carries the maps (src/models/*.model.json), CreateMeshPartAsync makes the mesh. A SpecialMesh+TextureId gives colour only — that is why the first rifle looked flat.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Drawing a weapon from imagination gives toy proportions. Use a template with real ratios (rbx_firearm) and customise on top; put the origin at the grip so tools need no Grip offset, and a.anchor() every point the game needs (Muzzle, Sight) instead of hard-coding offsets.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Roblox's mesh import turns an FBX 180 degrees about Y and ignores the FBX axis header (verified by uploading the calibration mesh and reading vertices with CreateEditableMeshAsync). The exporters pre-turn geometry; never patch it per asset with Grip rotations.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): First-person arms: lock the hands to the gun's Grip/Support anchors every frame and put the shoulders OFF SCREEN in the bottom corners (right: bottom-right past the grip, left: almost straight below the support). Shoulders behind the camera make the arms fill the screen; a left shoulder far to the side drags the forearm across the barrel. Angle the hip pose inward (~10 deg yaw) so the gun's side shows.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Rebuild from the owner's reference image: identify the silhouette family first (long boxy railgun vs classic sniper), then the read-from-afar features (fin ridge, clamp bands, inset panels, ring light, nose slits, frame stock). rbx_firearm kind railgun + style dark encodes that look.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): The quality bar works because it is MEASURED and LOCATED: crease density, empty expanses with coordinates, detail KINDS (copies do not count), triangle budget with the biggest costs named. But it measures detail, not resemblance — proportions came from the compare image + the written critique, which is why hero/aaa builds require a fresh review every iteration.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Small coloured details must have enough TEXELS: a model's groups share one texture, so a 0.045-stud letter got ~1.5 texels of stroke and baked as a smear (letters vanished). Lights + markings go in their own group (rbx_firearm does this as 'marks'); the texel_density gate fails hero/aaa builds where a high-contrast detail gets < 5 texels.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): A cluster that only touches ITSELF still floats: the scope + rings + mounts all touched each other and passed a loose 0.05-stud contact tolerance while hovering 0.026 studs above the gun. Contact tolerance is now 0.2% of the model size, the report gives the gap, and mounts must sink INTO what they sit on. Look at build/renders/<name>_details.png every iteration.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Blender 5.x: a material's node_tree.id_data is the node tree itself ('Shader Nodetree'), not the material — look materials up by the Material object you iterate, never nt.id_data.name (that bug silently blanked chip/grime/emissive masks on every bake).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): High-to-low (floaters): never bake AO selected-to-active when the high contains a copy of the low — Cycles evaluates it under the floater and bakes black wedges. Bake AO on the low with floaters as occluders and use a coverage mask (kit_floater attribute) for the footprints. Debug any bake with KIT_BAKE_DEBUG=1 (raw passes + measured quality views).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Never make two parts' faces coplanar (e.g. an end cap centred on a cylinder's end face): the AO bake treats each as occluding the other and bakes BLACK, which reads as a hole/open tube. Make one stand proud by a few thousandths.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Surface wear must be CURVATURE-driven, never a symmetric edge mask: wear/chips/polish only on convex exposed edges (divergence of the baked tangent normal), dirt in concave corners + occlusion, broken up by large-scale noise. A symmetric mask from the bevel normal chalk-outlines every recess and panel line (measured: concave edges lightened 0.223 vs flat 0.138).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Sizes that matter visually must follow the model's scale: a fixed 0.03-stud baked edge radius is a crisp highlight on a 5-stud rifle and a 4.9 mm blob on a 1.6-stud pistol (37% of the texture became 'edge'). Defaults are now % of the largest dimension.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Boolean UNION of closed parts: do NOT enable use_self (self-intersection mode) on the Exact solver — it tore 72 edges on a clean receiver+grip union; plain Exact union was manifold.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): A quality metric can reward an artefact: the floor's detail/empty measures counted chalky wear lines as surface detail, so the pipeline steered toward noise. When fixing a visual defect, re-check which score components moved and why.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): TRACE ONLY ORTHOGRAPHIC REFERENCES: a 3/4 photo traced 'fine' (IoU 0.95) and the model matched a WRONG outline (yaw inflates heights, the far end shrinks). Use the maker's product side shot / a manual drawing / a blueprint, and write refs/<x>.spec.json with height_ratio from published dimensions - kit trace --spec fails a perspective photo.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): kit trace --exclude must never eat the object: add --protect boxes over the object itself and --part to trace removed things (bipod, monopod) as their own outlines; the preview tints REMOVED pixels red - look at it. Lost real parts (a carry handle, a magwell lip) were modelled as holes before this.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Do not 'level' a reference blindly: the M107A1 rail measured 0.5 deg front-low - a real 27 MOA canted long-range rail, not camera tilt (the barrel was level). Check which member is straight before rotating; build canted parts in their own frame (a.frame rot) so slots and the scope follow the cant.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): rbx_trace.clip (Sutherland-Hodgman) on a concave traced outline leaves zero-width bridges and the extrusion collapses ('outlines do not overlap'); use rbx_trace.region() (raster + contour, cleaned at the photo's pixel size ~1 mm) - and clean() stairs off, or a slanted edge reads as a saw blade.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): A traced opening is traced AROUND anything solid in the photo (the trigger inside the guard): cut T.hull(hole), not the hole, or the receiver keeps a trigger-shaped lump your real trigger collides with.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Coplanar faces are the #1 boolean failure: a cutter flush with a face (rib box at the wall plane, pocket starting exactly at a face) tears or leaves fins; a fused part as wide as its partner (knuckle = leg thickness, lip resting flush) makes 3-4-face edges. Overshoot cutters, overlap fused parts; the kit now retries cut/fuse with a nudge/inflate and repairs fins + T-junction cracks.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Point-in-solid by ray parity is fooled by grazing boolean faces: one ray gave 77 false 'magazine inside barrel' hits. Vote over 5 rays and require a CLUSTER of hits (a real penetration is a line/patch, noise is scattered pairs).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Design-spec-driven modelling: write the parts list FROM THE MANUAL first (what / attaches / visible / source), then model to it. The M107 manual moved the barrel springs to the handguard (seen through the bottom vents), the mainspring+buffer to the rear, the chamber AHEAD of the magazine, and turned my 'sling ring' into the midlock pin's finger ring - none of which a photo alone tells you.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): ONE SHAPE PER SEAM: when a part is cut out of a traced region (a magazine out of a receiver, a grip out of a frame), subtract it on the SAME raster (rbx_trace.region(..., minus=[(piece, gap_mm)])) and build the piece from that exact shape. Cutting with a separately simplified outline left ~1-3 mm shelves under the grip and the magazine (owner spotted them); building the magazine from a fitted quad while the receiver used the wavy trace left a 3 mm gap and then a collision.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Traced outlines need manufactured radii: rbx_trace.region(round_mm=2) fillets the outer shape BEFORE the mating subtractions (a closing would fill a magwell's corners into the magazine), then an opening after them knocks off the pointed shards two converging cuts leave (the trigger-guard spike).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): A part built as a loft from traced heights must cover the traced top AND seat into its host: the grip's stations skipped the heights where the trace had no crossing and the grip hung 8 mm below the receiver - attach checks passed because its sides touched. kit now measures visible gaps on seats (attach_gaps).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Measure features above a slanted edge: the magazine's bottom rises 8 deg to the front; its front edge measured at a fixed 17 mm height came out 10 mm short and the cartridges poked into the receiver. Take edge positions above the highest point of the slanted side.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Markings: put text on a clear flat area of ONE part (marking_placement gate). The model engraving crossed a pin and the grip-seat/guard junction and floated over air.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Gate design lesson: a gate that never fires on the defect it was written for is worthless - prove each new gate on the broken model first (sliver, plate_support, marking_placement, attach_gaps all failed on the owner's defects before the fixes), then tune false positives with geometric meaning (parallel faces = shelf, flat back = plate, facing surfaces + no nearby contact = gap, small-on-big = seat), never with thresholds alone.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): ONE GLOBAL SCORE HIDES A WRONG REGION: the M107 silhouette was 0.935 overall while the trigger-guard area was 0.69. reference_<view>_local scores 40x2 windows along the length and fails any below 0.80 with its position - proven on the owner's circled area before the fix.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Model a part the photo shows as its own part (a trigger guard, a handle, a bracket) from its OWN traced shape: the guard was what was left over after cutting the opening (a stub and a hook). Guard = the opening grown by the bar width minus the opening, opened at the top so it stays a U.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Derive hidden-in-silhouette parts from the trace too: a traced opening goes AROUND what is solid in the photo, so hull(hole) - hole IS the photo's trigger (0.70 local -> pass).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Silhouette checks cannot see lines INSIDE the outline (a magwell plate's slanted bottom edge, a panel seam): measure those on the photo (gridded crop) and build to them; next kit step: an edge-map comparison (photo gradients vs the model's crease render, per window).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Reproducibility: Python's hash() is salted per process (Blender ignores PYTHONHASHSEED) - never seed noise with hash(name), use zlib.crc32. The M107's edge wear moved on every build, so its maps and build_id never repeated.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Blender booleans and merges are NOT reproducible run to run: identical builds emitted 26 of 95 parts in another element order, some with 1-ulp noise, and merge survivors land up to the merge tolerance apart (8 bipod vertices 0.06 mm). Never key a cache on an exact mesh hash, and do not 'canonicalize' by sorting + rebuilding the BMesh (tried: still not exact, AND it broke attachments). Compare geometry with a tolerance (KD-tree, 2x the merge tolerance) and reuse the cached mesh itself.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Bake cost is per PASS, not per pixel: a 1-sample emission pass costs 0.3-1 s whatever it carries (M107: 109 passes = 63 s). Pack one-number channels three to a bake (R, G, B) and split - byte-identical, 63 -> 26 s. And 1-sample emission is faster on the CPU than the GPU (0.82 vs 0.92 s at 2048).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Never compare all pairs in Python: the mechanism gate's cluster count compared 45,503 points pairwise (267 s for one fixture); a spatial hash (cells = the radius, look in the 27 around) gives the exact same count in 6 s.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): AO bakes see every part (contact shadows), so ANY geometry change makes every group's maps stale; a material-only change does not. Key bake caches as: whole-model geometry + per-group materials/settings. And fingerprint what ships (mesh arrays + map bytes), never FBX bytes - an FBX embeds its creation time, so every unchanged rebuild expired the judge's verdict.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): INTERNAL LINES: one edge detector (TEED, kit vision) on the reference photo AND on a shaded render of the model, compared in the same frame, sees what a silhouette cannot. A fixture with a perfect 1.0 silhouette IoU had 1% of the photo's inside lines; on the M107 it found the faint magazine ribs, the wrong A2 grip and a rail-base seam 5 mm off (59% of the photo's lines). Read build/renders/<asset>_lines_<view>.png every build.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Trace a part from ITS OWN outline with one click: kit trace --part-point grip=x,y (SAM 2.1) hugged the M107's A2 grip exactly (score 0.94) — the grip that was wrong twice when built from boxes and hulls. Use --cutout (BiRefNet) for photos on a real background.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Vetting downloaded models: vendor small model code (TEED 150 lines, BiRefNet 2100) instead of trust_remote_code; safetensors weights (or torch.load weights_only=True); pin commit + sha256 and re-hash after download; run with a socket guard (any network attempt raises). 6 local lines replaced three pip packages (timm/kornia/einops) — check how much of a dependency the code really uses.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): When a model's first test looks bad, suspect the test: BiRefNet scored IoU 0.29 against a 'non-white' mask of a transparent PNG (its hidden pixels are black, so everything was 'object'); against the real alpha it was 0.968. SAM's full-image box picks the BACKGROUND (score 0.99): prompt parts with points or tight boxes.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Studio renders: Cycles' OpenImageDenoise on the GPU (scene.cycles.denoising_use_gpu) gives the same picture (within 1/255, like a CPU rerun) 5x faster: 4.8 -> 1.0 s per presentation shot. Freestyle line art is the remaining cost (CPU, ~1.5-5.6 s per view, set by geometry, not resolution).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Never min-max normalise a detector's output per image before a fixed threshold: TEED reads ANY flat surface as ~0.44 and real lines as 0.94-0.99, but stretched per image a flat grey slab became 97-100% 'line' (a perfect box-like model failed). Threshold the raw output at a fixed level (0.6), give the detector white paper around the object (pad), and erode masks with the outside counted as background. Prove every check BOTH ways: a positive fixture that must pass clean, not only a negative one.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): A SCORE IS NOT THE JUDGE - LOOK AT THE HERO RENDER EVERY ITERATION. Agents told to lift the starter crate, chest and blaster past the prop bar carved grooves into every plank, slatted the chest lid with see-through gaps and bricked the crate's faces: scores went 33-47 -> 55-58 and the owner graded the results 1/100. The clean versions scored LOWER than the noise. Never add detail the real object would not have to move a number; never accept a score (yours or an agent's) without opening the sheet.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): WOODEN props: material(pattern="wood") + every board its own part. The grain follows each part's own long axis (a 45-degree brace grains along its length), each board gets its own tone, the seams come from small gaps over a dark core. The crate (4 boards a face, nails, stencils, a bolted handle) and the chest (slats round the arch from extruded outlines, bands, hinges, lock) read as real at ~54-56. The 'empty' measure still calls every honest plank face featureless (it cannot see grain) - do NOT answer its 'put grooves there' advice on planks.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): A handle floats when its END POINTS sit off the face, not because they 'only touch': P.handle sinks each end `radius` into whatever face the end points lie on, and the floating check counts any overlap as attached. The old crate's ends were 0.12 studs out (a 0.05 air gap after the inset). Put a_end/b_end exactly ON a real face - a board or a mount plate (mounts also read as how a real handle is fixed). P.handle(segments=6) is round enough for a thin handle (triangles grow with segments: about 3/4 of 8). Text is never made inside a.mirror() - a mirrored word reads backwards: one a.text per face, each with its own facing.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Edge wear used to turn EVERY material's edges toward bare grey steel (metallic, smoother): wood boards and plastics got chalky grey outlines. Fixed in rbx.py: rubbed edges go toward steel only as far as the surface is metallic; wood/plastic/rubber edges just lighten a little in their own hue.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): The quality score was TEXTURE-BLIND on cached rebuilds: maps restored from the bake cache's .blend come back named '<x>_color.001' and the exact '_color' match missed them, so grain, wear and paint all measured as flat grey. Match baked channels by regex and report maps_seen; a score that cannot see its maps must say so.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Grade BLIND and measure the score against the owner: 21 shuffled, authorship-hidden grades showed the old score agreed with the owner at rank 0.32 (same-object pairs 55%, pass/fail 67%). Weights refitted to them (kit grades --write: ridge toward the hand weights, every grade predicted WITHOUT itself) reach rank 0.49, pairs 70%, pass/fail 71% (re-measured under measure 6) - better than the old score on all three; against the hand weights (0.31 / 75% / 57%) better on rank and pass/fail but one same-object pair worse, and all inside the noise of 21 grades (Spearman SE ~0.22) and some measures were shaped on these same grades: only FRESH grades prove a judge. The owner's grades pull weight onto texture, surface story and triangle budget, and concavity ('silhouette') to the floor. All three score-chased agent builds now fail (two PASSED the old score); the owner's two favourite 'flat' builds fail too (the judge sees flat colour, the owner liked them) - an open disagreement to settle with more grades.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Reference-free SigLIP judging does NOT track the owner: zero-shot quality prompts correlate ~0, look-alike kNN 0.25 at best, and adding either to the fitted score made it WORSE leave-one-out. SigLIP's strength is similarity to a REFERENCE (photo, style target) - use it there, not as a free-floating taste meter.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Presentation colour must be measured, not eyeballed: AgX at 0 EV rendered 18% grey at 0.59 and halved warm-wood saturation (the 'pale pink chest', gold near-black); Khronos PBR Neutral at -0.8 EV + a reflection sky renders a chart of known albedos within dE 6 (selftest: studio is honest / lying studio caught). Every flat or measuring render must reset exposure after a studio render.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): Plausibility the geometry CAN judge vs only NAME: wood carved instead of built is charged on a mostly-wooden model (4+ groove/recess-shaped cuts, or 8+ cuts of any shape; forty grooves cost ~60%; a few joinery cuts are free); see-through slits cost only on a mostly-wooden model (the owner's 80-graded railgun shows MORE slit than the agent chest - rails are design); cuts that split a part are only named (the M107's scope rings are split on purpose).
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): The owner's recurring note is FUNCTION: a lock that could not work, a scope mount that could not hold, a blaster with no practical layout, a rifle with no insides. Model how the real thing works (mechanism, attachment, access, assembly), not only how it looks.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): The quality score's 'texture' was the 1-px Laplacian of the baked maps: it counted bake noise and never saw wood grain (a grained and a flat-painted twin scored the SAME, raw 42.9 — the old selftest only passed because the flat twin also lacked wear/grime). Texture is now the albedo a player sees: band-pass contrast at 2-18 px on crease-free surface (rbx_image.visible_texture). Lesson: a fixture pair must differ in ONE thing, or it tests something else.
- 2026-10-05 (mayusi, Parallax): A gate that decides which scorer the kit uses must be stated before the numbers and give every metric a test sized to it: three reviews rejected one-sided gates and a bootstrap that could not veto on same-object pairs (a pair enters a resample only when both of its builds are drawn). kit grades now runs a sign test on the pairs the two weightings order differently, McNemar on pass/fail and a bootstrap on rank: a refit is refused when any test says it is worse (p < .05) and kept only when one says it is better (p < .20) - measured on these grades, that keeps a refit that is NO better about 1 time in 4, and the sign test vetoes a pairs loss only from 5 discordant pairs. With ~20 grades nearly everything is noise: the weights record the grades they were fitted on, and kit grades reports them on FRESH grades (given later) - ask the owner for fresh blind rounds to prove a judge.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): A plausibility gate that traces its own evidence must trace ALL of it, and its fixtures must assert the MECHANISM: the wood-carving gate tagged only a cutter's first material (a comb fused of a spine and steel teeth: 28 grooves counted as 4), judged a part by its first material (a block fused into a steel nub carved freely), never saw seams baked on as floaters, divided by hidden internal wood, and - measured per face - counted the groove walls out of the wood, so 289 dark grooves carved the score-chased crate down to 24% wood, under the 25% line, and switched the charge off. Each now has a fixture the old code passes (plank_comb_fused counts 28, fused_nub, floater_seams, hidden_wood, metal_carved). Re-measuring the owner's graded builds after a measuring change is what caught the last one: diff every component that moved.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): Owner-only acts are confirmed at a REAL terminal: Lune's stdio.prompt fails with 'not a terminal' in an agent's shell even with piped stdin, so kit grade and kit accept --lower can be started by a model (in the owner's terminal) but finished only by the owner's keystrokes. Once a floor is COMMITTED, the committed one holds — found case-insensitively and by name anywhere, through git run as the repository says (GIT_* dropped, no replace objects), failing closed when git cannot answer (no edit, deletion or breakage of the file moves it; a change of floor takes effect once committed; a floor never committed is only its file), and the one re-base without the owner is a build MADE by the floor's own script (the file Blender ran, sha recorded at build time) that is the floor's model (fingerprint: triangles, size). Trusting the working file in any form lost to an edit: removing components, restamping the measure, a NaN.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): Whole-model ray parity is fooled by parts that SHARE a face exactly (the kit's own region(minus=) seams, a plate seated on its host): the mechanism gate said a drum turning in a clear window was buried (136 points) and the plate gate said seated plates rested on nothing. Inside/outside is now tested per part, on that part's own faces.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): A one-click SAM part (--part-point) on a big structure returns the WHOLE object: the Gjallarhorn's stock and grip came back as the whole launcher, small parts (crest, drum) as slivers. Box prompts (--part-box) gave the right parts at scores 0.83-0.95; the trace now fails a part covering >= 75% of the object.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): Settle which end is the FRONT from the maker before tracing: the Gjallarhorn render read front-right (a long thin 'barrel', a claw 'muzzle') but its designer puts the largest wolf on the FRONT, so the thin tube was the rear exhaust and the frame a thumbhole stock. One search flipped the whole layout and made the grip, trigger and stock make sense.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): Someone else's design (a game's weapon, a film prop) is rbx.Asset(private=True): never compiled into src/models, never uploaded. Under a plain name the next kit upload would have sent it to Roblox (the dry run listed every one of its maps). Keep its picture in refs/private/ and its files out of git.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): The mechanism TRAVEL check never worked: BVHTree.FromObject builds in the object's OWN space, so a group posed through its travel (matrix_world) was tested where it rests, and the >8-colliding-pairs rule could not fire on low-poly parts anyway. A lid swinging through a wall read 'clear'. Trees are now world-space, the depth test runs at five stops along the travel, and after= poses the joints a sequence needs first.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): Check the pixel before blaming the model: SAM 'failed' on the Gjallarhorn's high-res profile for six probes because the wolf click had been read off a resized grid without scaling up: the pixel was pure white backdrop and SAM correctly returned the backdrop. The trace now fails a click that lands on the backdrop.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): A reload slide must be derived from the geometry, not from a description: the Nerf replica says 'the entire top housing slides forward', but a whole top sliding far enough for its rear overhang to clear the bay (300 mm) runs into the muzzle's claws, and one sliding 175 mm leaves its overhang over the bay (the shell cannot lift out). The working design: a fixed rear cover, the housing from the seam forward sliding 145 mm (its trailing edge past the bay), riding on rails as long as its travel, every end 1 mm clear. The kit's travel sweep and spec attaches found all three faults; the Gjallarhorn, 2026-10-06.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): A blanket bmesh dissolve_degenerate over every edge is not a cleanup: on a carved spine it tore 3 edges open and, run after a targeted pass, made a new zero-area face. Clamped bevels where several sharp edges meet (a pointed bar's tips on a curved band) leave faces 0.003-0.009 mm across, just above its 1e-5 tolerance: collapse ONLY the edges of faces under the validator's zero-area line (rbx._drop_micro_faces). Fixture: kit/selftest/blender/build_bevel_tip.py.
- 2026-10-06 (mayusi, Parallax): On a MASTER, floaters are the high-quality detail, not a bake: they stay real geometry in the group they decorate (checked like every part, so seat them) and are tagged kit_floater_onto for the game version. Conversely a GAME build's baked floaters must still count as the spec's parts (inspect never sees floaters: a rivet the spec names read 'missing'). - 2026-11-12 (mayusi, Parallax): A half-empty texture map is the packer's PLACING, not its pads or its settings: before tuning anything, measure the pad-only bound (every island in its own outline plus half a pad) and run every packer setting once. - 2026-11-12 (mayusi, Parallax): A constant baked per material is NOT an id map: Cycles blends an island's edge texels, so what a one-number channel holds is the materials' numbers weighted by how much of each texel each material COVERS. To take numbers out of a bake (so they can change without baking), bake each material's coverage (1 where it shows) and compute number x coverage, summed - an id map looked up in a table moved 1% of the final texels (hard edges where the bake had soft ones); the coverage sum is within one level of 255 of the old bake on 0.1% of texels. And a Cycles normal pass is not bit-stable between two fresh bakes (a few texels of a million, one level): hold 'the same maps' to that room, never to the byte across two renders.
- 2026-11-12 (mayusi, Parallax): Moving an effect from shader nodes onto baked passes is only the same arithmetic where nothing CLIPS in between: a byte pass stops at 1 (a bright colour under a scan runs to 2.4 x its mean - the nodes darkened it before the clip, the pass clipped it first: 57 levels too dark) and at 0. - 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): A bake gives a texel to the face that covers its CENTRE. - 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): A material's own layers (a.material(layers=[rbx.layer('rust', where='chipped', amount=0.8)])): direct a surface by the report, not by eye alone - the summary says per layer the share of its PLACE that was asked and the share of the MATERIAL that makes ('rust where chipped 80% -> 5%'). - 2026-11-13 (mayusi, Parallax): Two things subdivision does that a build script cannot see.
