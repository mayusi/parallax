- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): The wind-up must read at the impact's scale: a 2-stud glow under a 50-stud blast is invisible in the strip. Scale the telegraph (bigger glow, ring, light) with the impact.
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): duration must cover delay + the longest particle lifetime, or the holder is destroyed mid-fade and the effect pops (cut_off gate).

Stage long effects in phases (uplink, lock, inhale, impact, aftermath) with delays, match the server's impact delay to the preset's impact beat, and keep the dust wall smaller than the play space (a camera inside it washes everything out).
- 2026-10-04 (mayusi, Parallax): Matching a reference strike: the drama is the CLOUD COLUMN and a THIN beam, not a fat one.
