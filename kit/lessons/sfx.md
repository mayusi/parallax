# Lessons — sounds

- 2026-10-13 (mayusi, Parallax): Short noise bursts (a 90 ms brown thud) roll new dice every take: the same recipe swung 8 % -> 38 % sub between takes. Give noise layers a fixed "seed" and let takes vary by pitch_spread / modal strike / round-robin samples; the set gate (takes differ but stay in the family) catches both failures.
- 2026-10-13 (mayusi, Parallax): BS.1770 loudness on SFX: a 0.5 s sound sits right at the 400 ms block edge — decoded from OGG (128 samples shorter) it got 1 block instead of 2 and read 2.5 LU louder. The last block must end at the sound's end. And use the standard's 48 kHz K-weighting coefficients (resample first): a shelf rebuilt from its parameters gave +0.43 dB at 1 kHz instead of +0.69.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): A mix limited to -1 dBFS decodes ABOVE full scale after Vorbis (+1.1 dBFS measured): leave ~3.5 dB for the encoder, and converge loudness in rounds (gain, limit, re-measure) - one gain step left a sparse scene 7 LU short.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): ffmpeg -ac 1 downmixes with its own pan law, not the channel mean: measure a stereo file's loudness by decoding 2 channels and averaging them yourself, or the number disagrees with the tool that wrote it by ~2.5 LU.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Never push a SPARSE scene to a loudness target through a limiter: every event clamps to the ceiling and the dynamics die (five walkers leaving measured flat). Cap gain reduction (~4 dB) and let it land under the target, saying so; measure a moving source's step levels to prove it moves.
- 2026-10-24 (mayusi, Parallax): Distance is ~ -6 dB per doubling (far = -24 dB) plus air (a lowpass) plus more of the room; a linear 0.7 cut with a rising reverb send cancels itself out (a walker 'leaving' stayed at one level).
- 2026-10-25 (mayusi, Parallax): A tonal recording can join a tuned synth chord: measure its spectrum peaks first (ffmpeg -> rfft, 5 Hz smoothing), layer only a recording whose strongest peak IS a chord tone, band-limit it round that tone (q ~10) and re-measure the render - the chord tones within ~2 dB of each other and the recording's off-chord peaks >= 15 dB down (Stormhold voice: Sound of Essen 293 Hz = D4; q 6 left a 340 Hz band at -10.8 dB, q 10 -> -16.4)
- 2026-10-25 (mayusi, Parallax): Choose round-robin takes from a long recording on PEAK-ALIGNED windows (what align:peak actually plays): picking splashes by onset windows gave a worst pair of 11 and the family gate failed three times; peak-aligned the best four scored 6.45 and passed first time (a puddle splash can peak 0.2 s after its onset). Envelopes / high-passes on the body to force a family only made it worse
