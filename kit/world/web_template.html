      Controls

      Orbit — drag to turn, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom.

      Fly — drag to look, WASD to move, Q/E down / up, Shift to hurry.

      Walk — an R6 at eye height (4.5 studs, 16 studs/s, jump 7.2): drag to look, WASD, Space to jump, Shift to run. Walls block you; ledges under 2.4 studs step up.

      Map — click to travel there. 1 2 3 switch cameras, H hides the interface.

      Everything here is drawn from the same blueprint the kit compiles into Roblox: the heightmap, every part, the lights and particle emitters, the terrain colours and the lighting preset. The Roblox build looks like this only as far as Roblox's own renderer allows — this page is the art direction, not a promise of Studio's pixels.
