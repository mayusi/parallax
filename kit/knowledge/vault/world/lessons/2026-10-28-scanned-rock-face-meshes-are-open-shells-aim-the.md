Scanned rock-face meshes are open shells: aim the scan's surface (its mean normal, +Z for Poly Haven rock_face_01 after kit import) DOWNHILL; aimed into the hill you see the open back as pale slivers. Pale granite scans under moonlight read as snow: wear a dark copy of their own texture (luminance kept, hue dropped).
