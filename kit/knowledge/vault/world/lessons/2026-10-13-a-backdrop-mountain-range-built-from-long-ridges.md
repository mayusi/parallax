A backdrop mountain range built from long ridges reads as a WALL from the map (a 2 km dead-straight crest under haze looks like a flat-topped block). Peaks = two SHORT ridges crossed at right angles, the crossing one lower and off-centre, plus far land under them (horizon floor) - and keep them lower than the hero landmark (Erdtree) so they frame instead of compete.
