Runnable walls must be FLUSH: a 1.5 recess between piers snagged a wall-hugging R6 on the next pier edge, and any shelf (a dado top) ends a wall run as a landing. Do relief as non-colliding inlays >= 8 tall (the wall-run ray accepts them) over one collision plane.
