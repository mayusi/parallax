The building generator centres its walls on the footprint's edge. Two buildings whose footprints touch are ONE party wall built twice (faces in one plane inside both houses), and a street between them is a wall narrower than typed. A neighbour's footprint is its lot less half a wall all round: wall faces then stand on the lot's lines, party walls back to back, and kit world check measures the street at exactly its planned width. With trim keep a wall and a stud between footprints on EVERY side: the ridge beam reaches a wall and a half past the footprint, posts and bands less. A house floor a hair (0.1) over the pavement it stands on, never level with it (card my7f's flicker).
