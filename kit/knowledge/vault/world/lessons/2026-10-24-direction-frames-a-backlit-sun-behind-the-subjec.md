Direction frames: a backlit (sun behind the subject) shot leaves the face black at mean ~0.1; add a camera-side bounce light (fill, 2-5x the key on a dusk sky) and measure the face's mean luminance (aim 0.2-0.4) before looking - it saves image budget.
