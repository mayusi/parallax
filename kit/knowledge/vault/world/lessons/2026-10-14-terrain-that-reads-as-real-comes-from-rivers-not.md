Terrain that reads as real comes from RIVERS, not noise: thermal erosion alone makes talus-angle cones; stream-power incision (two scales) gives branching valleys + sharp ridges; the painter colours from flow/deposit/curvature. Erosion moves the min height -> pin a design anchor (the lake bed) and keep basin/plateau(+edge)/ramp(+shoulders), or lakes go dry and castles become traps (kit world check catches the trap).
