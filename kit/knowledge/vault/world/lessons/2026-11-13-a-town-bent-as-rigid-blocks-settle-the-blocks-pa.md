A town bent as rigid blocks: SETTLE the blocks pair by pair (each two across a street moved until their nearest points are the planned width apart), never spread them all from the centre by one factor. The spread kept every street at least as wide as planned and made a 12-stud lane 20.8 studs at its narrowest (bend 8, 24 towns); settled it is 12.5. Measure the widths a generator really makes before writing 'no street is narrower' as its only promise
