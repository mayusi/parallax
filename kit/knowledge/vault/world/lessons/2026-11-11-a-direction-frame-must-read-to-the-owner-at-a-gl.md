A direction frame must read to the owner at a glance. The GothicCourtyard test frame (2026-11-11) - a wide-angle block-out looking UP at a lit flank, unlabelled - got REDO: 'I don't know what I'm exactly looking at ... I'm just gonna give it, a bad'. Show a direction the way he plays: from where a player stands (Roblox's default third-person camera behind his R6 rig, about 12 studs back and 7.5 up, vertical FOV 70), with the rig in the shot for scale, plain-word labels on what he is looking at (the cathedral ahead, the houses you can walk into, the stair, each lamp), and the source's real frames beside ours, each pair labelled 'the source: ...' / 'ours: ...'. Then ask ONE plain question: is this the look you want (yes, or what to change)?
