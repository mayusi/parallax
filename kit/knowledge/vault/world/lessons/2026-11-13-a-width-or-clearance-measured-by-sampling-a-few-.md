A width or clearance measured by SAMPLING a few heights misses whatever lies between them: my slanted-lane measure read three heights of the body band and walked through a 1-stud bench at y 1.6-2.6, so a lane failed on an axis and passed at 45 degrees. Meet each thing by its own shape (an upright box: where the line meets its footprint, if its height overlaps the band at all), and test one obstacle at several angles for ONE verdict
