A world's R6 fit cannot be checked from hand-written claims alone: the kit's own generators built below the owner's rig and nothing saw it - building floors of 12 (11 clear: 'a room a head too low', his District 6/10), bridge rails of 2.2 (the knee), lamps every 24 studs. Each generator now CLAIMS what it builds (every floor's clear ceiling, the door as an opening, the stair step, the bridge rail, a roofed room's ceiling and doorways) and a new world names its rig sheet ("scale": "r6", the world lock asks), so kit world check holds generated geometry to the sheet. Give generator claims MIN-only kinds (ceiling h >= 12, opening 4.5 x 7.5): a great hall or a castle gate is legitimate and a max flags every one. Do not claim window sills (a high sill is a choice) or a roofless yard's ceiling, and claim the top floor's whole height under a flat roof (none under a gable). Bad example: kit/selftest/world/_selftest_vaelmouth.json (5 errors).
