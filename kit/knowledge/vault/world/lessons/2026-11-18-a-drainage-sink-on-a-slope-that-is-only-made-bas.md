A drainage sink on a slope that is only made base level (its cells pass no water on) CUTS LESS, not more: it stops the water already flowing through it and holds the land above at its height. An interior sink must also be LOW (laid at the land's base level) before the rivers run, and on the two-scale solver a sink smaller than a coarse cell must stay off the coarse pass (laid as a whole coarse cell it cut the slope above it less than no sink at all).
