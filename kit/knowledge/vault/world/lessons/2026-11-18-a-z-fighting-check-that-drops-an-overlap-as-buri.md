A z-fighting check that drops an overlap as 'buried' by probing a few points (the middle, near the corners) hides seen faces: a part over most of it leaves a visible rim, two parts with a strip between them cover every probe, and glass buries nothing. Probe a grid over the WHOLE overlap with its edges, count only opaque parts, and search neighbouring normal buckets (two faces either side of a rounding edge are one plane). Run it over every selftest fixture before wiring it as an error: the sloped town had 220 real pairs nobody had seen.
