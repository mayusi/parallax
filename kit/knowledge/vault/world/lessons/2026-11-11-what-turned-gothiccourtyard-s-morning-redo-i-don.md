What turned GothicCourtyard's morning REDO ('I don't know what I'm exactly looking at', an unlabelled block-out looking up a wall) into a KEEP the same day ('Keep it'): (1) the look source's REAL frames beside ours, each pair labelled in plain words ('the source: ...' / 'ours: ...') - he said yes to the look from that sheet, then 'Yes, build it'; (2) every view from where a player stands - Roblox's default camera behind his R6 rig, level, plain-word labels on what he sees (the cathedral ahead, the stair, a house you can walk into, each lamp, 'you'), plus a view inside a house; (3) real meshes over the blockout - gothic house skins with walk-in rooms, traceried bays, lampposts - made in the kit (kit world meshes) BEFORE the piece, so the piece itself took about 10 minutes of its hero hour. He kept it although told it read dark and plain beside Bloodborne: what he could read as his kind of place came before polish.
