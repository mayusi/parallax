Blender's light is not Roblox's, and the look's Blender pane hid it: GothicCourtyard (night preset, exposure 0.5) read navy with every wall visible in kit world look, and in Roblox Studio (kit world studio, 2026-11-12) the same stations are near black outside three lamp pools and the brazier, while the candle-lit room is cold and dim in Blender and hot orange in Studio. Judge a world's LIGHT only in the Studio pane. And with nothing uploaded Studio has no mesh: kit world studio shows the blockout hidden under each mesh skin so the place has walls, but anything set INSIDE that blockout (the cathedral's lit lancets sit in its wall) cannot be seen until the meshes are uploaded - that pane judges layout and light, never carving or windows.
