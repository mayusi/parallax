'A lamp everywhere' (the owner on the District, 2026-10-28) is crowding, not a count or a spacing: measure the median lamp's neighbours within 80 studs, with lamp/lantern parts and meshes merged within 4 studs so a post, its head and its light count once; 3 or more is the fault (kit world check design:). A street lit on both sides at 90 studs has 1 (its opposite) and passes; the row generator's old 24-stud default had 11 and SampleCity has 10. A plain count fails big lit cities and a spacing rule fails two-sided streets. The row generator's lamp default is now 90.
