GUN TRICKS (owner 2026-10-26, repeated): the HAND drives the gun - every gun motion (a twirl, a toss, a swap to the other hand, a flip) is caused by a visible hand/arm movement that would physically produce it (a wrist flick before a twirl, a throwing arc before a toss, a reaching catch), never a gun turning or flying while the arm sits still. Key the hand's motion FIRST, then the gun follows it.
