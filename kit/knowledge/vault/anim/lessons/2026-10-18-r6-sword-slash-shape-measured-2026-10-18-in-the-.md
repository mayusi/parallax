R6 sword slash SHAPE (measured 2026-10-18 in the TORSO frame over the owner's loved Sword Fox 1-4 + BEST R6 1-3): the hands sweep 4.5-5.5 studs across the body, from high and wide on one side (y 1.7-2.9, x ~3) to LOW on the other (y -0.5..-1), arms 0-170 deg; the hold is low and relaxed, the torso ~10 deg forward; the legs never move against the torso. Our rejected greatswords kept the hands in a 2-stud box at the shoulders (arms ~110 deg throughout) - every timing number matched, the SHAPE did not. Sword Fox is a COMBO: each cut ENDS in its follow-through and the next starts there (no return to guard). strike.py: a blade turning ~180 deg between two keys slerps the short way through the floor - add a key between; a hand passing near its shoulder pivot flips the one-piece arm (a one-frame pop) - route the grip round the outside
