A PHYSICAL TOSS that reads (style v4, 2026-10-27, measured on the bake): (1) an R6 fist can never be nearer the shoulder than the arm's reach (1.58): a run-up path inside that sphere is pushed outward and the fist's speed no longer matches the gun's - run the throw on a line >= reach ahead (z 1.62: the arm slides there, fist = target); (2) the hand's run-up = 4 frames quad_in then 2 frames at the launch speed (quad_in ends at twice its mean speed); (3) the spin must run the SAME way as the move after the catch: a backflip caught and snapped down onto the target reversed its spin (a jolt) - a front flip (turns -1) flows into the snap; (4) keep the gun along the swinging arm in the wind-up (cocked 75 deg in the fist it turned 20 deg/frame against an arm at 2-7: a ghost).
