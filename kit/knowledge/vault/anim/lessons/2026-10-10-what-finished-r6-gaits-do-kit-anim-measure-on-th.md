What finished R6 gaits DO (kit anim measure on the DevForum '[R6] Run + Walk' pack and azazinchic's run): legs +-45..+-100 degrees AND translated (swing leg 0.5-0.7 up, 0.8-1.3 forward; landing leg reaching down 0.2-0.5; stance leg dragging back 0.25), arms +-20 with 0.3 translation (walk) to +155 with the hand 0.85 down and 0.7 forward (sprint), a constant lean 6-17, torso 0.1-0.2 low, twist +-6 to +-18 against the leading leg, the head countering the twist with correlation -1.00 and the lean fully, 60 fps linear keys. A generator without translation offsets cannot get within 25 of them; with them, fitted, 14 (the arm roll curves are the residual).
