A generator carries ONE key's change into its neighbours' poses (strike.py overlap and drag: turning the loved kesa's pose 3 by 15 degrees moves pose 4 by 4.2 and pose 5 by 8.7), and adding a key renumbers every pose after it. So anything said about ONE pose (the owner's note, 'was it answered?') must follow the pose by its AUTHORED key, never by its number or by 'the pose changed'. The first build of the blocking stage did both wrong and a fresh review caught it: a key inserted before a noted pose reported the note answered with a change nobody made.
