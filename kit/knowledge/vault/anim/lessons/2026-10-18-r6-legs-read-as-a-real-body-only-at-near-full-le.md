R6 LEGS read as a real body only at (near) FULL LENGTH (owner 2026-10-18: a crouch that slid the legs 1.2 studs into the torso = 'like his a baby man'): never push a leg in more than ~0.3-0.45; the crouch must come from the stance, and the body RISES over a passing leg (a real walk vaults). Keep the legs under the torso (a cone ~33 deg from the rest line, no slide OUT, the leg box turning ~70 % with the hips). Real footwork (measured on swordsmen): small quick steps, the step landing just BEFORE the cut, the body travelling with it; a step is ONE smooth segment with an arc (a mid-air key stalls the foot). The owner's pick so far: ayumi-ashi (passing steps). strike.py: feet track + leg_cone / leg_slide_out / leg_twist_follow / leg_in_max
