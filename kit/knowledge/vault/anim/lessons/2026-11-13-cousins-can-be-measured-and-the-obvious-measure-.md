Cousins can be measured, and the obvious measure is wrong: inside the two sets the owner loved the closest two attacks run their blade tips 6.1 studs apart on average (the path sampled start-to-Hit and Hit-to-end, so a slower copy is the same path), while one cut made again - every key turned up to 25 degrees, the grip moved half a stud, or re-cut for the other weapon - stays within 1.3; the ANGLE between two cuts does not separate them (two loved attacks of one set cut within 14 degrees of each other). And a cut's direction for a design is read from how the BLADE's direction changes, seen from behind: the tip's travel alone calls an overhead cut a thrust.
