Two checks that sound right were MEASURED on the kit's fixtures (7 loved cuts, 3 rejected) and do not separate them: (1) the order the body fires in - loved cuts peak torso, arm and blade within one frame of the Hit, and so do two of the three rejected ones; (2) the smoothness of the blade tip's path - a loved underhip cut turns its path 153 degrees in one frame, a rejected side cut is flat to 0.05 studs. Neither is built as a gate (board/plans/archive/2026-11-13-anim-methods-audit.md). What the fixtures do show is the body's travel (loved under 0.11 studs/s, rejected 2.3-4.8): the 'dip, not a stance' already checked as stilts.
