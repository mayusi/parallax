A straight R6 leg cannot buy an R15 foot clearance by tilting: when the R15 ankle passes under the hip low (a late-swing knee fold) the leg snaps back-to-front 55 deg in one frame. Fold the leg along the R15 hip->ankle line and slide it UP into the torso where the floor is in the way (its top hides inside the torso); hold the sole 0.08 over the floor, above the gates 0.04 contact band, or the metric counts the swing as planted and reports skate.
