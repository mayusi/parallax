What a finished anime-style R6 fighting set DOES (d5v's A1-A4, measured by the kit): 0.5-0.67 s per hit; anticipation 0.07-0.17 s, the strike ACTION 0.07-0.27 s, recovery 0.27-0.4 s (more than half the clip is follow-through); the striking arm peaks 59-115 studs/s; the torso turns at 1100-3300 deg/s at the hit (a whole-body whip, not an arm swing); a block-break is 0 anticipation, 1 frame of action, 0.5 s stagger; a hit recoil moves the HEAD fastest. The Free Friday finisher by contrast: 0.2 s anticipation, 0.07 s action, 0.9 s recovery. These numbers are the bar for any attack the kit generates.
