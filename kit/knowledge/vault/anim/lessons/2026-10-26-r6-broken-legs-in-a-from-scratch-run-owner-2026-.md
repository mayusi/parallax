R6 'broken legs' in a from-scratch run (owner 2026-10-26) = no fake KNEE: a straight leg hinged at the hip corner pokes its top out behind when it swings back. Loved runs (HqRun, RefSprintRig) swing back FURTHER (-90..-98 deg) but slide the leg block down ~0.65-0.9 and back ~0.5 as it goes (_scratch/gt/legs2.py measures slide by swing angle). Drive leg slides by ANGLE, not by phase; check the planted-foot sweep (travel_speed ~ 25-29 for a run) - tilting both legs back (leg_lean) dropped it to 5.5 (skating). Also: a rigid torso (21-66 deg/s vs loved 135-180) reads stiff/robotic.
