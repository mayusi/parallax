A multi-strike import gets one Hit per strike (the frame after each local peak of the faster hand's speed reaching half the clip's top, peaks 4+ frames apart); the metric judges each Hit against the NEAREST such peak of the fastest effector, else a combo's second and third Hits read 24-41 frames off the clip's single top. A roll or a knockdown: "hit": null in the import spec.
