Before a recipe op rewrites a clip, check three things on the built result, not on the op's own measure: does the clip already pass the gate (then leave it), does the build hand the change to the game at all (a root track under 0.05 studs is dropped), and does the change switch on a runtime behaviour the clip did not have (a new root track makes the game move the character).
