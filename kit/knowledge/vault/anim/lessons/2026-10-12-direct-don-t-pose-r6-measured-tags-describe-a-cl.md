DIRECT, DON'T POSE (R6): measured tags describe a clip well enough to search it in words (705 clips, kit anim find), but two things cannot be measured from the R6 body: KICKS (on 15 clips named kick the lifted foot's speed did not separate them from dash tucks - the tag says 'legup', kicks come from names) and DASH / JUMP travel (R6 clips rarely move the torso; the game's physics does). Aim cameras at the measured TORSO, never the root: a knockdown clip puts the torso 2.5 studs under its root and a fighter that vanished collapses a two-shot onto the victim's head.
