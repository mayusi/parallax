R6 arm ROLL swings the arm block: the shoulder pivot sits half a stud off the arm's own axis, so rolling an arm ~180 deg (e.g. to follow a gun pointing behind the back) sweeps the whole block through the torso (draw/holster: 49% sink). Let the arm keep its natural roll (pose key right_arm.arm_edge = null) and turn the weapon in the fist instead.
