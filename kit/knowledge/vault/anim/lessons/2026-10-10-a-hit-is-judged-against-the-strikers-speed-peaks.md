A Hit is judged against the STRIKERS' speed peaks (the blade when the clip carries one, else the hands), not only the fastest part: on a dash the head and the root travel are the fastest things on screen while the hit is where the steel peaks (UAL2 Sword_Dash_RM re-armed failed by 4 frames until the rule knew that). A one-shot with a root track must carry its blade with the root in the metric AND the look, or the blade floats away from the body.
