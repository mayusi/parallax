A repair tool must be judged by the gates NEXT to the one it serves: the pass-through repair cleared its own check on 24 built clips and took 8 of them off the hand (the in-hand gate), because it reused the check's own blind spot (a hand far along the blade read as nobody holding). Before reporting how many clips a repair clears, run every other gate's reader before and after on each of them.
