Measure foot CONTACT and PENETRATION at the sole CENTRE, not the part lowest corner: a tilted box corner dips 0.5*sin(tilt) (an R6 leg at 30 deg: 0.25 studs), so every R6 plant read as penetration while the sole sat on the floor. The same sole point is what the R6 fold grounds (kit/anim/clip.py bottoms, Anim.foldToR6).
