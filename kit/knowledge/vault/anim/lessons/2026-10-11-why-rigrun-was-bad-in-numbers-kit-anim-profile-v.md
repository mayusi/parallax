WHY RIGRUN WAS 'BAD', IN NUMBERS (kit anim profile --vs CommunitySprint): legs swung 51 vs 75, arms 73 vs 103 and LATE by 0.1 cycle, twist 10 vs 18, bob 2x, a perfect mirror (twinning), and floaty (snap 1.8 vs 2.8). Three tool bugs hid behind it: every key re-keyed every controller (held values froze limbs between keys), a second easing pass flattened offset keys to linear, and planting the forward-reaching contact leg inverted the bob. A pump wider than 180 between two extremes swings OVER THE HEAD without a bottom breakdown. RigRun2, rebuilt with the research workflow: 0 gaps.
