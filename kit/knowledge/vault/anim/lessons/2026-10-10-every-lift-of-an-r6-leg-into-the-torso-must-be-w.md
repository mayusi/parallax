Every lift of an R6 leg into the torso must be world-vertical, expressed through the tilted torso's frame (R^T * (0, deficit, 0)): dividing by the torso's up component and clamping it at 0.5 undershot by 0.15 studs on a 64-degree lunge lean, so an 'in-air' sole sat at 0.015 and read as a sliding plant.
