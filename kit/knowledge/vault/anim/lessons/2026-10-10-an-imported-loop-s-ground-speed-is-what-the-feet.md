An imported loop's ground speed is what the feet DO over a whole plant: each contact span's foot-centre travel over its duration, spans weighted by frames. The median of per-frame lowest-corner steps overshot (19.07 vs 13 studs/s on UAL1's Sprint): the corner flips heel to toe inside a span, and keyed library feet land slow and leave fast.
