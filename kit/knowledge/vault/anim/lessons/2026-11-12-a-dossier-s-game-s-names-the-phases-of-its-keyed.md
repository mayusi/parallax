A dossier's game_s names the phases of its KEYED build only. A procedural candidate of the same dossier is timed its own way (katana_kesa: cut 0.15-0.31 s in game_s; its calm candidate c10 hits at 0.53 s; the loved Roundhouse hits at 0.48 s against a 0.42 s contact), so by time every pose after the wind read 'zanshin' / 'follow-through' and would have been looked at beside the wrong reference frame. proc_dossier.phases_for now names a clip's phases by time only while its Hit falls in the dossier's hit phase, else in ORDER round the Hit (the pose at the Hit = the hit phase, back from it the wind then the guard, on from it the hold), and the sheet says which. The real fix is upstream: a builder should write its beats (phase spans, by the dossier's phase names) into the clip it makes.
