A one-piece R6 leg cannot roll heel to toe: a standing span holds ONE ground point in the WORLD (the span's R15 touch points fitted to the ground-speed line; a one-shot: a fixed point minus the root travel), solved in the world frame (the leg's axis from the hip pivot at that point). Per-frame contact corners flicker (an idle slid 1.47) and a span anchored at its flattest frame still skated.
