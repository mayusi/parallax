Every new animation starts with a MOVE DOSSIER (assets/animations/dossiers/<move>.json; kit/anim/proc_dossier.require refuses a thin one): sources + what was taken, phases with real/game timing, reading cues, mistakes, R6 translation, build keys, numeric checks measured on the clip. Roundhouse lesson: the Hit is CONTACT, after the foot's speed peak (impact = 41-54 % of peak speed); hips must lead the leg at toe-off or it reads as a leg lift; R6 straight-arm guards above ~105 deg cover the face.
