MediaPipe world landmarks are centred on the HIPS: 'ankle furthest from the hip centre' cannot find a kick's extension (a standing straight leg is as far) - use reach OUT over the ground (x/z). And the hip line TILTS 18-44 deg as the hips turn over, so its yaw over-reads the turn; an R6 torso is one block that SHOWS the chest - compare it with the shoulder line (real roundhouse: chest 44-84, hips 74-106 deg by extension)
