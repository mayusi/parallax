ANIME R6 LEGS (owner 2026-10-18: 'the only thing i ever have problem with is ur leg animations ... the placements ... aggresive type anime'), measured on the loved anime combat (TheNuggettier Dash combos, JJK, HQ Combat5): the body 0.7-1.4 studs LOW and leaning 30-67 deg into the strike, a 2-3 stud stance with the front leg thrown forward, the lead foot switching strike to strike, feet lifting (a chambered knee, a passing step). Ours stood in one fixed 1.65 stance. Doing it on R6: move ONE foot at a time (both at once reads as a straddle jump); a moving foot must LIFT (a flat foot sliding fails the slide gate); a passing foot goes WIDE of its hip and the body rises a little (passing under the hip in a deep crouch flips the leg 40-100 deg in a frame); never switch lead inside dense keys (a fast pendulum) - keep the lead and sink / rise instead; lean the body without tipping the blade (rotate the grip, keep the blade's world direction)
