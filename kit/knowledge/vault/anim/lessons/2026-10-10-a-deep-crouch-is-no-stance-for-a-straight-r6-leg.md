A deep crouch is no stance for a straight R6 leg: when the R15 pelvis sits more than 0.35 studs below the straight-leg height (a roll's exit), follow the source pelvis and treat both legs as in the air (hip-to-ankle line, sole a hair over the floor). Forcing the sole onto the floor there pushes the leg 1.2 studs up through a tilted torso and the pelvis jumps 1.4 studs in one frame.
