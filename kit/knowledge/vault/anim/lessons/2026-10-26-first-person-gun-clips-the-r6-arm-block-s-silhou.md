First-person gun clips: the R6 arm block's silhouette covers the space just IN FRONT of the fist from the eye, so anything turned there (a twirl) is ~70% hidden; turn it out of the fist's INNER face with the axis tilted ~30 deg toward the eye (measure with an eye-ray occlusion scan, not by guess). And scan every clip per frame for one-frame spins and hard stops (an ease that accelerates into a key) - the critic is calibrated on melee and rewarded a buggy arm spin as 'lively'.
