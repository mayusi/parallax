A wall run on R6 reads as running ON the wall only when the code rolls the body until its down axis points at the wall (75 deg; real Wall of Death runners sit ~77 off vertical) - at 30 deg the wall stays a steep slope beside the legs (owner 3/10). Author the owner's own run with every sole's centre on the wall plane in the root frame, lifted off along its normal. Two traps: the kit's foot gates judge the clip's FLOOR (a wall clip shows 0 contact frames = a hollow green: check on the wall plane yourself), and R6 hips pivot at x +-1 with the leg box 0.5 inside, so a modelled leg box drifts on a steep plane - bake, measure every corner's depth with FK, correct the targets along the normal, re-bake (3 rounds converge to the face). A hand on the wall caps the roll near 55 deg (the arm reaches 1.58 + 1.1 slide).
