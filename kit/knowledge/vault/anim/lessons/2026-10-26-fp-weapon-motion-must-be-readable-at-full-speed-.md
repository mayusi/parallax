FP weapon motion must be READABLE at full speed: a hand-to-hand throw needs ~0.4 s in the air and no fast spin (a 0.2 s flight + a backflip whipped the grip at ~28 studs/s and read as 'teleporting'); tips and tosses ~0.3 s each way. Check in the ENGINE by pausing the track and stepping TimePosition (Studio MCP playtests run ~6 fps); measure grip speed and grip-to-fist distance (owner 2026-10-26, Sample Game round 9).
