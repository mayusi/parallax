Real fight footage does NOT measure hit reactions with MediaPipe (2026-10-16, head hook): 7 clips (broadcast fights, compilations, slow-mo replays) - at broadcast distance the head is ~20 px and occluded by gloves, a back view still gets a guessed nose/ears (a 164 deg 'snap'), and the strongest 'snaps' were the PUNCHER's own head (one was a lone shadowboxer). Kicks worked (a whole limb crossing the frame); head reactions need close, single-subject footage - use the loved R6 reactions (HitReaction1/2, Victimupd: statistics only) + the owner's verdicts instead.
