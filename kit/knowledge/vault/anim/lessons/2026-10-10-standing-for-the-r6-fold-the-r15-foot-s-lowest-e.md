Standing (for the R6 fold) = the R15 foot's lowest edge within 0.02 of the floor AND bearing weight (the hip above the foot: the hip-to-ankle line within 70 degrees of vertical). A knockdown's body touches the floor with its heels and stands on nothing - without the weight test the leg snapped 67 degrees to a vertical anchor.
