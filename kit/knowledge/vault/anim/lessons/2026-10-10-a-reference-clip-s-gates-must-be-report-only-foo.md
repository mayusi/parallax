A reference clip's gates must be report-only (foot_slide 10, pop 180, loop_seam 360, hit 999): a finished animator's loop may not close (Free Friday walk: 60 degrees), its feet skate, its keys snap - that IS the data. kit anim fails fast at the first failing step of a multi-clip run, so one strict reference stops every clip after it.
