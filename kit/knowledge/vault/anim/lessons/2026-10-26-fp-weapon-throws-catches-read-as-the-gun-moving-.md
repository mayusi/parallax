FP weapon throws/catches read as 'the gun moving by itself' unless they are PHYSICAL: the gun leaves the throwing hand at that hand's own velocity, flies a ballistic arc spinning at a constant rate about its centre, and the catching hand meets it moving with it, then absorbs; keyframing a path through points + snapping into the grip at the catch reads as a ghost / a jump (owner 2026-10-26, rounds 3-5). Keep the hands WIDE apart for exchanges.
