R6_IK legs are rigid 2-stud bars pinned at the FOOT and aimed at the hip: a foot placed higher than or nearer than 2 studs to its hip overshoots PAST the hip, and its far end can go under the floor (a raised trail foot in a slide dipped 0.29); a leg lying flat needs its foot lifted ~0.5 (half its thickness); a squash key that drops the hips lets the rig's floor clamp (Foot z >= 0) push the leg deeper than its key says - measure leg_slide_studs on the bake, not the key (2026-10-27)
