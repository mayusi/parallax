Authoring an R6 attack from scratch with the fresh-judge loop works: CraftCross went 3.5 -> 4.5 over seven judged versions (the owner-loved sprint: 4.0 on the same prompt). Keys that moved it: arms torso-relative (swing/out/slide), Hit on the fastest frame AND at full reach (no arm drag at the strike), a hip-lead breakdown so the strike travels 4 frames, lean <= 15, a coil turning further away than the guard, torso home before the arm. R6 HAS NO WAIST: the torso is the hips - a 76 deg torso turn swung both legs at 1400 deg/s (a whole-body snap); keep it ~45 deg. Judge packets must give world part speeds (a joint-relative head speed read as 'head locked to torso').
