A joint-turn or limb-slide LIMIT fails what the owner loved: on the clips he rated (355 loved, 101 ok, 132 bad) the loved ones turn the head further against the body (90th percentile 85 deg, bad 57) and slide limbs further out of the socket (median 0.9 studs, bad 0.3), and the loved kit cuts (GsCombo2SideRight, Katana2GyakuKesa) slide the arm 2.3-2.4 studs, past 99 percent of that library. Measure a proposed gate on the rated clips before building it; what holds a joint stays limbs through the body and parts through the floor.
