A material's own layers (a.material(layers=[rbx.layer('rust', where='chipped', amount=0.8)])): direct a surface by the report, not by eye alone - the summary says per layer the share of its PLACE that was asked and the share of the MATERIAL that makes ('rust where chipped 80% -> 5%'). A small second number with a large first one means the place is small: choose another place (cavities, low, edges), never more than 100%. 'chipped' needs chip= on that material. Layer tones come from measured scans (rbx_layers.KINDS), never typed.
