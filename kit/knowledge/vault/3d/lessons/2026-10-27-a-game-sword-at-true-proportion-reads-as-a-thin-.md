A game sword at true proportion reads as a thin rapier on an R6 body (R6 girth is ~1.6x a human's: the torso is 2 studs wide on a 5-stud figure). Keep the LENGTH at the reference's ratio to the character and widen every part ~1.5x across (X, Z) in the build (Greatsword 2026-10-27: CHUNK = 1.5 on the imported parts' sizes and centres); the grip also has to fill the 1-stud R6 fist (stretch the wrapped section, not the pommel).
