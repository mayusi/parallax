A library model's numbers can be STYLISED: the CC BY Colt 1911 assembly's grips carry checkering at a 6.2 mm pitch in their normal map (kit library inspect -> baked_detail, relief picture), while real 1911 grips are ~1.3-1.6 mm (16-20 lines per inch) — the author enlarged it to read at 9.6 px/mm. A measured real dimension (a published spec) outranks a model's number; a model's numbers are a fallback with that caveat.
