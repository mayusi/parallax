Foliage that reads natural (ivy_wall 8 -> 9): every leaf a real photographed leaf (SAM-cut, own outline + photo), its colour matched to the LIT green pixels of a photo of the plant in place (matched to all greens incl. shadows it went olive-grey), a vein normal map from the leaf's own photo, waxy roughness; the plant GROWN from sourced botany with phototropic leaves (out/up, tips down, small rolls: shingles) and per-shoot lifespans (ragged edge). Random leaf rolls read as scattered cards.
