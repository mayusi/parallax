Do not 'level' a reference blindly: the M107A1 rail measured 0.5 deg front-low - a real 27 MOA canted long-range rail, not camera tilt (the barrel was level). Check which member is straight before rotating; build canted parts in their own frame (a.frame rot) so slots and the scope follow the cant.
