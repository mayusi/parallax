A PART CAN READ RIGHT FROM EVERY SIDE AND BE THE WRONG SHAPE INSIDE (the Glock, 2026-10-10): its frame was a solid extrusion where the real Gen 5 frame is a U-shell with troughs, a rail and a rounded dust-cover nose 2.8 mm behind the slide's face. Every traced view matched (IoU 0.96+); only the reference MODEL cut at stations beside the build showed it: kit refs sections <asset> --ref <library id> (red the reference, blue ours). kit plan's Shape stage now asks for it whenever a hero has a reference model, and says when a station is over 1.5 mm off. Those sections took the Glock to the owner's 85/100.
