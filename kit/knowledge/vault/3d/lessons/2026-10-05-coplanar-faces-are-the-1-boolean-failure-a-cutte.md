Coplanar faces are the #1 boolean failure: a cutter flush with a face (rib box at the wall plane, pocket starting exactly at a face) tears or leaves fins; a fused part as wide as its partner (knuckle = leg thickness, lip resting flush) makes 3-4-face edges. Overshoot cutters, overlap fused parts; the kit now retries cut/fuse with a nudge/inflate and repairs fins + T-junction cracks.
