Grade BLIND and measure the score against the owner: 21 shuffled, authorship-hidden grades showed the old score agreed with the owner at rank 0.32 (same-object pairs 55%, pass/fail 67%). Weights refitted to them (kit grades --write: ridge toward the hand weights, every grade predicted WITHOUT itself) reach rank 0.49, pairs 70%, pass/fail 71% (re-measured under measure 6) - better than the old score on all three; against the hand weights (0.31 / 75% / 57%) better on rank and pass/fail but one same-object pair worse, and all inside the noise of 21 grades (Spearman SE ~0.22) and some measures were shaped on these same grades: only FRESH grades prove a judge. The owner's grades pull weight onto texture, surface story and triangle budget, and concavity ('silhouette') to the floor. All three score-chased agent builds now fail (two PASSED the old score); the owner's two favourite 'flat' builds fail too (the judge sees flat colour, the owner liked them) - an open disagreement to settle with more grades.
