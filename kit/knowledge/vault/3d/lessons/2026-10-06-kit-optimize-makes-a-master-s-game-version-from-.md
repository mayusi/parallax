kit optimize makes a master's GAME version from the same script: a collapse decimation to 15% of the triangles TEARS a mesh where it folds a thin wall (12 open + 6 three-face edges in the Gjallarhorn's tube and crown) — close the small tears and drop micro faces after every cut (rbx._mend). And at Roblox's 1024 map cap, small coloured details smear in the main mesh's shared map (a trigger pin got 2.7 texels): move them to a static 'detail' mesh with a map of their own (the runtime welds unjointed groups to the root).
