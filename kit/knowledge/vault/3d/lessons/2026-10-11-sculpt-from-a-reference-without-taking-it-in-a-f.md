Sculpt FROM a reference without taking it in: a.form_match(<checked model.glb>, height=, rot=) is the teacher — EXTRA / MISSING mm per region and hot spots in mm (model frame). Calibrate first (the reference itself as the build: 0.10 / 0.07 mm). Each round reads the top spots as anatomy (the 8 mm 'extra' under the eye = the canine fossa, the missing at the base = carves cut too high) and moves the clay there: the skull's own primitive sculpt went missing 1.68 -> 0.72 mm, side IoU 0.92 -> 0.95 in five rounds. Shape at a coarse voxel (0.8 mm: minutes), detail at the fine one (0.45 mm: ~25 min).
