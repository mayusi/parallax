Design-spec-driven modelling: write the parts list FROM THE MANUAL first (what / attaches / visible / source), then model to it. The M107 manual moved the barrel springs to the handguard (seen through the bottom vents), the mainspring+buffer to the rear, the chamber AHEAD of the magazine, and turned my 'sling ring' into the midlock pin's finger ring - none of which a photo alone tells you.
