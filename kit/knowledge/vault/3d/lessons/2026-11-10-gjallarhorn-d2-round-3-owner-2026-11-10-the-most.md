gjallarhorn_d2 round 3 (owner 2026-11-10: 'the most horrible thing I've seen ... worse than the 61'): never show the owner a mid-work build whose panels are machine blobs. Splitting an ornate object's panels out of ONE lit render failed three ways - hue thresholds (highlights and shadows fragment every panel), SAM clicks (whole structures come back), a material map from kit vision albedo + metalness (right in the large, wobbly edges, bronze bleeding into the brown frame). Seated as pillows, 62 noisy pieces read as camouflage, worse than a plain body. A designed panel has clean runs between real corners: redraw it from the picture's own pixels on a mm grid, and look at the build yourself before the owner does.
