A pairwise question beats an absolute grade: the owner's re-grades moved 5 points on average, and the score's fit to absolute grades is weak (leave-one-out rank +0.20). kit grades --pairs-page makes a self-contained 'A or B?' page (renders embedded, sides shuffled, authorship hidden) and --import-pairs brings the owner's picks in with the same build-identity checks as a blind round; kit grades then reports how often the score picks the owner's winner. Built with DOM methods only (innerHTML on generated pages is an XSS pattern, even with kit data).
