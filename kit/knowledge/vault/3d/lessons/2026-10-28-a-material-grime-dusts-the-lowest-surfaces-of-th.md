a.material grime= dusts the LOWEST surfaces of the object, not only crevices: on a boot's sole/welt (grime 0.35-0.4) it baked the whole sole side light grey - a pale band under the boot that read as the leg showing through. Soles and anything at ground level: grime=0. And a real boot scan warped onto an R6 block keeps its sole's WAIST at the arch: fill the leg's footprint the sole's full height or the block's corner shows
