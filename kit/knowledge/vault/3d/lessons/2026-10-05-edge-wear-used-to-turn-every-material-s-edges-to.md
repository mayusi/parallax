Edge wear used to turn EVERY material's edges toward bare grey steel (metallic, smoother): wood boards and plastics got chalky grey outlines. Fixed in rbx.py: rubbed edges go toward steel only as far as the surface is metallic; wood/plastic/rubber edges just lighten a little in their own hue.
