Fine ridged relief at ~3-5 voxels per ridge pinches off pockets the voxels cannot resolve: the tree stump came out as 1 piece + 31 specks under 7 mm, each read by the build as a sliver. rbx_sculpt drops closed pieces under 4 voxels across after meshing (facts: specks_dropped); a single sliver left where a sharp ridge folds over is a sign the relief is too sharp for the voxel — lower its amp or a finer voxel_mm.
