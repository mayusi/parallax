A take-apart jam is a missing real feature, not a path to fudge: convert its 'around (x, y, z) studs' to the part's own mm and look there. The Silverballer's 8 jams were a 1911 nose keyhole (barrel lug), a sight dovetail, a grip-safety pocket open to the rear, the hammer slot open upward, a follower 0.2 mm wider than its magazine's inside (14 - 2 x 0.8), and parts taken off but left parked in the next part's path (out, THEN aside). Rounding a tube's INSIDE corners pinches what slides in it: round the outside only.
