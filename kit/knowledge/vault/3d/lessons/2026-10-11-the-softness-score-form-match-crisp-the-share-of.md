The softness score (form_match crisp: the share of surface folding within 2 mm) is surface-wide: on a skull, pinch brushes along rims left it at 12%; a ridged relief at bone scale (s.noise(0.35, 6, kind='ridged')) took it to 21% against the reference's 22.5% with form match unchanged — the judges' 'smooth plastic' is missing fine relief, not soft rims.
