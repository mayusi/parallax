A downloaded reference model's part NAMES can lie (the CC BY 'Colt 1911 Assembly Animation': 'Trigger1' is the hammer, 'Magazine_spring_catch' the follower) and its meshes are often not watertight (topology holes = unknown for the frame and slide). Trust kit library inspect's tiles and the see-through openings (measured from the picture, any mesh), not names or genus; and a model is its author's reading of the object: cross-check a size that matters against a published dimension.
