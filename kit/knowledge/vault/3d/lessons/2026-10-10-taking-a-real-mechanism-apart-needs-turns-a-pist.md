TAKING A REAL MECHANISM APART needs TURNS: a pistol barrel tilts its chamber down and back before it slides out — a straight path collided every time. a.disassembly paths take {"turn": deg, "axis": (x, y, z), "pivot": (x, y, z)} steps (about the carried pivot); move it forward first so its hood clears, pivot at the ring it rotates in. Springs compress: the mechanism checks treat parts named ...Spring as soft (a recoil spring the slide closes on is no collision).
