TRACE ONLY ORTHOGRAPHIC REFERENCES: a 3/4 photo traced 'fine' (IoU 0.95) and the model matched a WRONG outline (yaw inflates heights, the far end shrinks). Use the maker's product side shot / a manual drawing / a blueprint, and write refs/<x>.spec.json with height_ratio from published dimensions - kit trace --spec fails a perspective photo.
