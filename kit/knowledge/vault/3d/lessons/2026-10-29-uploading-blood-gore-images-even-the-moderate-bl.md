Uploading blood/gore images (even the 'Moderate' blood decals of a gore switch) gets the uploading Roblox account moderated mid-upload: after the first ones go up, every Open Cloud call answers 403 'User is moderated'. Never upload blood or gore art (standing order, shared/orders.md); a planned content-maturity label does not make an image upload safe. Read the owner's ledger + orders for the CURRENT state right before any upload, not only at the start of a task.
