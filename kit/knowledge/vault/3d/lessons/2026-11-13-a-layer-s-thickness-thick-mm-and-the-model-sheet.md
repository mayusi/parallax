A layer's thickness (thick_mm) and the model sheet's colour plan both depend on their input. A relief is sized by the mesh group's AVERAGE texels per mm (a part given its own density by a.texel is off by that factor) and says 'too flat to show' or 'too steep for this map': believe that line and change the map, not the number. A colour plan gives roles only to MEASURED part colours (kit pack <asset> colours) read inside a TRACED outline; on a sampled palette it shows shares and the three-grey read and no roles. Measure the parts before showing the owner a sheet.
