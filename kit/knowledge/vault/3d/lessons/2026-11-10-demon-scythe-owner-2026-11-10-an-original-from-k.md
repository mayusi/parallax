demon_scythe (owner 2026-11-10, an original from kit design direction, pick 'A1 body + C demon crown': 'phenomenal ... it's original ... really well made'): the first build from the director's sizes was timid - a wire of a blade, a dot of an eye, the head lost on a 1.9 m haft; the keeper came from making the HEAD half again as big and the blade twice as wide, the red kept to the bevel's outer quarter. On a long polearm size the head against the whole silhouette first (look at the 6-view sheet before any detail). A part turned about the haft's axis must END where a face-on feature sits (the socket ran up through the eye and hid the pupil: seen only in the close-up).
