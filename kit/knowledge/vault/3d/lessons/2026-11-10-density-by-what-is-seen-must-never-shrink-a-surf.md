Density by what is seen must never shrink a surface the owner looks INTO: an automatic rule (fewer texels for faces that see out in few directions) gave the car rim's threaded lug holes half the texels and turned the baked threads to blobs, worse than before. What is hidden in use (a barrel under its tyre) only the builder knows: a.texel(part, factor); the kit only reports how open each part is.
