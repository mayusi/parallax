REFERENCES FIRST, PER PART — the owner's correction (2026-10-07): never model from one low-res picture. Before tracing: kit refs find per part and per side (full gun both sides, field strip, exploded / cross-section drawings, the maker's parts), kit refs patent, the real object's published dimensions; kit refs coverage must show >= 2 px/mm per hero feature, and ask the owner for what only they can get (a game's design: screenshots). The Silverballer re-checked against a real Hardballer photo at 12 px/mm; Browning's 1911 patent cross-section (public domain, Wikimedia) gave every internal part. For a mechanism: every real part its own piece with a.joint travel + kit schematic + kit showcase.
