A UV-SPACE PATTERN WITHOUT A REAL SIZE scales with the texture, not the object: the powder-coat look's 'coat' orange peel came out as hammered metal on an 80 mm block (cells per texture width, huge on a small part) — the looks sheet showed it at a glance. Give every micro-pattern pattern_mm (orange peel ~1.5 mm at depth 0.4, brushing ~0.3 mm) so it is the same size on a pistol and a crate. Look at kit materials --looks --sheet after changing a look.
