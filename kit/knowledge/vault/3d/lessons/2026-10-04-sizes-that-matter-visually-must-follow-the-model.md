Sizes that matter visually must follow the model's scale: a fixed 0.03-stud baked edge radius is a crisp highlight on a 5-stud rifle and a 4.9 mm blob on a 1.6-stud pistol (37% of the texture became 'edge'). Defaults are now % of the largest dimension.
