A FLAT section is not automatically more real: a skull's zygomatic arch rebuilt as s.strap (4.5 mm plate) judged 39 -> 35 ('a flat slab', 'a white cube clipping at its root'); bone arches are rounded straps. Use s.strap for straps, rims, ribs and plates seen face-on; keep ellipsoid plates for bone bars. Strap ends now stop at their end points (the overlap had stood past them as a block).
