First-person arms: lock the hands to the gun's Grip/Support anchors every frame and put the shoulders OFF SCREEN in the bottom corners (right: bottom-right past the grip, left: almost straight below the support). Shoulders behind the camera make the arms fill the screen; a left shoulder far to the side drags the forearm across the barrel. Angle the hip pose inward (~10 deg yaw) so the gun's side shows.
