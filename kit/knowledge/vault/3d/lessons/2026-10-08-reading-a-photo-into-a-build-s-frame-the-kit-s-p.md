Reading a photo into a build's frame: the kit's pistol frame has f growing toward the MUZZLE; a photo with its front on the left runs the other way (raw f = 2*fr + mm_per_unit - f_left_to_right). Check a traced part's extents against your mapping before modelling from it — the Glock's safety blade sat on the trigger's REAR for days (the face was one solid slab: the owner's 'paddle trigger').
