Measure what is SEEN: a part's whole box is not what a reference shows. The ref_sizes gate ray-casts each part from both flanks (2 mm grid, half a step added at each end) — it found the Gjallarhorn's top rails 10 of 20 mm visible because the housing's shoulder (37.5 mm out) hid their lower half.
