A check that compares two readings must carry its own NULL: take the same reading where the fault cannot be (inside one island, no seam) and judge only what is beyond it. The first seam check compared one side with the other and gave the same 20-30% with no seam at all - it read the texture. Ask a reviewer for the null test before trusting any measured share from built models.
