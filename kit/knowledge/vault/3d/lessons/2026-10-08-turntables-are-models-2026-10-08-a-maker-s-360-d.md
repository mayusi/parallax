TURNTABLES ARE MODELS (2026-10-08): a maker's 360-degree player is a fixed-camera turntable — kit refs hull finds the camera from the photos (distance, row elevations, aim, turn direction: GLOCK's turns -15 deg per column), carves the visual hull and scales it to the published length; the rest is MEASURED (G17 Gen5 FS: 202 x 34.0 x 136.7 mm vs published 202 x 34 x 139). GLOCK's player tiles: /3DPlayer/<model>/c{0..119}_l0_{y}_{x}.jpg (2x2 tiles of 758 px, 1 px overlap). Thin protrusions (a lever tip) are its weak spot: two sessions gave widths 34.0 and 32.1.
