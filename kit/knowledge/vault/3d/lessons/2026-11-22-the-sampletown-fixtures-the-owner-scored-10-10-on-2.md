The SampleTown fixtures the owner scored 10/10 on 2026-11-15 (town_house_window, town_house_door, town_avenue_lamp: 'the bricks, everything, all the details') shared one route: a written study of THREE real photos before any modelling (per reference: silhouette, proportions as ratios, shape language, why it looks good in three numbered reasons, its surface, what is weak and left out), real sizes from a sourced pack (metres / 0.28 = studs), then named CAD components each from that study. Keep the study's 'why it looks good' as the brief of the detail pass: the depth of a moulded surround, a value step, the ironmongery read.
