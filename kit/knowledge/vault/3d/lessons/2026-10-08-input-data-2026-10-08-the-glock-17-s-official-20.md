INPUT DATA (2026-10-08): the Glock 17's 'official' 204 mm was the Gen4's length (GLOCK's own manual: Gen4 204 mm, Gen5 202) — a fact belongs to a VARIANT; Wikidata / Wikipedia often give the FAMILY. Start a real object from its MAKER: the spec page, the owner's manual (component parts list with numbers, exploded drawing, a technical data table naming sibling variants), the maker's patent list ('virtual patent marking'), the maker's 360-degree turntable (24 x 5 frames at one distance and light: true side views at 3.4 px/mm, proportions within 1% of the published sizes). kit dossier records it all graded.
