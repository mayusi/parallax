A hanging chain must be BUILT threaded, never placed as a column of links: P.hanging_chain seats a loop 0.5 mm into the host and spaces flat links so each lower wire sits inside the next hole (11.5 mm for 16 mm links with a 10.2 mm hole), the ring through the last link, a bail through the ring; split a traced part's lanyard loop OUT of the 'chain' mask (cutting everything under the grip as chain removed the loop the chain hangs from: the owner, 'the chains arent even connected to the gun').
