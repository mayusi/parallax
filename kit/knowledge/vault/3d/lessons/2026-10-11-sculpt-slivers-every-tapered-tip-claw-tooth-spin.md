sculpt slivers: every tapered tip (claw, tooth, spine, horn point) under ~0.85 mm radius is a thin rod the slivers gate flags — and lost at a 0.35 mm voxel; end tapers at >=0.85 mm. A brush/pinch placed at UNSCALED points beside a scaled feature draws fins. On a mirrored canvas a sliver cluster's box centres at x=0 in thin air: read its span and 'thinnest at' (the report now prints both). Region close-ups for a design with no real object: refs/<name>.regions.json boxes on the concept's front view — the judge then sees guard/grip/pommel, not empty blade.
