A reload slide must be derived from the geometry, not from a description: the Nerf replica says 'the entire top housing slides forward', but a whole top sliding far enough for its rear overhang to clear the bay (300 mm) runs into the muzzle's claws, and one sliding 175 mm leaves its overhang over the bay (the shell cannot lift out). The working design: a fixed rear cover, the housing from the seam forward sliding 145 mm (its trailing edge past the bay), riding on rails as long as its travel, every end 1 mm clear. The kit's travel sweep and spec attaches found all three faults; the Gjallarhorn, 2026-10-06.
