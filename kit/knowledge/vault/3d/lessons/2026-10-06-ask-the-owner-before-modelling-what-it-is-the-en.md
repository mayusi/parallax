Ask the owner BEFORE modelling: what it is, the end goal (master / game / showcase), quality, how much must WORK (each moving part), the extras it needs to work (a key, a magazine, the inside of a box), references, and whose design it is. kit new asset writes <name>.brief.md with these questions and every build warns 'brief' until they are answered — the owner (2026-10-06): too much and too little both waste their time.
