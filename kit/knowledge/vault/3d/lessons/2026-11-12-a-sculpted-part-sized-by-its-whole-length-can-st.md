A sculpted part sized by its WHOLE length can still be wrong inside. The Gjallarhorn's nose wolf had the right overall length (190 mm) for five rounds while its head was nearly twice the chart's (85 mm from nose to brow against 43): a generator draws a head-and-wings with a head about as long as its wings, a carved one has a small head and a long wing. Nobody saw it until the part was laid beside the chart through one window with a mm grid. And its head pitch (--nod 18) was judged from the FRONT view, 11 degrees too far by the side view. Measure the parts of a part against each other (head against wing) in the view that shows them, before any fit; kit sharpen --tail lengthens what lies behind the head.
