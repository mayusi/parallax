A scan stands in for a material only if its STRUCTURE matches, not its colour: Concrete034 on bone gave a skull punched dark pits and a ring stamp (3924 dark colour blobs, 2 craters, 85% of its relief broad blotches). Before giving a look a scan, measure it: isolated pits below mean-3sd, colour cv, fine-vs-broad relief share. No matching scan = no scan; a sculpt's own fine noise is the porosity.
