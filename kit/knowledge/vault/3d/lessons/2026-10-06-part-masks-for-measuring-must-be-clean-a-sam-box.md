Part masks for MEASURING must be clean: a SAM box prompt can fill its box, merge with a neighbour (the Gjallarhorn's grip and guard were one trigger-guard area) or drop a thin piece (its crown lost the bottom claw); --part name=box is the whole object inside the box. Link a spec part to a traced part ("ref") only after looking at the trace overlay; prompt small parts with points or SAM 3 text.
