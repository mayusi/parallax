When the owner names a part by its role ('the biggest part holding the jewel from the outside'), find it in the game model's EXPLODED view before building: on Scarborough Fair it was the barrel's HUB block, not the jewel setting - two rounds went to the wrong part. A separate slab standing proud next to a sculpted part reads as a box too: widen the host with a blended zone instead.
