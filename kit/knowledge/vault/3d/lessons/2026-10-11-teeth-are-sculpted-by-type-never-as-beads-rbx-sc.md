Teeth are sculpted by TYPE, never as beads: rbx_sculpt_parts.dental_arch(ADULT_UPPER / ADULT_LOWER, y_occlusal=, upper=) builds chisel incisors, pointed canines, two-cusped premolars and four-cusped molars along the arch, set back into the jaw. Two judges called ellipsoid teeth 'a string of pearls'; with typed crowns the skull's form-match EXTRA fell 2.05 -> 1.71 mm (the beads stood proud of the face).
