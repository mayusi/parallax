A MODEL'S BOX IS NOT ITS BODY (2026-10-08, blind review): I recorded the Glock hull's 32.2 mm as 'width without the slide stop levers' - its box was a lever bump on one side, the magwell on the other and a 0.6 deg tilt; the NSW model's 32.5 was lever to lever. Before naming what a model's size means, find where its extremes are (vertex map: kit refs measure's mesh, numpy over the OBJ); measure without a protrusion with kit refs measure --cut L0..L1,H0..H1 (the hull: 31.8, the NSW model: 32.3, both set by the flared magwell). A measured-on fact's number is a script's key, its meaning needs two keys: kit dossier now counts such a row only once two reviewers (one blind) accepted it, and a disputed row never.
