WOODEN props: material(pattern="wood") + every board its own part. The grain follows each part's own long axis (a 45-degree brace grains along its length), each board gets its own tone, the seams come from small gaps over a dark core. The crate (4 boards a face, nails, stencils, a bolted handle) and the chest (slats round the arch from extruded outlines, bands, hinges, lock) read as real at ~54-56. The 'empty' measure still calls every honest plank face featureless (it cannot see grain) - do NOT answer its 'put grooves there' advice on planks.
