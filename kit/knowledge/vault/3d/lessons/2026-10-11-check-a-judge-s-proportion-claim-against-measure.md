Check a judge's proportion claim against MEASUREMENTS before sculpting it: 'the vault is too big for the face' was false (our cranium slices within 2-5% of the real skull's); the real cause was a guessed landmark — the bite plane at y 59 where the reference's midline profile puts it at 50 — which squeezed the maxilla. Read landmarks off the reference's midline profile (a.form_match now reports midline gaps).
