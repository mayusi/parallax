CORRECTION (owner 2026-10-28, round 5 = 3/10): R6 stands have NO fists - the hand is the arm block's own bottom 0.25-0.3 wrapped in a cuff with knuckle studs on the outer side (InspWinship stands: armour goes 0.02-0.09 below the arm bottom, never a hanging fist). Shoulder = rounded cap over the top ~0.86 of the arm at the arm's own width (+0.03 up). Armour = shells 0.05-0.10 proud of the R6 blocks; muscles = tapering stacked plates, not an anatomical torso. Arms sit ~1.4 out, tucked 0.1 into the torso. My earlier 'fists >= arm width / arms out to 2.2' read the silhouette, not the pieces - analyse reference models PIECE BY PIECE (_scratch/the_world/deep_analyze.py) and look at region close-ups before building.
