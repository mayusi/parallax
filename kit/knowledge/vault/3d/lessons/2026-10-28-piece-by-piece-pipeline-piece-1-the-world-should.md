Piece-by-piece pipeline, piece 1 (the_world_shoulder_cap): direction doc -> kit design explore -> owner pick (C4) -> kit design concept -> kit trace (--box keeps the shadow out; --part-box for a square panel; panel/gem 'overlap' doubts are expected when a gem sits on a panel) -> build FROM the trace (loft stations = the outline's half-widths row by row, slab panel, gem mesh from the traced heart with sharp facet edges + P.tag_gem, ring-pillow bezel) -> kit design review. Two traps: a VLM cannot screen a ban like 'no copy of a downloaded model' (it dropped every plate of a direction - keep such rules in the intent); the review compares the doc's View - build the piece facing its design face that way and keep fit references (an R6 arm) OUT of the piece.
