The owner kept his 10/10 on the car rim when it went from 55,304 triangles of real geometry (round 5: every round a CAD fillet, the lug threads modelled) to a 5,098-triangle low with the threads and every fillet baked into its normal map (S1): at a prop's viewing distance sub-millimetre form reads the same from a normal map as from geometry. Put rounds, threads, lettering and casting marks on the HIGH only (Asset(low=True); a.cad(..., low=False) for a part that is all detail) and spend the low's triangles on the silhouette - never cut a part's detail or quality to fit a triangle budget.
