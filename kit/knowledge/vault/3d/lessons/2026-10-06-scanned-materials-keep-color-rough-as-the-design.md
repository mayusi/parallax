Scanned materials: keep color/rough as the design's averages and let scan_mix decide how much of the scan's variation shows — 0.3-0.5 on polished or hero metal (a full Metal008 reads dirty), 0.7-1 on worn, raw or organic surfaces (leather, wood, rock). Tile size: the scan's listed size, else scan_m.
