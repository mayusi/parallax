A hero hilt built to its side TRACE passes the outline (IoU 0.93) and still reads horrible (owner 6/10, 'idk why the hilt is horrible'): the trace measures outline, not form. Four form faults it could not see: (1) the cord wrap built as nine round tori reads as a coil spring - a leather wrap is FLAT broad bands, near flush, spiralling; count the turns but copy the band's section; (2) the fitter TILTED the cross 7 deg to match a perspective photo - never fit an orientation param to a photo trace, a cross is square to the blade; (3) the cross's arm built as a straight bar + bell ends reads as a spool - the plate's arm swells at the root, thins, ends in a flat disc cap: copy the arm's profile from a crop at 1:1; (4) a placeholder escutcheon block and a stiff horizontal lanyard rod (a cord hangs by gravity). The part review answered all 11 parts 'ok' from small crops: answer each hilt part from a 1:1 render beside its plate crop.
