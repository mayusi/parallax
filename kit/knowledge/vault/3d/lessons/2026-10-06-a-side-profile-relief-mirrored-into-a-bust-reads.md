A side-profile relief mirrored into a bust reads as a lump on a head (the bas-relief squashes the big form). Give the head its form (a loft through the trace) and press only the relief's FINE part onto it: a.displace(..) before the part's cuts. 1-2 mm boolean cuts (nostrils) on the dense, displaced snout left a wire edge — let the pressed detail carry them.
