Library models are usually STAGED or ANIMATED: a backdrop plane, a punch tool lying apart, parts mid-assembly-animation and the whole gun moving in the animation all corrupt a measurement. kit refs measure / library inspect leave out flat backdrops and anything not touching the main group, measure at the most-assembled frame, and track motion relative to the biggest part. Still LOOK at the side mask: the front must point right (--front min).
