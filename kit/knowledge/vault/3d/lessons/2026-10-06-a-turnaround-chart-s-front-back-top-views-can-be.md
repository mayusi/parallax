A turnaround chart's front/back/top views can be drawn MIRRORED (a one-sided part on the wrong side, front and back swapped): the Gjallarhorn chart's three small views matched at IoU ~0.42 until flipped (0.76/0.70/0.90). trace_match now names it (reference_<view>_mirrored when the flipped outline fits >=0.15 better) — flip the PICTURE and trace it again, never bend the model to a mirror image.
