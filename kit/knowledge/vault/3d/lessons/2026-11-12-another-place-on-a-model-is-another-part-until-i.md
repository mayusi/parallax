Another place on a model is another PART until its own reference says otherwise. The Gjallarhorn has two wolves, one on its nose and a small one at the stock's heel, and they are two sculpts: the heel wolf has a bigger head and a slimmer wing. The nose wolf's sharpened part fitted small for the heel (rounds 6-7) read as a wing with a beak under it; made from ITS OWN picture - the heel cut out of the chart's side view, 182 x 100 px, redrawn large, generated, sharpened: 31 s + 114 s + 6 min - it read as a wolf at once. The originality rule holds inside one model too. And a part generated as one solid piece is a wedge: laid on flat flanks it needs kit sharpen --level, or its front half sinks into its host.
