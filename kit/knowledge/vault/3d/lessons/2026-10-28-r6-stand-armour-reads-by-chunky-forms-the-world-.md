R6 stand armour reads by CHUNKY forms (the_world r7 = 0/10 'disgusting'): the refs use a few big puffy pieces per region - straps 0.7 stud wide of fat stacked segments, the chest filled with big pillow blocks, thick irregular overhanging arm shells. Thin ribs, bead strings, barrel-hoop rings and small ellipses on a flat block read as a radiator / skeleton. Detail scale 0.25-0.7 stud, not 0.05-0.1. Before showing, render the build and the reference at the SAME camera side by side and compare chunkiness.
