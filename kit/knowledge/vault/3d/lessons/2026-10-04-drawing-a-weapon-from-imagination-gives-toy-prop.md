Drawing a weapon from imagination gives toy proportions. Use a template with real ratios (rbx_firearm) and customise on top; put the origin at the grip so tools need no Grip offset, and a.anchor() every point the game needs (Muzzle, Sight) instead of hard-coding offsets.
