R6 stand proportions measured over 21 Roblox stands (_scratch/the_world/analyze_stand.py: silhouettes in R6 studs, scale from the leg pair): torso a FULL block belt-to-shoulder 1.98-2.15 wide (a sculpted muscle torso that tapers reads 'too small for the legs' - keep the block core, sculpt on its front); shoulder span 4.06-4.29, arm reach 2.05-2.2, shoulder = flush rounded cap (arm top 4.0-4.1), never a dome on top; fists = blocks >= arm width (fist span 4.0-4.15); arm depth 1.1-1.25; legs 2.0 x 1.1-1.2. Measure your build with the same script before showing it.
