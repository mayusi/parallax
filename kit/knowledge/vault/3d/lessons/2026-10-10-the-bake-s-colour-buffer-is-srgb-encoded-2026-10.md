THE BAKE'S COLOUR BUFFER IS sRGB-ENCODED (2026-10-10): a 0.028 linear black is 0.18 there (47/255). rbx_wear compared its LINEAR burnish tone (0.16) with it raw: 'only ever lighter' never fired, and no dark finish in any build ever showed burnished edges — eight fresh judges in a row scored the Glock's surface 'no wear at all'. Fixed (rbx_wear.to_srgb) and pinned by a test. Any colour constant mixed into that buffer must be sRGB-encoded or say it is (grime's dirt is an sRGB dark brown on purpose: it darkens, so it cannot show on a black finish; chips' under is baked sRGB like the colour). The wear fix alone took the judged Glock 58 -> 61.
