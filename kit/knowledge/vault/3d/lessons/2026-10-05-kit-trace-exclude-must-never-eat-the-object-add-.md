kit trace --exclude must never eat the object: add --protect boxes over the object itself and --part to trace removed things (bipod, monopod) as their own outlines; the preview tints REMOVED pixels red - look at it. Lost real parts (a carry handle, a magwell lip) were modelled as holes before this.
