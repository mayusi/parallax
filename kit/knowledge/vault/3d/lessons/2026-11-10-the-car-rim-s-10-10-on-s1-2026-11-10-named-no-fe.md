The car rim's 10/10 on S1 (2026-11-10) named no feature: the owner's only words were 'Yes, go to S2', and a minute earlier 'Yes, fix it first' on exactly what S1 had baked - its painted lug threads and the wavy, sawtoothed edges of its sharp low corners. So a high-and-low game version keeps what he LOOKS INTO as geometry on the low: the threads (rbx_parts.thread, a light real ridge), every round that shows (rounded() corners, fillet_mm mesh rounds), round holes; only detail finer than a texel or two is baked. A tenth of the triangles is no rule and the budget never cuts a part he looks into: show the cost and let him decide (the rim: 5,098 -> ~17,000 triangles with real threads, rounds and round lug holes).
