A fillet on an extruded slab is still a slab (flat faces, square shoulders): the owner called SF's filleted frame/grips 'the straightest edges ON human history'. Sculpted plates are INFLATED with a.pillow (height from the distance to the outline: never fails, unlike OpenCASCADE offset+loft domes on traced outlines). Check a traced host's HOLES: SF's grip region was a hole in the frame, so the handle was hollow between two panels.
