The sharp_edges gate's 'bevelled share' can FLIP on a lofted part: dissect joins the loft and every fillet into one transition network and calls it bevels while it is under 10% of the part's size wide, a curved surface over it — the Glock frame read 84% at 19.2 mm and 0% at 22.9 mm with the same real edges. The gate now says when a network sits by that line (share_unreliable) and names the longest sharp runs with their place (Roblox axes, mm). Round those runs; never reshape a part to the line.
