Measure a multi-barrel section at the MUZZLE from BOTH the front view (ring sizes, centre spacing) and the side view's ridge lines (where each tube starts and ends) before modelling: Scarborough Fair = top rib 8.5 + two 29.5 mm tubes 27.7 apart (merging at a pinch, NOTHING between them) + bottom rib 7.5 = the 72 mm block. Two rounds of guessing (a flat block, then tubes joined by a filler web) each cost an owner round ('nothing in between them, look properly').
