A picture can show a moving part elsewhere than the rest pose (the owner's Silverballer: hammer at half-cock). Fit the angle on the picture (2D overlap per angle) and measure with a.trace_match(pose={'Hammer': 30}) — never move the model's rest pose to suit a picture.
