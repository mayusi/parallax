A SCORE IS NOT THE JUDGE - LOOK AT THE HERO RENDER EVERY ITERATION. Agents told to lift the starter crate, chest and blaster past the prop bar carved grooves into every plank, slatted the chest lid with see-through gaps and bricked the crate's faces: scores went 33-47 -> 55-58 and the owner graded the results 1/100. The clean versions scored LOWER than the noise. Never add detail the real object would not have to move a number; never accept a score (yours or an agent's) without opening the sheet.
