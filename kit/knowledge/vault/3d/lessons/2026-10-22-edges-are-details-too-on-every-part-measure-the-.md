Edges are details too, on every part: measure the art's rounds (kit refs edges / kit look edges: the 10-90% ramp across each edge, calibrated on Blender slabs, resolution-aware) and set round_mm from the data. On Scarborough Fair every part read sharper than the art at the same resolution (the sharpest edges equally crisp in both, so not image blur): spur -4.9, jewel -2.2, hammer -1.7, trigger -1.2, grip -1.1, barrel -0.85 mm
