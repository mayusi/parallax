A check that meets a map's triangles pair by pair dies on real meshes: one counter top with many-sided faces cut into slivers gave 8 thousand million box pairs to throw away (four minutes); three rewrites of the pair search did not fix it. Counting coverage on the map's own grid of texel centres costs what the MAP costs whatever the faces look like (141 built groups in 96 s) and gives the truer number (the share painted twice). Time a new check on the WORST built model before building on it, and set its bar from what built models measure (101 of 141 clean).
