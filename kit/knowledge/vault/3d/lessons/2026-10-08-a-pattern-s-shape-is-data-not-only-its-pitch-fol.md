A pattern's SHAPE is data, not only its pitch: fold the photo's texture into ONE lattice cell (fit pitch + angle by the folded cell's variance, after a 1.5 mm blur is subtracted) and read the feature's size off it. The Glock Gen 5 grip: 2.23 mm pitch, raked 21.65 deg, flat-topped squares 0.56 of the pitch across with ~0.1-pitch edges — the kit's pointed 0.44 'pyramids' rendered as faint specks beside the photo (now reshaped).
