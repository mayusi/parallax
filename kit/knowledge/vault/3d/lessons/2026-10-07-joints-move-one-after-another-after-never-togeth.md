Joints move one after another (after=), never together: a linkage (a 1911 barrel link swinging on its shaft WHILE the barrel slides) cannot be expressed. Ride the link with its driver and give its eye an oval for the travel (honest, collision-free), and say so — a linked joint (with=) is the kit's next mechanism step.
