REFERENCE MODELS (2026-10-08): measure a model WITHOUT its accessories (kit refs measure --skip olight): the NSW Police G17 Gen5 model's weapon light stuck out past the muzzle, so scaling the whole to 202 mm shrank the gun 5.7% (slide 175.9 instead of 186) and its 'height 130' looked like a short scan. Check each part against its own published size (slide length / width) before trusting the whole. Holster makers publish real 3D scans: T.REX ARMS' library (files.trex-arms.com/3d/manifest.json, 249 OBJ scans by maker) — a sibling's scan (G22 Gen5: slide 27.5 vs 25.5 wide) is not the object.
