PICTURES (2026-10-08): a site's image resizer can serve MORE pixels than exist (GLOCK's close-ups: 2080 px requested = an upscale of the 1040 px original) — take a page's pictures at the size the page itself uses. Detecting an upscale without its original is not reliable (the detail kept by halving depends on the content: a soft native close-up 0.50, a sharp 2x upscale 0.53), so the kit reports the number and flags nothing. End-on turntable frames are perspective views (the far end 13% small): never a square front / rear.
