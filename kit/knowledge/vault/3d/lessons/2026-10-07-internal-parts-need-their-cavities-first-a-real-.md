Internal parts need their cavities first: a real 1911 grip safety is U-shaped, the fire control sits in a pocket between the frame walls, the mainspring in a bore up the housing, the firing pin and extractor in bores in the slide. Cut them (frame cuts with w=, plate pockets= / channels=, slide bores=), then place each part from a public-domain cross-section laid on two pins you already have (hammer pin, slide stop pin) — else internals sit in solid metal and the floating / buried / interference gates fight you.
