Traced outlines need manufactured radii: rbx_trace.region(round_mm=2) fillets the outer shape BEFORE the mating subtractions (a closing would fill a magwell's corners into the magazine), then an opening after them knocks off the pointed shards two converging cuts leave (the trigger-guard spike).
