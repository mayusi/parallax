A maker's dimension drawing can show an older or simplified part (Hikvision's drew a turned spindle arm with a ball joint; the camera as sold has a thick straight arm on a 4-rib boss). Measure sizes from the drawing, but take the SHAPE of each part from the maker's current product photo - the owner judges against the photo (cctv_bullet 'not a fan of the arm and lens' -> 'A STAR').
