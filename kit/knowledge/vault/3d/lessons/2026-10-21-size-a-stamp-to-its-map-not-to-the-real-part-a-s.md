Size a.stamp to its MAP, not to the real part: a stamp's finest feature must cover >= 2.5 texels of its group's baked map (facts.texel_density px/m) — the ODM's 3 mm cross-drive screws (0.7 mm slot = 0.7 texels) and 1.6 mm seams (1.5 texels) baked as nothing on a 2048 map of a 1.5 m model; 6 mm domed heads and 2.8 mm seams read. The stamp_texels warning names them; recessed stamps now also darken the colour map (cavity) so vents / grilles read.
