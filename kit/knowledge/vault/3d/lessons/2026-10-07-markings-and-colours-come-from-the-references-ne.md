Markings and colours come from the references, never invented — but NOT by projecting a lit render onto metal (a.project detail mode made the Silverballer's right side grimy and left the other side bare: two different guns; the owner: 'two completely different textures'). Engrave each marking: a.cut(<part>, a.text(..., "engraving", depth=0.6 mm, facing=...)) at its measured place — the same metal both sides. Colours: sample the most NEUTRALLY lit reference, each from its own pixels only, and check a known-neutral (black rubber) first: a colour-graded render reads black rubber as navy.
