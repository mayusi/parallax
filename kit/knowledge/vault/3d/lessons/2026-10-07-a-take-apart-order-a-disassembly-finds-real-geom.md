A take-apart order (a.disassembly) finds real geometry errors no other check sees: a frame whose rails were one full-width slab blocked the barrel's lug (a real 1911 has two side rails, the middle open); a pin buried inside the walls cannot be pushed out (give it holes through the walls); a sight with no dovetail cannot drift out. Declare the strip early — it is a design check, not decoration.
