FLUX concept sheets for generators must be PHOTOREAL studio photographs (vision concept --sheet views), not a drawn turnaround: a drawing's outlines and flat fills reached the painted model (a treasure chest came out plain brown). Projecting the concept's views (registered, never measured) is what gives a prop its grain, bands and fittings.
