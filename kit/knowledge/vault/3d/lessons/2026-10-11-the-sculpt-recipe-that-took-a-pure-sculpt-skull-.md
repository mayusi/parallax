THE SCULPT RECIPE that took a pure-sculpt skull from judges' 28 to the owner's 75 (2026-10-11): measure the reference model first (landmarks, slices, midline) -> block at measured sizes, trim to the MESH's outline -> fit_to the large forms -> check numbers not judges' proportion claims -> close the softness gap with fine relief at material scale -> colour from the ALBEDO, never a photo's pixels -> details last (snapped creases, typed teeth that touch) -> judge only structural changes. Builds in minutes; judges at milestones.
