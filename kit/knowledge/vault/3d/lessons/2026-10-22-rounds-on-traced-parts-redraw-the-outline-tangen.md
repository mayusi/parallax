Rounds on traced parts: redraw the outline TANGENT (lines + arcs meeting smoothly where the art is smooth) and fillet in CAD (a.cad fillet_mm): OpenCASCADE refused 30 hammer edges at 1 mm on a kinked redraw and took every one after; a tangency is accepted only if its tangent point lands at the real joint (two circles touch on their centre line - an arc then swept the long way round, a 63 mm loop)
