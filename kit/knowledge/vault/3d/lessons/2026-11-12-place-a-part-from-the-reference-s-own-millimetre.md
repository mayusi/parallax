Place a part from the reference's own millimetres, never by eye from a close-up. Cut the reference through a WINDOW of the model's own frame (scale from the silhouette's length, origin at the front end and the axis) and render the build through the same window at the same pixels per mm: the two pictures lie side by side at one scale, with a mm grid on the reference. Three eyeballed close-up previews put the launcher's second wolf 50 mm too far back and 20 degrees too steep (round 6); one same-window pair put its nose on the right point (round 7) and showed in one look that the build's frame round it was wrong. A close-up shows whether a part looks good, not whether it is in the right place.
