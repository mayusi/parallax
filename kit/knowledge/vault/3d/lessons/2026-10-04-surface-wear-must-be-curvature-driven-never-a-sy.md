Surface wear must be CURVATURE-driven, never a symmetric edge mask: wear/chips/polish only on convex exposed edges (divergence of the baked tangent normal), dirt in concave corners + occlusion, broken up by large-scale noise. A symmetric mask from the bevel normal chalk-outlines every recess and panel line (measured: concave edges lightened 0.223 vs flat 0.138).
