Pick between fan models of a game prop by its HERO FEATURE at close range, not by face count: Jonathan3D's 67k Greatsword lost to lvdagmil's 23k one because only the latter has character-action's open-jawed skull (its detail lives in the normal map). A one-mesh model splits by connected islands (the blade's sheets, the grip tube); keep its own normal/metal-rough maps and relight its base colour in the export (a near-black albedo lifted to antiqued silver).
