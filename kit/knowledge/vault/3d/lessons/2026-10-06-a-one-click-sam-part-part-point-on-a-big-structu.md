A one-click SAM part (--part-point) on a big structure returns the WHOLE object: the Gjallarhorn's stock and grip came back as the whole launcher, small parts (crest, drum) as slivers. Box prompts (--part-box) gave the right parts at scores 0.83-0.95; the trace now fails a part covering >= 75% of the object.
