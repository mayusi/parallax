rbx.profile (2026-10-10): the outline operations the Silverballer and Glock builds proved, now the kit's — clip_h / clip_f / span_at / vspan / edge (a measured edge's MEDIAN: trace noise and a part in the way averaged out) / simplify / between / rotate, catmull (a measured section swept through its keys with no crease: each polyline key reads as a hard edge), smooth_run (a traced strap without its 0.1-0.3 mm pixel wiggle — extruded, each wiggle read as a ridge), u_floor / u_section (a trough or channel with a half-ellipse floor and moulded chamfers). In a build: g = rbx.profile; in a.cad code: profile.
