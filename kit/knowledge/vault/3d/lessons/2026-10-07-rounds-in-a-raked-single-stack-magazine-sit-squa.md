Rounds in a raked single-stack magazine sit SQUARE to the magazine (noses ~18 deg down in the gun): level ones need 34.5 mm in a 35 mm well and poke through its walls (the mechanism check caught it). A pistol's dust cover runs INSIDE the slide's hollow nose lobe — model the lobe hollow or the slide rams it at 8 mm of travel.
