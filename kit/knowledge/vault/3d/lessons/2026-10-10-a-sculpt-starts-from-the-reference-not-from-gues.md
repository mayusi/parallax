A SCULPT STARTS FROM THE REFERENCE, not from guessed ellipsoids (2026-10-10): s.from_traces(side=, front=) turns the kit trace outlines into an exact 2D distance raster per view, extruded through the object and intersected — the photos' visual hull as clay. The kit-make chest's two traced views came back at trace_match IoU 0.994 (side) / 0.996 (front) in 1.1 s; mode='keep' trims a free sculpt back to the photographed silhouette with one view. Sculpt the detail INTO that form.
