R6 clothes: the legs TOUCH at the middle — a trouser tube there cuts 35 mm into the other leg, an open inner side shows bare grey leg mid-stride, a boot trimmed AT the face leaves it bare. Lay the inner wall flat on the face (tube centre moved out) and keep a thin wall past it. Find these by G.coverage / G.clip_check / G.pose(stride) numbers, not by squinting at stills; a coverage ray cast from inside a solid (a boot sole) sees only the far wall — count an exiting hit as covered.
