Build 6 pass 2 (the revolver, 2026-11-11): a one-line trigger-slot notch change went straight to the build without kit cad check, and the 0.8 mm frame fillet broke on the new notch (cad_solid: Frame not valid) - a 32-minute rebuild that failed past the owner's deadline. Re-run kit cad check after EVERY a.cad edit, however small: it runs each part's fillet_mm the way the build does (3 min for the frame), far cheaper than a failed build.
