A scan stamp only where its STRUCTURE matches the surface: Rubber001's grain stamped on a skull at 0.34 mm read as clusters of dark pits (at 0.12 mm the 0.45 mm voxel lost it). Check what a height map's features ARE (dots, pores, ridges) before stamping it, and check depth vs the voxel: features under 1.5 voxels are lost.
