TWO BIG DARK MATERIALS NEED A VALUE STEP (the Glock, 2026-10-10): a polymer frame at 57 sRGB under a 47 sRGB nitride slide read to two fresh judges as 'the same blue-navy' — one mass; at 73 / 47 they were two materials, and 86 / 47 is the build the owner rated 85. The dark_finishes check (a warning) names two big dark non-metals under 15 sRGB apart, and on a hero a big dark finish with no burnish / rub. The rated values are presets for ANY material: a.material(..., life="used") (a nitride slide: burnish 0.9 over 2.5 mm, rub 0.3, smudge 0.5; the frame's polymer: color=(0.095, 0.094, 0.092), rough=0.72, its own class's).
