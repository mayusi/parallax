Settle which end is the FRONT from the maker before tracing: the Gjallarhorn render read front-right (a long thin 'barrel', a claw 'muzzle') but its designer puts the largest wolf on the FRONT, so the thin tube was the rear exhaust and the frame a thumbhole stock. One search flipped the whole layout and made the grip, trigger and stock make sense.
