A gate that decides which scorer the kit uses must be stated before the numbers and give every metric a test sized to it: three reviews rejected one-sided gates and a bootstrap that could not veto on same-object pairs (a pair enters a resample only when both of its builds are drawn). kit grades now runs a sign test on the pairs the two weightings order differently, McNemar on pass/fail and a bootstrap on rank: a refit is refused when any test says it is worse (p < .05) and kept only when one says it is better (p < .20) - measured on these grades, that keeps a refit that is NO better about 1 time in 4, and the sign test vetoes a pairs loss only from 5 discordant pairs. With ~20 grades nearly everything is noise: the weights record the grades they were fitted on, and kit grades reports them on FRESH grades (given later) - ask the owner for fresh blind rounds to prove a judge.
