Read a gun's SECTION off its FRONT view before modelling the barrel: Scarborough Fair's over/under barrels are two separate ROUND tubes with a groove between them (the owner, v1: 'barrels are both rounded'), not a flat-sided block — a stadium section looked fine from the side and wrong from every other angle. Inlays / logos on a curved part: cut the traced shape from a thin SKIN of the part's own surface (extruded shape intersected with an offset shell), never a flat plate - a plate floats off a tube.
