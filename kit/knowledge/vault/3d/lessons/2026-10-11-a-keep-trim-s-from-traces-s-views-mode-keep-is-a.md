A keep trim (s.from_traces / s.views mode='keep') is a PRISM through the whole object: every thin island or spur in the traced outline (a skull's styloid and condyle neck seen side-on) survives as a fin of clay wherever the sculpt has volume there — the judge saw 'dark spikes under the ear', the gate a 0.9 mm sliver. Trim with min_mm=4 (the report now warns of thin outline features). And a crease drawn by eye floats over a dome (skull sutures 4 mm above the vault cut nothing): crease(..., snap=True).
