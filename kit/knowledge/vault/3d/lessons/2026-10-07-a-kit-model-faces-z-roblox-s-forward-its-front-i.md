A kit model faces -Z (Roblox's forward): its FRONT is at -z, its back at +z — a part 'at the back' goes at +z. A reference made by kit refs measure has its front at the length's max end, shown pointing right like the kit's own side view, so they line up only when the build follows -Z = front (a grip put at -z read as a mirror image: trace_match's reference_side_mirrored said 0.59 as drawn, 0.999 flipped).
