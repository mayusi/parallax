HERO WEAPONS (2026-11-10, measured on the record): the loved weapons (rim 10, karambit 9, ODM 9, Scarborough 9, EA and scythe 'phenomenal') are NAMED COMPONENTS each made by one machining step in CAD from 3D or engineering truth sharper than their smallest feature (a reference model's outlines in mm, sourced specs, the game's orthographic renders, the director's own sections); the failed ones were read off a LIT 2D picture. Gjallarhorn round 3's 62 colour-traced panels were 8-10 source px wide (1.40 px/mm) with outlines turning 2.0-2.3x a clean shape (4-5 direction flips per 100 mm; reference-model outlines 1.00-1.09, 0-0.35): the shading became the shape - 'the most horrible thing I've seen'. Before building from any colour trace measure it: median width in source px (>= 12) and the turning index (sum |turn| / 360, ~1 for a designed shape); a hero's panels are drawn as designed parts, never taken from a render's colours (graveyard G9).
