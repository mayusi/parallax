kit trace's perspective check assumes straight members (a barrel keeps its thickness); a curved organic form fails it falsely (the skull's side photo: '25% along its length'). kit trace --organic skips it; kit make passes it when the object's words name an organic form (make.ORGANIC_WORDS, whole words: 'rocket' is no rock).
