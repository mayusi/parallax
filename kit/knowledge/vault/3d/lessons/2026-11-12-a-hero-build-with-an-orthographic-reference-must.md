A hero build with an orthographic reference must DECLARE it (a.trace_match) from its first round. The Gjallarhorn's anniversary build never did, so the kit's outline check never ran on it and its whole lower rear stayed misread through 7 rounds: the stock's thumbhole was built as a trigger guard with the trigger in it and a pistol grip behind it, the stock 26 mm too far back. The same check, declared later against the official chart's side view, failed that frame at once (reference_side_local down to 0.36, the model up to 97 mm off, IoU 0.878). Trace a chart view of a pale object with kit trace --cutout: a plain trace took the pearl panels for backdrop (10 holes instead of the 2 real ones).
