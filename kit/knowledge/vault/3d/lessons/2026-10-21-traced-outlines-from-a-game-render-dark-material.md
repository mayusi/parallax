Traced outlines from a game render: dark materials (black grip panels, a jewel's black bezel) read as BACKDROP in kit trace - notches along the grip edge and a false hole (the owner: 'rough edges'); close the mask across dark areas, fill pockets under ~1500 px that no part covers, and smooth each part mask (Gaussian sigma ~6 px, re-threshold) before it becomes a polygon; frame slabs then round at 6 mm.
