Reference-free SigLIP judging does NOT track the owner: zero-shot quality prompts correlate ~0, look-alike kNN 0.25 at best, and adding either to the fitted score made it WORSE leave-one-out. SigLIP's strength is similarity to a REFERENCE (photo, style target) - use it there, not as a free-floating taste meter.
