A platform limit is two numbers - what its maker SAYS and what you have SEEN arrive through your own route: keep them as two constants (rbx_optimize.ROBLOX_MAX_TEX 4096 said, GAME_MAP_CAP 1024 seen) and let only the test move the second. H3's first version moved game maps to 2048 on a staff post alone; had our uploads arrived at 1024, every optimized main mesh would have been HALF as sharp as the four 1024 maps it replaced.
