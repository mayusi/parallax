Measure a claim with the very CALL the product makes: 'never under Blender's packer' was measured with pack_best(at_least=None), while the builder passes at_least = the box cover x 1.005 - a different start for a search that is not monotonic, and the real call lost on one set. And when a number is the edge of a razor (a gain threshold that six equal squares sit 0.02% under), do not pick a fixture on it: change the rule (keep any fuller layout, SAY it only from 3%) until the fixture sits a margin away.
