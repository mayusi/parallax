A check's allowance must come from what was APPROVED, never from the piece under test. The drift check (a built effect held to its timing animatic) gave a light that 'takes its time' a quarter of its own arrival on top of one drawing: a spark at the beat's start made the effect's arrival three frames long, and that bought a snap three frames late - the very case the gate says is named. If the measured thing can widen its own tolerance, it will. And when a tolerance seems too strict on a real piece, look at how the piece is READ before loosening the rule: the real fault was the read (it timed the run that holds the brightest frame, not the first arrival).
