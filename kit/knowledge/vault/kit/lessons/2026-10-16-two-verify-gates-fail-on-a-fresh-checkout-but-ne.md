Two verify gates fail on a FRESH checkout but never on the owner's: tests that read a git-ignored data folder (_artifacts/r6data) and a bake whose derived steps run before the phase that writes what they read (kit anim's rearm before the R6 fold). Prove HEAD in a sandbox worktree with an EMPTY build/animations once in a while; a gate that is green only on one disk is not green.
