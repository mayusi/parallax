A tool that makes something reads its own output back against its plan before it calls it made. kit vfx animatic records its flat shapes and then measures where each beat's light arrives and how much of the frame it covers: its first run failed its own check four times in ten minutes, and each failure was a real fault - a colour the read did not see, shapes cut by the booth's ground, and a clock fault in the two sprints BEFORE it (recordings read by the chart's time instead of their own real time). No review had found that one.
