A check's tolerance can hide a wrong MODEL. The timing animatic's own check allowed 1.5 frames between where its plan put a hit and where its recording showed it; the clock model behind it (a sum of the hit-stops) was itself up to a frame and a half off, so a real two-frame error on two beats passed for hours. Made exact (the recorder's loop, frame by frame, held to the times a REAL recording posted), the error stood out at once and its cause was real: the hit lay between two drawings. Before you trust a tolerance, hold the model to one real output of the real tool (a golden test, never numbers made by the same formula), then look at what the tolerance was hiding.
