Identical parts stacked on ONE place in the UVs must also be cut into the SAME triangles: BEAUTY picks an n-gon's diagonals by shape and a turned copy's float noise flips its ties, so the copy's corners were laid from other islands (the car rim's Spoke2: 54 corners guessed, a striped blotch on one spoke, rework 6ebz, 2026-11-10). rbx_uvparts.triangulate_like cuts each copy along its original's diagonals and keep_copies checks every pair again on the triangles (facts copies_dropped). And make copies by turning ONE part (a.cad(..., rot=)), never by turning inside the CAD code: each angle is meshed and rounded its own way and only two of five spokes matched.
