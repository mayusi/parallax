Draw it before you trust its tests. The town generator passed the world check with 0 errors and its first plan page showed blocks with an empty half facing a street (70% of the fronts built) and the landmark 10 studs off the gate street's line of sight. And sweep SIZES and option sets, not only seeds, before writing 'always': my 30-seed sweep at one size found a landmark every time; a fresh reader's sweep found 139 of 3,400 towns without one at another size and gate pair, and a crash on a range typed backwards.
