A lock that holds a build to its plan must hold each PART, not the build as a whole: 'the build used a.repeat somewhere' let one token repeat of two cover a plan of twelve teeth and a fence (the methods lock's review, 2026-11-13). Count per part and per piece from the build's own record, and prove the lock on a REAL build of a bad fixture - a probe that calls the checking function directly stays green when the wiring is removed.
