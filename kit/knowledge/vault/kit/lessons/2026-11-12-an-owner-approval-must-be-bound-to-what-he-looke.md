An owner approval must be bound to what he LOOKED AT, not to what it was made from. V1's style sheet was approved by an id hashed from the preset, the set and the chart's plan: the same preset recorded again (a texture changed, the preset's file did not) and drawn again kept the id, and the old yes read 'approved' for pictures he never saw. Bind three things: the artefact names its pictures (a hash read from the picture files at every check, never from its own record); the record keeps that hash and ONE comparison is shared by the lock and the page (LaneLocks.holds); the click names what the page showed and the command is told it too (--expect) and reads the artefact once more itself. A second fresh review found it: the first review's fix was only half of it.
