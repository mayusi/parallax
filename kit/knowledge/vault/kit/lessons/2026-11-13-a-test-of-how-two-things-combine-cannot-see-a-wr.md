A test of how two things combine cannot see a wrong order when the two commute: a row inside a row, a part whose own centre is the origin, a mirror across an axis the row does not run along all pass either way (the repeat stage's review, 2026-11-13). Pick pairs that do NOT commute: a ring inside a row, geometry far from the part's origin, a row along the mirror's own axis, a turned frame
