When each review of a fix round finds its serious faults in what THAT round added, stop adding. Three reviews of the timing animatic's fixes each found holes in the newest rule (an allowance for slow arrivals, a special floor for our recordings, a layer started 'a thousandth early', a bundle looked up by name). The round that held took them OUT: one rule instead of two, the exact sum the player itself computes instead of a safety margin, and a third answer - 'not read' - where the checker cannot tell, instead of a cleverer guess. Before the next fix, list what the last round added and ask of each: can the fault be removed by removing this?
