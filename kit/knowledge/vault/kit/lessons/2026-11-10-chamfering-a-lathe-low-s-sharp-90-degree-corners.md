Chamfering a lathe low's sharp 90-degree corners (the mid-poly fix for wavy edge highlights) leaves no edge over the seam angle: the whole revolve becomes ONE closed region (topologically a torus), and an unwrap that sends closed regions to the smart projection drops the map from 78% to 25% used (the car rim's barrel, rework 6ebz). Open a closed region at its sharpest edge loops before classifying it (rbx_uvparts._open_closed), then cut it into strips as usual.
