A number that describes a picture is laid on the frames BEFORE it is trusted, and the page that shows it is built first. V0 (2026-11-12): the first timing read called the brightest frame 'the hit' - on a fire arc that is there in one frame and then creeps brighter for 0.7 s, that is frame 25 of 28, and the read said 'lead-in 0.63 s' where the frames show a one-frame snap. The unit tests passed (made discs peak when they arrive); one look at the first dailies page showed it. Same look, two more: a pad round our beat let the next beat's flash in, and an upload's doubled frame was called a freeze. So: make the look-at page the first thing a measuring tool produces, run it on one real clip, and write the test from what the real clip broke.
