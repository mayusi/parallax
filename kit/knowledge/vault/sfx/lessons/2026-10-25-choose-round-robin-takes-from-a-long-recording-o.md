Choose round-robin takes from a long recording on PEAK-ALIGNED windows (what align:peak actually plays): picking splashes by onset windows gave a worst pair of 11 and the family gate failed three times; peak-aligned the best four scored 6.45 and passed first time (a puddle splash can peak 0.2 s after its onset). Envelopes / high-passes on the body to force a family only made it worse
