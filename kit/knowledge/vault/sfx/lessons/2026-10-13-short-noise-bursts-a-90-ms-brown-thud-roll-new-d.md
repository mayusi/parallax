Short noise bursts (a 90 ms brown thud) roll new dice every take: the same recipe swung 8 % -> 38 % sub between takes. Give noise layers a fixed "seed" and let takes vary by pitch_spread / modal strike / round-robin samples; the set gate (takes differ but stay in the family) catches both failures.
