a flying crescent wave must FACE the camera that sees it: an arc standing in its own flight plane (rotate Y 90) read as a sliver from behind and in front (celestial_slash round 1); stand it in the camera's plane (rotate Z = its tilt) and turn the world flight into the arc's frame for move (local = dy*sin, dy*cos, dz) - and make it outgrow its flight (scale 9 over 150 studs) or it shrinks to nothing
