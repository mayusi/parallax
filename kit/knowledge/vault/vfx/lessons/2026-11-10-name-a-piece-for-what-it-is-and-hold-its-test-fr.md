Name a piece for what it IS and hold its test frame to the scene's own intensity (flame_arc_keybeat, REDO 2026-11-10: 'you're saying Flame Arc dance ... the whole thing ... even if it's just a slash, it just doesn't look good'). The technique's name promised all five beats of its breakdown (ignition, the dance arc, the trailing ribbon, the clash sparks, the embers); only the arc was shown. Show one beat under that beat's own name (flame_arc_dance_arc), or build every beat. And beside the scene's key frame ours was a smaller, softer, uniform crescent: the scene's arc fills the frame with a WIDE white-yellow inner band, a saturated orange body, torn dark-red flame chunks off the rim and sparks - layers, light and density ours lacked.
