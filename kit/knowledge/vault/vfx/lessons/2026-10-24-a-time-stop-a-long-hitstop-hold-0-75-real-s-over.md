A time stop = a long hitstop (hold 0.75 real s over span 0.07) + a swarm whose DRAG spikes to 50 for exactly that span (and whose attract lets go at its end): the rocks lose their motion while time is stopped and fall from rest after, instead of flying on with the pull's momentum. Freeze MID-motion (a wide field), not after it has collapsed into a ball - tune both with kit vfx swarm (gravity_lance).
