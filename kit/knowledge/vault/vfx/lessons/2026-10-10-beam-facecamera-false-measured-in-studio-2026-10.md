Beam.FaceCamera=false (measured in Studio 2026-10-10): the beam becomes a ONE-SIDED wall whose width runs along Attachment0's Y axis and which is visible only from the attachment's -Z (LookVector) side — invisible edge-on from above and from behind its back. A wall of light seen from the side = two such beams with flipped attachment orientation (VfxStage beam plane: up | flat).
