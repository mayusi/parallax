Big strikes: beams cap at 50 studs wide — a giant energy column is a Neon/ForceField Cylinder mesh element. Stage long effects in phases (uplink, lock, inhale, impact, aftermath) with delays, match the server's impact delay to the preset's impact beat, and keep the dust wall smaller than the play space (a camera inside it washes everything out).
