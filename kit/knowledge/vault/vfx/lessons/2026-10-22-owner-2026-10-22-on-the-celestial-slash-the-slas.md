owner 2026-10-22 on the celestial slash: 'the slash u made isnt even straight' - a crescent tilted 15 degrees off level that climbed 30 studs in flight, beside a lopsided diagonal smear, reads CROOKED. A slash wave is level (or exactly on the swing's line), symmetric, and flies dead straight down the blade line; the smear is the same arch in the same plane so the cut visibly becomes the wave. Any rise/dissipation happens after the flight, never as drift during it
