A layer's 'point': '@name' is NOT one of the preset's own points: '@' names the booth RIG's points (a beam's '@guard' / '@tip' = the actor's weapon), and a sprite / mesh / light / sound with '@contact' silently fell to the origin (flame_arc_v2 2026-11-11: the clash drew at the ground, the blade beam stood upright). Preset points ('points': {...}) are referred to by plain name ('from': 'guard') or given as coordinates - look at a new layer's position in the first recording.
