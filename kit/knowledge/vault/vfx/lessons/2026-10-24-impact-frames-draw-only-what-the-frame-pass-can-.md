Impact frames draw only what the frame pass can see: lit Lambert shapes (the actor, debris, Lambert swarms) become INK figures, bright saturated layers become ENERGY; Glass / dim meshes, decals and the ground are paper. Put a hit's frames on the beat whose shapes are drawable AND at their peak: hoarfrost's crack frames at the eruption were empty (Glass ice, flat at the hit) - moved to the SHATTER (shards = debris = ink figures) they hold the instant the crown breaks. If a strike needs its shape grown under the frames, start it ON the hit (the hit-stop's span runs the effect clock under the frames); never before it (that is a ramp into the hit).
