Measurable lessons become TASTE RULES (kit/knowledge/vault/vfx/director_rules.json, checked by lune run kit vfx director lint <name> and inside director review): a mistake caught once is caught on every future effect. The first five: trails on meandering swarms draw hair, dots under 0.55% of the camera distance vanish, a ground ring past 0.6 of the camera distance is a plate, pale grey cel smoke under a warm/day sky reads as rocks, untextured Neon meshes read as plastic (it flags the rejected Rasengan on its own).
