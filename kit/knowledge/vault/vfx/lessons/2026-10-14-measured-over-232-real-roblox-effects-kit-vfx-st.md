Measured over 232 real Roblox effects (kit/vfx/study.luau, _roblox_pro_norms.json): pros keep ~44 % of layers NON-additive (LightEmission 0) for contrast and colour, lives median 0.58 s, curves front-loaded (91 %), streaks VelocityParallel, rings VelocityPerpendicular; black = normal-blended dark particles. My all-additive, long-lived mesh effects washed out — build to these numbers.
