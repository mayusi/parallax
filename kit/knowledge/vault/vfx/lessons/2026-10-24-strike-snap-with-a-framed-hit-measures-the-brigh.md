strike_snap with a framed hit measures the brightest CALM frame up to h + 0.35 s + the frames' held time - a second beat rising inside that window (firewhirl's collapse ring 0.45 s after the pinch, behind 0.17 s of frames) is read as the strike's own slow ramp. Read metrics.json views.<v>.frames: if the curve snaps but the gate says slow, find what peaks late in the window and move it out; a light that INHALES (dims) in the 0.3 s before the hit puts the pre-hit minimum at the hit (anticipation) and kills a drift
