Matching a reference strike: the drama is the CLOUD COLUMN and a THIN beam, not a fat one. Particles take offset (emit up in the sky) and area (emit through a Box/Sphere/Cylinder volume): a burst of big vol_smoke puffs in a tall cylinder 200-700 studs up + a wide bank on top reads as a cumulonimbus; light the inside with lightEmission, the bank with lightInfluence. Review it from a far scriptable camera (BindToRenderStep at Last, unequip first-person tools).
