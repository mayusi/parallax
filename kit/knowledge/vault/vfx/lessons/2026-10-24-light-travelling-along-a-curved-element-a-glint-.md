Light travelling along a curved element (a glint on a wire, a pulse along a beam): a short window on the element's OWN arc - sweep head and tail both advancing (head [[0,0.08],[1,1]], tail [[0,0],[1,0.9]]) - follows the exact curve; a sprite riding a path is a chain of short emitter windows and reads as specks off the curve (seam). A taper strip (seam_cut) makes the window a sliver, not a block.
