A runtime mesh alone reads as SOLID matter even with a wispy alpha skin (vortex's torus read as marbled stone); the owner-loved hybrid fixes it - the mesh carries the SHAPE (a torus), a sheath of short-lived soft lit cards on its rim (a Cylinder-surface emitter riding the same path) carries the softness. Keep the sheath thin and faint or it buries the shape (vortex round 3).
