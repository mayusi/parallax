Detail pass 2026-10-15 (after the player's VelocityParallel fix): round 'smoke' / 'dust_billow' textures read as BUBBLES or grey blobs at small sizes - use ul_wispy_smoke, fewer and thinner; any flat sprite ring (ring texture) seen edge-on is a white bar - make rings feathered mesh discs facing their flow; a tube shell starting at a muzzle needs fadeIn or it shows a hard dark wedge; a burst of streaks from one point needs a wide spread or it fuses into one capsule.
