Translucent saturated Neon on a LIGHT ground is a colour wash: a ground ring's crest, its crack decal and its point light each painted a pink / lilac PLATE on sand (sumi_seal, gravity_lance) - even after the size was right. On light ground the ground answer is opaque (an ink ring, rocks, dust) or the colour stays in the air. Taste rules neon-band-light-ground, glow-decal-light-ground, light-paints-plate.
