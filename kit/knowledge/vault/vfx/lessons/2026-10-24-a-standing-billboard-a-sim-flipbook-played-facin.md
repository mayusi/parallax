A standing billboard (a sim flipbook played FacingCameraWorldUp at the origin) whose bottom sits AT or under y = 0 is sliced by the ground plane along a straight horizontal line - it reads as a hard flat base however soft the sim's floor fade is (quenchstrike's steam, 2026-10-25). Seat the card's bottom just above the ground (point y = size / 2 + 0.2) and let edge_floor fade the base
