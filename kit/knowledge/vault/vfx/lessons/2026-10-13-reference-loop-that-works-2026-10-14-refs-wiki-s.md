Reference loop that works (2026-10-14): refs wiki <set> <wiki:Page> (whole pages via the API; a 0-image page = wrong title, the tool lists the real ones) -> refs rank (auto hue; LOOK at the numbered sheet) -> refs measure -> build -> record -> refs compare (colour score 0-100). Measured colours are what the anime frame SHOWS: feed them straight into additive Neon layers and they wash out (Kaioken 55/100); pre-saturate per form (x1.6 yellow .. x2.4 red/orange) and KEEP whichever version compare scores higher. compare judges colour only — look at the sheet for shape.
