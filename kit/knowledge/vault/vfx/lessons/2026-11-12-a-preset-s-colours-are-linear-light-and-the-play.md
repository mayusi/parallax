A preset's colours are linear light, and the player blooms a flat bright card: a colour written as it should LOOK shows brighter and yellower ([255, 140, 25] on a Neon card showed pale yellow, hue 56; [150, 22, 0] shows (254, 146, 38), hue 30). Anything that must land inside a measured colour band - a blocking shape the timing read has to see - gets its colour from what was MEASURED to show there, and the tool reads its own recording back and says when its shapes are not seen.
