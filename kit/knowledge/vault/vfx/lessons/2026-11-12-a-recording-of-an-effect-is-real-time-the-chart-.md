A recording of an effect is REAL time, the chart is the effect's clock. The player's clock stands still through every hit-stop (and runs slow through a timewarp) while the recording runs on: a beat the chart puts at 1.90 s, with hit-stops of 0.12 and 0.06 s before it, is at 2.08 s of its recording. Reading a beat's stretch of a recording by its chart time shows the beat BEFORE it for every beat after a hit-stop (flame_arc_v2's ribbon, clash and embers were read so by the dailies and the style sheet until 2026-11-12). Cut the stretch by the recorded preset's own clock (denseclip.clock_of) and end it two frames before the next beat.
