A camera that moves inside a shot makes every frame differ from the last: the made flame, drawn on twos, traced as 12 one-frame drawings under a 3 px pan and as 6 two-frame drawings once the background was followed (kit vfx track, trace --track). Film grain alone reads as a trembling camera unless it is smoothed away before points are followed; a flat background must be said to be unfollowable, never guessed
