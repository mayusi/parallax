Moving a colour of a straight-alpha picture: move its LIGHT (colour x alpha) and let the new alpha be the brightest moved light, never the moved alpha - moving the alpha with it draws an opaque dark ring round any shape that has little of that colour (ice blue, cyan); and test with a coloured shape, white hides it
