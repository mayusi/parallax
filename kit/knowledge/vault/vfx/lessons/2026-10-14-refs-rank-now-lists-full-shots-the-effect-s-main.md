refs rank now lists FULL shots (the effect's main piece uncut by the frame) + a _full.png sheet, and refs silhouette measures those. LOOKED at the SSJ full-shot sheet: most 'full shots' are golden HAIR on cards / HUD screens / portraits, only ~4 of 40 are full-body auras - colour masks cannot tell an aura from same-coloured hair, so outline advice for golden auras (widen x2.45) is wrong. Trust silhouettes only for effects whose colour the character does not share (Spirit Bomb, Bankai crescent); for auras, pick the stills by eye (--pick).
