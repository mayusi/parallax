A sweep that starts at ZERO length has no centreline at its own hit: slash frames there draw nothing (frames_draw_cut 0 - sandfall's gust; seam only passed because its taut thread was alive at the cut). Enter the cut already partly drawn (head [[0,0.35],[0.15,1],...]) or keep a guide arc alive across the hit. Also: a Glass haze/shimmer shell at transparency 0.93 reads as a pale BUBBLE in the player - keep it near 0.985.
