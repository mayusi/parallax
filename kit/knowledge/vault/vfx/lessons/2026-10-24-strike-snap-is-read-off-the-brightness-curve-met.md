strike_snap is read off the brightness CURVE (metrics.json views.<v>.frames[t, mean]): hi = the brightest frame within 0.35 s AFTER the hit - anything brighter landing in that window (quenchstrike's lit water ring, mean 47-50 vs the sear's 39.8) makes a clean strike measure slow; t10 = the last frame below 10 % of the rise BEFORE it - ANY drift into the hit counts (a decal warming colour, a light changing colour, sparks still accumulating). Read the curve first (kit vfx director review prints it now); make the strike dwarf the scene's drift rather than chasing each drift. Quenchstrike took 6 blind rounds on this before reading the curve.
