A whole technique built as a THIN stack reads cheap beside his loved pieces: flame_arc (REDO 2026-11-11, 'genuine dookie') was 61 layers of 5 kinds, 85 % flat sprites; his loved pieces use 7-13 kinds and at most 67 % sprites, starblade r6 (9.5) 148 layers of 13 kinds (mesh shells, beams, lights, screen + fov + shake, decals, debris, a trail, a glow, sound). The taste rule thin-stack (kit vfx director lint) names it; build a key beat's look from mesh shells + rendered textures + light + camera + ground, not more sprites.
