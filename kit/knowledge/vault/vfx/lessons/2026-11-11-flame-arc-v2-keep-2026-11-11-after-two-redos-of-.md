flame_arc_v2 KEEP (2026-11-11, after two REDOs of the same scene - 'genuine dookie'): what changed was the ROUTE, not the tools. (1) His own look first: the beat chart with every beat beside the real scene frame and ONE test frame of the key beat, approved in his words ('Yes, build it') before anything else was built, and the source clip confirmed by him - the REDOs stood on a blanket approval an agent wrote onto the chart. (2) The full stack like starblade r6: 74 layers of 11 kinds - textured MESH shells (fat flame-tongue strips scrolled out along a tilted swing plane: saiyan_ssj_3d's loved shell mechanic on a slash), beams for the burning blade, lights, screen + fov + shake on both hit-stops, ground marks, debris, sound - where the REDOs were 61 layers of 5 kinds, 85 % flat sprites. (3) The scene's own measured colours held beat by beat: a deep orange-red body with dark-red flames inside and a crisp white-hot band (hard cel bands), read against the scene's key frame with kit vfx keyread before he saw it.
