Blowing sand / dust is GRAINS streaming (a swarm attracted far downwind + curl), not a cloud: lit dust-puff sprites read as cumulus, smaller ones as cotton clouds, velocity-aligned squashed ones as grey feathers scattered every way (sandfall rounds 1-3). Three forms of one wrong idea = change the idea, not the numbers.
