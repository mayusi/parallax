An imported texture source must be RGBA with alpha = the mask: an RGB PNG loads fully opaque in Blender and the finished sheet becomes a square plate (fxfinish warns not_clipped=1.0). A massive travelling wave needs a THICK texture made for it (tex_getsuga_crescent.py) - the thin slash arcs + stacked echoes read as a row of separate arcs at 130 studs/s.
