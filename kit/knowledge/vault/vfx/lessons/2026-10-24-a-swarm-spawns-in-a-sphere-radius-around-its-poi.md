A swarm spawns in a SPHERE (radius around its point): a 15-stud radius on the ground puts half of it up to 15 studs in the air - noctiluca's plankton floated as a cloud and buried the effect. For things ON a surface: several small local swarms placed where they belong (radius 2-3) + gravity + a floor. For a RING of particles on the ground: a sprite with shape Cylinder, style Surface emits on the rim only (the player and Roblox) - rings started outward in time read as a travelling wave.
