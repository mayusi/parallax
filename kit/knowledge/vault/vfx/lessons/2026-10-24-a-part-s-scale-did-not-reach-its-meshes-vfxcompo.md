A part's scale did not reach its MESHES (VfxCompose: an eased scale {ease, from, to} was not scaled and a mesh with no scale got none) - a shock_ring used at 0.42 kept its full 26-stud crest and filled the floor. Fixed 2026-10-24 with a spec; when a part looks the wrong size, read the expanded preset (kit vfx expand) before tuning the numbers.
