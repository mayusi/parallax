A made fixture of FAT shapes hides what a real frame and a thin shape show (kit vfx trace / ink, 2026-11-13). Three faults passed every test on a made flame and were found by a fresh reviewer or by ONE real probe: (1) an outline read through the CENTRES of the boundary pixels is a pixel thin - a square scores 98 percent and looks right, a line 3 px wide covers two thirds of itself; (2) 'the same as the frame before' is not 'held': a shape sliding a pixel a frame was one drawing held for ever - compare with the run's FIRST frame and say drift apart from a hold; (3) a curve through sparse points bulged 19 px past them on a real traced beat (long straight steps beside short ones), never on the fixture. So: a fixture needs a thin shape, a slow slide and uneven point spacing; the first real probe comes BEFORE the review; and every new rule gets one deliberate break of the code that must fail a test (the flat-tones rule made no difference on the flame until a striped shape proved it).
