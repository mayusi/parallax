Reading WHEN a beat's light arrives in a recording: (1) take the FIRST frame at most of the light its stretch reaches - a read that times 'the run holding the brightest frame' took the next beat's light, two frames early in this stretch's last frame, for this beat arriving thirteen frames late; (2) light that is already at the beat's strength in the frame BEFORE its stretch, with no jump when the stretch begins, is an EARLY arrival (walk back to where it began) or the beat before never going out - read inside the stretch alone it is 'there from the first frame', which is exactly what on time looks like; (3) ONE floor for every recording, the timing read's own: a special floor for one side let an 80 % spark pass as the beat; (4) allow ONE drawing and nothing more: an allowance taken from the effect's own slow start let a spark on time buy a snap three frames late; (5) a beat whose light is not its own - under three frames before the next, or another beat's shape still lit when it hits - is NOT READ, and said: read through its neighbour it was wrong either way.
