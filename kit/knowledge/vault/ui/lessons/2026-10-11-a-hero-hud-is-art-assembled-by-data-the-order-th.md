A hero HUD is art assembled by data. The order that worked (character-action gauges, Umbra gauge, 2026-10-11): real stills measured at 1:1 into REFS.md -> identity from those numbers -> every piece rendered (kit ui render: text/ring/gauge/gem) or traced (kit ui trace), painted motifs from FLUX cut out -> assembled from anchors (kit ui gauge; crest_width sets the scale) -> a look with sampled numbers at every milestone -> two fresh judges, median, gaps in the order they agree -> promote with the data. Every skipped step cost a round. `kit ui plan <Screen>` is this list as a stage board.
