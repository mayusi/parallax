When a holder is curved, everything inside it must follow the curve: the owner caught level rectangular cells sitting in a bowed bar ('is it that hard to understand'). Rule for any rendered frame with live children: the render writes each slot's tangent/scale into its anchors and the screen places children by their centre and rotates them to it. Never lay axis-aligned children into a warped parent.
