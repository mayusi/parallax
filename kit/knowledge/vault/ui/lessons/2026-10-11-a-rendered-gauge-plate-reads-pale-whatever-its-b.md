A rendered gauge plate reads pale whatever its base colour when the frame is a mirror metal: Cycles reflects the area lights. For dark game steel use frame {base ~0.1, roughness 0.4}, exposure ~0.12 (measure: the plate's mean RGB under ~90 of 255), and wear_scale ~50 for a brushed grain (the default 18 reads as marble blotches at HUD size). A text render can carry a 1-2 px hairline across the whole picture (a glyph's open contour); the render now clears it (despeckle: erode 2, re-grow 4, keep only there).
