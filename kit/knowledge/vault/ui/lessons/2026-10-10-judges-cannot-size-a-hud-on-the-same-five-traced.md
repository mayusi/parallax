Judges cannot size a HUD: on the same five traced gauges one pair read the crest as 1.4x too big and the next pair (after a 0.74 scale) as 1.25x too small — and the scores moved within noise (57-67). Scale from the MEASURED crest (kit ui gauge crest_width, from REFS.md), then stop re-scaling. Nine judge rounds on traced gauges plateau at 57-67 because the SOURCE is a 760x940 gallery sheet (cells 42x13 px, crests 176 px before the x4 upscale): the resolution of the reference is the ceiling of a trace, so get 1080p/4K stills per state before tracing a hero HUD.
