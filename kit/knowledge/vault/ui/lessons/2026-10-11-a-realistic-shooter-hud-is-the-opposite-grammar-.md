A realistic-shooter HUD is the opposite grammar of a stylized one: no plates, no fields, colour only as a signal (amber objective, blue friend, red damage), white condensed type with a soft dark edge. Gather refs and you find the genre's store shots have NO HUD; use those as the preview backdrop (identity art world.png) — a sunlit frame with an explosion is the hardest legibility case. Measure the one HUD still you have at 1:1 (mag numerals ~45 px tall, reserve ~23, name ~18, a 6 px bar) and size the type from it: the first build sized the numeral from a role guess and drew ~30 px, both judges' first gap.
