HUD icons come from REAL side views through kit ui ink --mode icon (outline + TEED interior lines + faint fill + a dark halo): the kit's own 3D builds (build/renders/*_side*) are the best source — original, measured, owner-approved. Make a second outline-only LOD (--lines none, thicker outline) for small uses: interior lines turn into a tangle at 50 px.
