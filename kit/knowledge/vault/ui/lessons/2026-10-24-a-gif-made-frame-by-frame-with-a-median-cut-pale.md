A GIF made frame by frame with a median-cut palette drops small bright pieces into the dark majority (Fulgurite's green glass shards came out grey-green, (103,130,119) vs the true (127,240,200)). Build ONE palette by octree from frames sampled across the whole moment and map every frame to it: the colours survive and the file shrinks (13 MB board -> 4 MB). Check a reel's key pixels by probe, not by eye.
