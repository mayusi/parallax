Composition gates must count plates: a user plate is marked kit after expansion (taste checks skip it), so the accent field read as 0% and the title passed. Count authored nodes, clip boxes to the screen, weigh a piece by its painted share (plates.py writes opaque into art.json): the same title then read 96% accent, which was the truth the judges had been saying.
