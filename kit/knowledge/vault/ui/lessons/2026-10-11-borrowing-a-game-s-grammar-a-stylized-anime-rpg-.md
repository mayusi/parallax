Borrowing a game's GRAMMAR (a stylized anime RPG) for another world (Heroine) works when the grammar becomes plates and rules, not pictures: torn/torn_black/halftone/rays plates + 'nothing level, nothing alone' (lean -6..-12, overlap, paper white under every cutout, one accent). Judges' first reads of the attempt: a square-clipped ray tile, hex-honeycomb halftone, level isolated islands, photoreal icons beside flat cutouts, the callout out-shouting the rank — each fixed by a plate or a rule, 53.5 -> 56 in one round; keep the hero piece the biggest chrome object.
