A realistic identity's written bans were STYLE (plates, chrome, glow, pills, gloss, the middle third) and never named what the owner rejected on FieldHud_v1 (1/10, 'AI slop, I asked realistic'): its minimap, kill feed, HP number and round count were plain panels and labels named Map, Feed, Hp, Rounds, Reserve. A component-type ban misses them, and a prose-only word list caught v1 only by the accident of an armour row named 'Plates'. Take banned_components words from the prose AND the owner's verdict on the rejected screen, match them by node type (base types too: plate) and by name, prove the rejected screen fails and the screen he approved after it still compiles (FieldHud v2 keeps a compass, so compass stays allowed), and show him the list before writing it: the identity is his.
