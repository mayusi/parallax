Roblox cannot take a custom TTF (2026): choose from Enum.Font. The preview draws the real faces (kit ui fonts fetches the Google Fonts twin, sha256-pinned); Roblox's own Garamond is the Guru face and Gotham / Builder Sans are proprietary, so fonts.json marks their twins approx.
