A cooler card animation is a story with an impact, not a tween (owner on the first WitchSmearCard reel: 'nice but could be cooler'): a smear FLIPBOOK (kit ui smear --pad N --no-crop: frames of different trail length on one aligned canvas, swapped by the reel's art prop) — stretched on the dash, held through a hit-stop with a white flash and a ray burst, SNAPPED short, settled; the world's own motif reacts (the Umbran clock whips round then crawls: Witch Time); the name punches last; a glint. Give a reel a 'strip' of key times: an even spread misses a 0.3 s impact. And a sliding parent must move the clip it imposes (preview: cdx/cdy).
