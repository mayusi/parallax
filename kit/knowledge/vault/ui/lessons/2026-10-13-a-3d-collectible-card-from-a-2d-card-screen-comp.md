A 3D collectible card from a 2D card screen: compose the screen's own pieces into one face PNG (rounded alpha), then kit ui spin --motion sway --depth 0.035 --bevel 0.008 --exposure -0.8; the sway's light sweep across the face sells it as a physical card (WitchCardReveal).
