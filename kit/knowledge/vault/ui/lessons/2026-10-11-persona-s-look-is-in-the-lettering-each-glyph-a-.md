Persona's look is in the LETTERING: each glyph a different face, mixed case, tilted, overlapping, one thick ink outline round the whole word and a thin paper rim (kit ui letter --style persona). One display face (Bangers) everywhere read as a comic and the owner called it ugly; the same layout with persona lettering, a two-tone torn band with a white fringe, zebra stamps and a rimmed black silhouette read as Persona at once.
