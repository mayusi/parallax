Across three blind-critiqued boards (SampleCity, SampleDunes, SamplePeak, 2026-10-24) the critics remember the same three things: a transformation of an object only this world has, the PLAYER'S OWN input shaping the UI (the finger drawing the grid: no two inventories alike), and a MARK THAT STAYS (seams that stay lit, a scar until healed, tally halves that slot together later). They forget stamps, name cards, number walls, corner pips, screen-eating strips - and any moment the sketch does not visibly show (the Smith's Wall quench: 'almost nothing happens').
