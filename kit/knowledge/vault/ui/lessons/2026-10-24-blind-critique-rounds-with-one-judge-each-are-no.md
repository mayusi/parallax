Blind critique rounds with ONE judge each are noisy: SampleCity's Paper Fuse went last -> 2nd -> last over three rounds with three different judges, while the Crosswalk Domain's move from last to FIRST (round 3) followed the one change every critic asked for (a mechanic only this place has: the crossing's WALK/STOP signals). Trust a movement only with 2+ judges per round (critique --round <label>); trust a change that answers a cliche every judge named.
