Motion rules (research 2026-10-14: Material 3 motion tokens m1.material.io/motion/duration-easing + api.flutter.dev Easing; animation-principles practice): ease OUT to enter, ease IN to leave, never linear for UI movement; entrances ~0.15-0.6 s (exits shorter); siblings staggered 2-5 frames; a small overshoot (~3-8%) on the hero beat; ~0.5 s still to READ. kit ui reel's critic checks these; shared hit punches and shakes are simultaneous on purpose.
