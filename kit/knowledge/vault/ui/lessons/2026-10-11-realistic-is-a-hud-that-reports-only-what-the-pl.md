'Realistic' is a HUD that reports only what the player cannot feel: the owner read a full arcade checklist (minimap, gun-icon kill feed, squad list, health number, plates, round count, crosshair, grey boxes) dressed in muted type as AI slop — and the references had said so (Insurgency / Ready or Not / Hell Let Loose frames show almost no HUD). The realistic grammar: magazines drawn as full as they FEEL + a mag-check phrase instead of a count, one thin compass, objective and teammates as small in-world markers, status only when it applies, no crosshair, no boxes. When the references show an ABSENCE, the absence is the design.
