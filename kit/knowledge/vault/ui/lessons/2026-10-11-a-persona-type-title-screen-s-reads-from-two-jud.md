A Persona-type title screen's reads, from two judge pairs: a flat axis-aligned region (a sky that stops at 72%) reads as a 'level panel behind everything' — run the texture full-bleed and give the menu its own leaning torn slab; a list of cutouts must be a PILE: three torn shapes (torn / torn_b / torn_c), leans that differ by 6-10 degrees, overlaps of 20+ px, the selected one bigger in the accent; a bevelled chrome logotype reads as WordArt — a custom mirror metal (roughness ~0.06, a hard horizon band, outline 0.1) reads as Heroine chrome; a FLUX silhouette needs a pose prompt (gun-kata, hair flaring, heels, no glasses) — the first take was clip art.
