Banning a source's NAMES is not enough: the SampleDunes 'cymatic call' had no Journey name on screen and still ranked last as 'Journey's shout ring' - its move (a call rippling in rings from your feet) was the source's own. List the source's signature MOVES in the brief (context.source_moves; check warns on a repeat) and keep at most the input, never the result. And sketch the real material: generic lines read as the generic thing (gold seams read as lightning) - use real forms (Chladni nodal figures, branching fulgurite tubes, cracks in ruin pieces).
