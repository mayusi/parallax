A spec's imageAlpha is the image's TRANSPARENCY (it compiles to ImageTransparency): 0 = solid, 0.9 = faint. StriveTitle's 'faint' paper at 0.12 came out solid and its 'strong' ink at 0.75 came out pale. A printed 3D logo: kit ui spin --gloss 0.05 --exposure -1.1 (the coat and a smooth face washed the red to pink mid-sway).
