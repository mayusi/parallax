A 3D render through the halftone ink route is a scribble: the suit is all ink and the mid-tones dissolve into dots (the owner's Heroine render). kit ui ink --mode cel flattens the picture's own colours to a few flat fills with grown TEED lines; --palette mono (black, two greys, white, a paper rim + an ink rim) is the P5 figure that sits ON the accent field — the identity palette (violet on violet) vanished there. Check the figure on the FIELD it will stand on, not on grey.
