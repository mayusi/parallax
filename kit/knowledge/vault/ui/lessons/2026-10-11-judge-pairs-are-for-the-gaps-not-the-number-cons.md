Judge pairs are for the GAPS, not the number: consecutive pairs read one build as 1.4x too big then 1.25x too small; the Umbra gauge sat at 45-49.5 while the owner called it 'genuinely impressed'. Fix what two judges agree on, measure sizes from REFS.md, never re-scale or re-judge on one judge's word, and let the owner's eye outrank the median.
