Ransom lettering is for one word (a header): a LIST in ransom letters was unreadable and the owner called the menu bad. A list is whole-word strips (stacked / per-item torn plates, alternating ink and paper, each leaning, stepping right) — the Persona command stair. Readability first, then grammar.
