Why kit UIs read as AI slop even with references, floors, focus and judges: every piece the compiler makes is a font glyph with a flat tint, a PIL rectangle or a generated blob — the medium is wrong. A real HUD is painted art. kit ui render (Blender Cycles, ~10 s a piece) renders chrome-bevelled logotypes and lit plates from a .render.json; the character-action rank went from flat yellow text to bevelled gold with a black edge in one render. Render the hero pieces; type only the numbers that change.
