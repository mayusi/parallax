Cartoon icon sheets come out usable on the first seed from FLUX.2 klein with the suffix 'a glossy cartoon mobile-game icon with a thick dark outline, bright saturated colours, a soft top highlight, Roblox pet simulator style, clean vector look' (18 of 18 kept, 20-28 s each); run the batch while the GPU is idle, cut out with BiRefNet, crop to the alpha box with 8 percent margin at 256 px.
