Both fresh judges of the first simulator pack scored every screen 51-66 for one reason: scale — labels, prices, buttons and slots far under the genre's measured sizes, panels with dead space. Three kit gates now catch it before a judge: the identity's minimums (text / button / icon floors measured from the references), the preview's empty-panel check (a grounded panel under half covered), and a ruler on every judge-packet tile (the sheet draws desktop at 50 percent — judges were reading sheet pixels as screen pixels).
