Text on a plate: the compiler takes the plate's colour as the contrast ground (bubble_good = that token, a card or frame = shape.fill), so dark text on a cream pill passes and white text on a green bubble is an error until it carries textOutline (the genre draws every white word with a dark outline anyway).
