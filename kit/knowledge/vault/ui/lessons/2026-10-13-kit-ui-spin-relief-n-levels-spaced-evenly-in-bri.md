kit ui spin --relief N: levels spaced evenly in BRIGHTNESS between the 2nd/98th percentiles, never by pixel-share quantiles (a big dark plate drags every quantile onto itself); relief steps get NO bevel (a bevel mitres the acute corners of a ragged brightness edge into long spikes) and slivers (4*pi*A/P^2 < 0.06) are dropped.
