A smear card (WitchSmearCard): the figure's OWN pixels dragged into a banded trail (kit ui smear: random band heights and lengths read as pixel-sorted speed, a plain blur reads as a mistake) that cools to the world's tint (Witch Time violet), echo frames as cooled silhouettes with a bright rim (under the trail they vanished), and --split so the trail is clipped inside the frame while the sharp figure breaks out over it. The card's language came from the source world (Heroine: black + gold Art-Nouveau, moon, Umbran clock as a separate turning piece), not from the last success (Persona).
