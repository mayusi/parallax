A rendered glass cell (gem item) clips to white under the logotype lights whatever its emission: the hot strip reflects in a coat 0.7 / roughness 0.12 glass. Measure the centre pixel (max channel under 245) and tune coat ~0.25, roughness ~0.3, exposure ~0.03, glow ~0.9 — the colour then comes from the emission, not the lights; the unlit cell at the same exposure reads as smoked glass (~25/255).
