A simulator / casual identity is the chunky family: shape.width 5 + gloss + shade + shadow draw bubble/pill/badge/card/banner per colour token (shape.buttons) and card_<tier> per rarity token; a panel or imagebutton with plate + shadow IS the bubble button (icon + outlined label inside), counters roll short (1.2K), bars take track + fillGloss, enters bounce, icons float, tags wobble. The preview backdrop follows the identity's world words (sunny/toy = sky + meadow) — a navy scrim token no longer makes the preview a night scene.
