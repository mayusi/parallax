Worn steel beats clean steel for a game plate: a noise bump + grime ramp in the roughness and base (render 'wear'), rims on the troughs, exposure ~0.3 and world 0.1 — a flat-lit slab reads pale silver however dark its base. Live cells read as glass when they are three slants: a dark edge, a bright inset, a 38 percent white shine. Pips at reference scale are ~56x66 px at 1080p with the unlit ones as dark metal (alpha 0.15, tinted line2), not faded ghosts.
