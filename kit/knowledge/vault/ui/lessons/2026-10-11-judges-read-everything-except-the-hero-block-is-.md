Judges read 'everything except the hero block is generic' when secondary blocks are bare text: give each block one structural cue the genre uses — kill-feed rows on faint strips with your kill edged, squad rows with per-mate health bars, armour as plate segments, spent rounds hollow. Two pairs moved 60/65 -> 62/67 on the hero numeral alone; the structure round is what lifts the rest.
