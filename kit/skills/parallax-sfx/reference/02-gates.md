## Gates
Upload gate (every file): mp3/ogg/wav/flac, < 20 MB, < 7 min, ≤ 48 kHz, 1-2 ch, not silent, no clipping, peak ≤ 0 dBFS.
Set gates (errors): every layer does its ROLE alone (a "sub" with no sub is a lie) · takes differ (no machine-gun
repetition) but stay in the family · one loudness per class (± 2 LU) · heavier is never quieter or brighter. · a
material matrix: one weapon on two surfaces differs by the ears (≥ surface_min, default 1.0). Loops: the SEAM gate (no
click; level / brightness across the seam within the gate or the loop's own movement; a bed ≥ 12 s). The anime / fighting
vocabulary: a hit's heaviest take drops at least its medium's SUB on the impact (median over takes, first 150 ms under
120 Hz); in move data (`kit move`) a swing / whoosh cue LEADS the first active frame by 50-300 ms.
Catalog: every event names a sound a recipe / set makes; known buses; numeric blend keys.
