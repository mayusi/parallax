# parallax-world

A map is gameplay first, and a top-down picture cannot judge it: prove it plays (`kit world check`), then look at it
from 4.5 studs up (`kit world look`). Blockout fast; the art pass only after the owner OKs the layout.
Plan + ideas: `board/plans/archive/2026-10-12-world-pipeline-v2.md`.

## The workflow (blockout ~10 min, a playable lit arena ~30 min)
0. A game? It lives in `games/<Name>/` (`kit game new` / `kit game use`) — its worlds go to its `assets/worlds/`.
1. `kit new world <Pascal> [--from BattlegroundsArena]` → `assets/worlds/<Name>.json` + `<Name>.brief.md`: ASK THE
   OWNER its questions first (the source and its landmarks, routes, scale, the parts plan).
2. Terrain (optional): `kit world terrain <Name> [--size 512 --seed 3 --relief 60 --erosion 60000 --from <image>
   --flat x,z,r]` → `assets/worlds/terrain/<Name>.hm.json` + a shaded relief + `<Name>.paint.png` — LOOK at both, then
   a `heightmap` terrain op (below). How land is made (in order): gradient noise (+ `--warp` studs) or a `--design`;
   RIVERS `--stream 80 --streamK 0.015` (stream-power incision at two scales: major valleys hundreds of studs apart,
   then gullies — ranges with branching valleys and sharp ridges instead of noise cones); `--thermal 40` (slopes
   collapse to their talus angle, also after the rivers); droplet `--erosion 300000` (brush erosion: gullies, fans,
   and the flow + deposit maps); then the PAINTER writes a role per cell from what the land did — channels mud, wet
   hollows lush, fans dirt, cliffs rock / dark cliff, scree aprons under them, a noisy snowline, a sandy shore
   (`--snowline`, `--shore`, or the design's `"paint": {"snowline", "shore"}`). Designed shapes stay as designed:
   basin / plateau (with its edge) / ramp (with its shoulders) / hill cores are re-imposed after erosion (`"keep":
   false` lets the water have one); `"anchor": [x, z, height]` pins one spot (the lake bed) so a re-run never floats
   the map under the water and the buildings; `"terraces": {"step": 14, "sharp": 0.35}` = rock strata on rim / ring.
   REAL-LIFE look = real data: scanned surfaces (ambientCG 1K, `kit materials --fetch <ids> --res 1K`; the viewer
   draws them as PBR with height-blended borders) and SCANNED MODELS (Poly Haven CC0, vetted by hand, VETLOG) listed
   in `assets/worlds/imports/<set>.json` → `kit world import [<set>]` (`"split": true` = a scanned set becomes single
   rocks, `"maxTris": 20000` = fits a MeshPart, textures as 1024 PNGs) → use the names in scatter / forest `"meshes"`
   and in `rocks` (`"meshes"` = scanned crags, `"spireMeshes"` = a tall scanned rock face per spire). Stylised packs
   (Quaternius) are fine for stylised worlds, never for a realistic one.
3. Blockout with the generators; `objectives` (where fights / goals are) and `checks` in the blueprint.
4. `kit world check <Name>` → `build/worlds/checks/<Name>.png` (reachability, exposure, camera, open room, sightlines).
   Errors: spawns that cannot meet, unreachable objectives, TRAPS (get in, never back). LOOK lint (warnings):
   parts that float or lie buried, trees through buildings, rocks / trunks on a road or trail. (Scatter, forest,
   rocks and cover keep off every road laid EARLIER in the blueprint by themselves: put roads first.) Warnings: unreachable
   platforms (named with bounds + height), unfair spawns, cramped camera, little knockback room.
5. `kit world look <Name>` → `build/worlds/look/<Name>.png`: from every spawn + an establishing shot, lit by the
   world's lighting, beside `assets/worlds/refs/<Name>/` references.
6. `kit world` → `src/world` (Workspace.KitWorld) + the build; walk it in Studio (`character_navigation`); the owner plays.
7. `kit world web <Name>` → `build/worlds/web/<Name>.html`: the whole world as ONE web page (three.js + the kit's
   own shaders: terrain layers, procedural materials, water, sky, height fog, sun shadows, HDR bloom, light shafts,
   grass, particles; orbit / fly / walk at R6 eye height; a parchment map; the graces and objectives as places).
   Show it to the owner — publish `<Name>.artifact.html` as an Artifact. It is the ART DIRECTION, not Studio's pixels.
   Examples (each its own game / place): `games/SampleRegion/` (an Elden Ring style region) and `games/SamplePeak/`
   (a northern hold in winter). Fast look: `kit world shots <Name>` (headless viewer, ~1 min) -> a contact sheet.

The check works on voxels (1 stud; 2 studs on maps over ~400k sq studs): walls thinner than a voxel still block,
corner seams are sealed and jumps need a body-wide (2-stud) path, but a gap narrower than the body that still holds a
whole voxel (e.g. a 0.8-stud slit between offset walls) can read as passable: close gaps you mean to close.
Floors: `center.y` is the TOP surface. R6 numbers the check uses: 5 studs tall, 2 wide, walks 16 st/s, jumps 7.2
(a gap of ~8.7 studs on the flat), steps up 1 stud; half cover 3, full 7; a third-person camera needs room behind.

## Terrain + lighting
```json
"lighting": "golden",   // golden | noon | overcast | storm | night | scifi_dusk, or { "preset": "storm", "clockTime": 15 }
"terrain": [
  { "heightmap": { "center": [0, 0, 0], "size": [512, 512], "source": "assets/worlds/terrain/<Name>.hm.json",
                   "material": "Grass", "rock": "Rock", "rockSlope": 38, "flat": [{ "center": [0, 0], "radius": 60 }] } },
  { "ground": { "center": [0, 0, 0], "size": [600, 600], "material": "Grass", "under": "Rock",
                "noise": { "amplitude": 9, "scale": 110, "seed": 11 }, "flat": [{ "center": [0, 60], "radius": 26, "height": 0 }] } },
  { "ball": { "center": [130, 2, -170], "radius": 26, "material": "Rock" } },
  { "water": { "center": [110, -6, 40], "size": [60, 8, 40] } } ]
```
`"lighting": "erdtree"` = golden hour under a golden haze; `"jungle"` = a humid green-gold morning; `"neon_night"` = a neon city; `"frost"` = a northern winter (low sun, blue skylight);
`"desert"` = a copper end-of-day (Journey). `"ambient": "snow" | "sand" | "ash" | "leaves" | "embers"` = weather around the
player (a client emitter over the camera; the viewer shows it too). `"decoration": true` = Roblox's animated grass on Grass terrain.
PAINTED terrain: a heightmap made by `kit world terrain` carries its roles; the op uses them when it has no `layers` or
says `"paint": true` (a role map remaps: `"paint": {"meadow": "Grass", "lush": "LeafyGrass", "dirt": "Ground", "mud":
"Mud", "rock": "Rock", "cliff": "Slate", "snow": "Snow", "sand": "Sand", "scree": "Ground"}` — those are the defaults;
`"paint": false` = off). Roblox fills each 4-stud column with its role's material (borders dithered); the viewer paints
the same map. Without paint, heightmap `layers` paint by height and slope (later wins):
`[{ "material": "Sand", "minHeight": 12, "maxHeight": 16, "maxSlope": 18 }, { "material": "Ground", "minHeight": 120 }]`.
`"terrainColors": { "Grass": [112, 122, 58], ... }` and `"water": { "color", "transparency", "waveSize", "waveSpeed",
"reflectance" }` at the blueprint's top. Designed land: `kit world terrain <Name> --design <file>` (rim / plateau / basin /
hill / ridge / ramp / dunes (crescents with a steep lee face: run it with --erosion 0) / ring shapes + a domain warp, then erosion) — example `games/SampleRegion/assets/worlds/terrain/SampleRegion.design.json`; far mountains around a map: a second, coarse heightmap with `"backdrop": true` and a `ring` shape (`SampleRegionFar.design.json`).
Heightmap samples ride inside the world model (`KitTerrain<i>_<k>` ModuleScripts, chunked under Roblox's ~200k-character Source cap, joined then destroyed at run time) — the game, the check and the look all
read the same heights. Applied at runtime by the kit's Environment (Rojo cannot carry terrain or lighting effects).

## Gates (kit world)
Errors: unknown palette/material, size outside 0–2048, below the kill plane, spawn buried, > 10k parts, a building
too shallow for its stairs, a heightmap source that is not one. Warnings: z-fighting, > 3k parts (mobile), no spawn,
scatter / cover that could not fit. `kit verify` fails if `src/world` drifts from the blueprints.
