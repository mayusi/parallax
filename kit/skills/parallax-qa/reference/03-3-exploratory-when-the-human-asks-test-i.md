## 3. Exploratory (when the human asks "test it")
Play the feature like a hostile player: spam the action, send it from far away, act while dead / mid-respawn,
two players at once, leave mid-action. Turn every bug found into a playtest check or a spec case, then fix.
Report: what you tried, what broke, what is now covered — on the task (`kit task note`) and in the handoff.
