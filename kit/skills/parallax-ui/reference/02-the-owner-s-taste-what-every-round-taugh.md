## The owner's taste — what every round taught (read before the first piece)
1. **Real sources beat generated ones; the source is the ceiling.** A 3D render through any filter was a splat;
   official 2D key art through `kit ui ink --mode cel` read at once. A prompt-made stand-in for something the owner can
   picture is "AI slop".
2. **Type is the grammar.** Persona = a face per glyph, mixed case, tilted, overlapping, one word outline + paper rim;
   one display face everywhere read as a comic. Realistic = small condensed type with a soft dark edge.
3. **Shapes carry the style:** scissor cuts (not per-pixel tears), three colours for Persona; chrome only in the
   metal worlds (character-action / Heroine gauges). Children follow a warped holder (cells along a bowed bar).
4. **Realistic means absence:** report only what the player cannot feel. The arcade checklist (minimap, gun-icon feed,
   HP number, round count, crosshair, grey boxes) in muted paint was slop; when the references show an absence, the
   absence is the design.
5. **Measure, never guess:** sizes from 1:1 stills by script; judges see the 50 % sheet — check their size and colour
   claims against the model and the pixels (the "red health" was white over sand).
6. **The owner's eye outranks the median.** Judge pairs plateau within ±5; after two rounds without movement, stop and
   show the owner. Show every round; anything animated as a reel GIF; present the HUD alone, never over game footage.
