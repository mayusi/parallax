# parallax-ui — identity first

A generated UI looks generated when nobody decided the look: default faces, one radius everywhere, an indigo
accent, a centred card with three tiles, lift-on-hover. The kit makes you decide first, then builds from that
decision with real art and real motion. **The Souls pack is the proof:** `lune run kit examples screen`.

## The library rule — a MUST (owner, 2026-10-12)
Everything the UI pipeline makes is saved, packaged by part and named properly, in `library/ui/`:
`<identity>/screens/<Screen>/` (README with the owner's verdict and a parts table, manifest, the spec, every picture
as `parts/<Node.Path>.png`, previews + reel), `<identity>/pieces/` (every piece the identity ever made, with
PIECES.md and its source), owner-rated versions kept under `versions/` when a screen changes; nothing is deleted.
`kit ui` and every piece command (ink, letter, render, trace, gauge, reel) save automatically; **`kit verify` fails**
when anything made is not saved or is stale, or `lune run kit ui vault` fixes it. Every piece needs a source (a sidecar
or a PROVENANCE.md row): the vault names the ones that do not. Reuse BEFORE making: `kit ui vault find "<words>"`,
`kit ui find "<words>"`, `kit ui vault get <Screen> --as <New>`; the index: `library/ui/INDEX.md`.

## The owner's taste — what every round taught (read before the first piece)
1. **Real sources beat generated ones; the source is the ceiling.** A 3D render through any filter was a splat;
   official 2D key art through `kit ui ink --mode cel` read at once. A prompt-made stand-in for something the owner can
   picture is "AI slop".
2. **Type is the grammar.** Persona = a face per glyph, mixed case, tilted, overlapping, one word outline + paper rim;
   one display face everywhere read as a comic. Realistic = small condensed type with a soft dark edge.
3. **Shapes carry the style:** scissor cuts (not per-pixel tears), three colours for Persona; chrome only in the
   metal worlds (character-action / Heroine gauges). Children follow a warped holder (cells along a bowed bar).
4. **Realistic means absence:** report only what the player cannot feel. The arcade checklist (minimap, gun-icon feed,
   HP number, round count, crosshair, grey boxes) in muted paint was slop; when the references show an absence, the
   absence is the design.
5. **Measure, never guess:** sizes from 1:1 stills by script; judges see the 50 % sheet — check their size and colour
   claims against the model and the pixels (the "red health" was white over sand).
6. **The owner's eye outranks the median.** Judge pairs plateau within ±5; after two rounds without movement, stop and
   show the owner. Show every round; anything animated as a reel GIF; present the HUD alone, never over game footage.

## The hero route (proven 2026-10-11 on the character-action gauges and the Umbra gauge — follow it in this order)

A hero HUD is **art assembled by data**, never typed. Every round that skipped a step below was called "AI slop"
by the owner or scored under 50 by two fresh judges; every round that followed it moved the picture. `lune run kit
ui plan <Screen>` is this list as a stage board — run it first and after every stage.

1. **References before anything.** Two or more real stills of the HUD you are matching or drawing in the spirit
   of, into `assets/ui/refs/<identity>/`, measured at 1:1 into `REFS.md` (source URL, frame size, every piece's
   pixel box and sampled colour; scale them to 1080p). A gallery sheet is NOT a reference: its zoom is unknown and
   a trace cannot exceed its resolution (the five character-action gauges plateaued at 60-67 on a 760x940 sheet). Crop with the
   picture's own pixels, never a pasted contact sheet (one was 180 px off).
2. **Identity from the measurements.** `assets/ui/identities/<name>.json`: tokens sampled from the stills (the
   compiler flags the generic indigo accent), fonts, shape language, bans (write the other identities' tells in),
   `minimums`, and `references` with `image` + `measured` (they land in the judge packet as refs.png).
3. **Pieces by route — rendered or traced, by name:**
   - **Replica** → `kit ui trace <identity>/<piece> --from <still> --box x,y,w,h [--cutout] [--gauge] [--lift]`:
     the reference's own pixels, upscaled, matted, cells blanked and anchored. Boxes generous; `--cutout` on
     plates too; bad trace = retrace.
   - `imageAlpha` on images and plates is TRANSPARENCY (0.85 = faint, 0.5 = a wash) — a backdrop at 0.12 is nearly opaque.
   - **A character, a logo, a hero motif that EXISTS as a picture** (the owner's key art, an official render, a
     photo) → `kit ui ink <identity>/<piece> --from <picture> [--mode ink|cel] [--palette own|identity|mono]
     [--colors 6] [--line-px 3] [--rim 12] [--rim-ink 4] [--mid halftone|accent] [--dark .36] [--light .68]`: cut out, split into ink / mid / paper, the mid-tone as a halftone screen (or the
     accent flat), TEED lines, a paper rim — Persona's character art from a real picture, with a `.ink.json` sidecar.
     The owner: "grab a proper picture ... and make it like the Persona one" — a prompt-made stand-in for a
     character the owner can picture is the AI-slop tell; `kit ui plan` warns on it (the sources stage).
   - **Original** → `kit ui render <identity>/<piece>` (Blender): `text` items for logotypes (chrome_gold /
     silver / red / black_steel or a custom metal, horizon band, outline, glow), `ring` for energy strokes,
     `gauge` for the plate (troughs + caps; `cap`/`cap_left: "horn"`, `bow`, `taper`, `frame_pad`, per-row
     `cell`; custom `frame` metal; `wear_scale` ~50), `gem` for ONE glass cell per state (lit with a halo, unlit
     smoked). Painted motifs (a butterfly, a crest) → `kit vision concept ... --sheet single` → `kit vision
     cutout` → square-crop; prompt "flat, front-facing, clean game-UI illustration, no text".
   - The render reads its picture back and names the knob when it is off (a gem clipping to white, a pale plate,
     a clipped logotype) — act on `next_action`. A hairline guard clears stray glyph lines.
   - **Never** a font glyph with a flat tint or a PIL rectangle as a hero piece. The compiler warns on a hero
     screen with no art at all.
4. **Type is a piece too — never a font with a tint.** Display words (a logo, menu items, a shout) are written as
   text with `"lettering": true` (or overrides `{size, seed, scale, caps, shadow_color, ...}`); the `kit ui` words
   step bakes them into `art:<identity>/w_<slug>` with the identity's `"lettering"` defaults (`style: persona` = a
   face per glyph, mixed case, tilt, overlap, word outline + paper rim — the round the owner called "genuinely
   amazing"; `flat` = one face, ink outline, hard drop). A word re-bakes only when its recipe changes; live numbers
   stay real text. Baked art keeps its desktop pixels, so give each GROUP one `"at": {"phone": {"scale": 0.55},
   "tablet": {"scale": 0.72}}` — it shrinks the node and everything inside it — then LOOK at phone and tablet
   (the first lettered build collided there on both screens).
5. **Assemble from anchors.** `assets/ui/<Screen>.gauge.json` → `kit ui gauge <Screen>`: cells laid over the
   plate's wells from `<plate>.anchors.json`; `crest_width` (the measured crest in 1080p px) sets the scale, never
   the picture's zoom; `cells: {art_lit, art_unlit}` for rendered gems; `pips: {art_lit, art_unlit, count, lit}`
   for live pips; `nudge` to move a traced piece off a bad crop. **Children follow a warped parent:** a bowed
   holder's wells carry their tangent (`rot`) and every cell is placed by its centre and rotated to it. Size
   from the data: plate_width so a cell is ~12 px tall at 1080p for a thin bar, the character-action 30x14 for a chunky one.
6. **Look at every milestone, with numbers.** Compose a contact sheet (the pieces + the HUD corner of the preview
   at 1:1 or 4x) and LOOK; sample pixels by script (a plate's mean under ~90/255 reads as steel; a lit cell's
   max channel under 245). `kit ui look <Screen> "<what you saw>"` after each look — the packet refuses an
   unlooked build. Views cost (10/hour across judges): batch, crop tight, measure by script when a number will do.
7. **Judge by two, read the gaps, not the number.** `kit ui judge <Screen>` → two FRESH judges (Opus, medium;
   sheet.png and the real refs, nothing else) → `--record` both → the median. Two judges differ by ~5-10 and
   consecutive pairs contradicted each other on size (1.4x too big, then 1.25x too small): measure, never
   re-scale on a judge's word. The owner's eye outranks the median (the Umbra gauge: owner "genuinely
   impressed" at a 49.5 median). Fix located gaps in the order the judges agree on; one pair per round.
8. **Ship with the data.** `kit upload`; `kit promote screen <Screen>` carries the identity, the art AND its
   render / trace / anchors specs, PROVENANCE.md, the gauge spec and REFS.md (an example must rebuild); a
   lesson for anything a future model would have to rediscover; the handoff names the next gap.

Time: an original gauge from two stills to a judged screen took ~70 min; each owner correction ~25 min.
The owner's budgets (10 min prop, 30 complex, 1 h hero) are the pace — look at hour 1, never grind blind.

### A realistic HUD (FieldHud, 2026-10-12) — the quiet grammar, by the same route
- **References:** the genre's store shots show NO HUD (realistic shooters keep it minimal): keep one as the identity's
  `art/<identity>/world.png` (the preview backdrop — a sunlit frame is the hardest legibility case) and find a real
  HUD still on a wiki (`kit ui refs <id> "<game>" "HUD" --wiki <sub>`); measure it at 1:1 by script (numeral height,
  margins, bar height, minimap box and fill luma) and size the roles from those pixels.
- **Identity:** no plates, square corners, colour only as a signal (amber objective, blue friend, red damage),
  condensed sans (RobotoCondensed / Oswald / RobotoMono), every text with `"textOutline": "shadow"`; `rules` with
  `accent_share` near zero; bans that name the stylized identities' tells.
- **Icons:** `kit ui ink <id>/<piece> --from <side render> --mode icon` (outline + TEED interior lines + faint fill +
  a dark `--halo`), from the kit's own 3D builds (`build/renders/*side*`); an outline-only LOD (`--lines none
  --outline-px 7`) for 50 px uses. Drawn-by-script pieces (minimap, compass strip, view cone) live in `_scratch/ui_<id>/`.
- **Structure lifts the secondary blocks** (the judges' consistent read: "everything but the ammo is generic"):
  feed rows on faint strips with your kill edged, squad rows with per-mate bars, armour plates, hollow spent rounds,
  a faint dark backing under any small text over a warm scene (three judges read white-on-sand as red).
- A picture that IS content (a minimap) carries `"attributes": {"Kit_Content": true}` so the empty-panel check counts it.

### Round 3 — "the font and the visuals look ugly, not Persona" (owner, 2026-10-11): the LETTERING is the grammar
- **`kit ui letter <identity>/<piece> --style persona --text "WORD"`**: every glyph its own face from a pool (Cinzel
  black, Oswald bold, Luckiest Guy, EB Garamond, Montserrat black + italic, Roboto Condensed black, Michroma), mixed
  case (the first letter a big capital), each glyph resized, tilted (`--wobble 7`), lifted off the baseline and
  tucked under its neighbour (`--overlap 0.06`); spacing by each glyph's INK; then ONE thick ink outline around the
  whole word (`--outline 0.07`) and a thin paper rim outside it (`--rim-px 4`). A logo: `--caps --rim-px 0 --shadow
  0.08 --shadow-color <accent>` (an ink drop vanishes on black). One face (Bangers) for everything read as a comic,
  not Persona. Words are baked as art (buttons with `text: ""` and the word as an image child); live numbers stay text.
- **Plates of the grammar**: `tear_band` (a long torn strip in two tones — the tint gives the accent and a darker
  accent) over `tear_fringe` (its white paper fringe, `color: paper`), `zebra` (hand-cut diagonal stripes, a stamp),
  `halftone` dots, `cut_*` splashes behind the selected item.
- **The figure is a black silhouette with sparse white and a paper rim** (`kit ui ink --mode cel --palette mono
  --dark 0.5 --light 0.72 --rim 9`), always on a loud ground (the band, an accent cut) — never on plain black.

### The owner's verdict on the first Persona-type title ("horrible") and what fixed it (2026-10-11)
1. **The figure's SOURCE decides everything.** A 3D render through any filter (halftone ink, cel-mono, two-tone,
   cleaned) was a splat every time. Official 2D key art (`kit ui refs <identity> "<game>" "<words>" --wiki <sub>`
   → keep the drawn full-body piece → `kit ui ink <identity>/<piece> --from <art> --mode cel --palette mono
   --clean 2`) read as an illustration on the first try. Pick a DRAWN picture; the render is for proportions only.
2. **Scissor cuts, not tears.** Persona's shapes are a few long straight runs with sharp corners: the `cut` family
   (`cut`, `cut_b`, `cut_c`, `cut_black`, `cut_field`, `cut_strip`). `torn` is pixel wobble — it reads as noise at
   field scale. The accent field = `cut_field` over an ink `cut_field` offset a few px (the double cut).
3. **Three colours.** Ink, paper, the one accent. The chrome logotype, the gold script and the grey crest were
   five materials on a three-colour grammar; the logo is `kit ui letter <identity>/<piece> --text ... --font
   bangers --shadow 0.09 --lean -5` (flat paper letters, ink outline, a hard drop). `--cards --wobble 6` = ransom.
4. **Layout from the measured still** (`kit ui measure`): the field high on the left falling to the bottom by 75 %,
   the logo top-left over the field's edge, the figure huge bottom-centre-right, the menu staircase ON the field
   lower-left (paper strips with ink type, the selected one inverted) — not floating on black (ink strips vanish).
5. **`plateColor`** on a label / button tints its plate and is its contrast ground; a list is whole-word strips
   (`cut_strip`), never ransom letters.

### What the kit does for you now (ideas 5-10 of `board/plans/archive/ui-pipeline-v4-ideas.md`, shipped 2026-10-11)
- **Composition gates** (`identity.rules`: `lean_min`, `lean_share`, `overlap`, `accent_share: [lo, hi]`, `hero_biggest`;
  `kit ui identity --from` writes them from the stills): the compiler measures the screen at 1920x1080 and warns
  `composition: ...` when the top blocks sit level, touch nothing, when the accent paints too little or too much
  (every tinted plate counted by its painted share, `opaque` in art.json), or when the biggest PICTURE (a file
  piece, painted area) is not the hero. A title names `"hero": "Title.Witch"` when the focus (the logo) is not
  the hero. The Umbra title went 96% accent / 1 of 4 leaning to 45% / 5 of 9 on these numbers alone.
- **Type treatments**: `"ransom": true` (every glyph its own cutout; the runtime rebuilds a changed word from the
  recipe, `Kit_RansomSeed` + `Kit_Inverted`), `"stacked": true` (a phrase as a stair of whole-WORD strips,
  alternating ink / paper, each leaning — Persona's command list; a word list is readable where ransom letters
  were not), `tracking`, chrome logotypes by `kit ui render`.
- **A picture through `kit ui ink --mode cel`**: the picture's own colours flattened to a few (`--colors 6`),
  TEED lines grown to `--line-px 3`, a paper rim + `--rim-ink 4`; `--palette mono` (black, two greys, white) is
  the P5 figure that sits ON the accent field — `identity` (ink / accent / paper) vanished on the field, and the
  halftone `ink` mode made a 3D render into a scribble. From the owner's render: cel-mono read as a figure, ink
  did not.
- **Owner anchors**: `kit ui judge <Screen> --owner 85 "the owner's words"` keeps the sheet in
  `assets/ui/anchors/`; every later packet shows them (anchors.png) and asks the judge to place the new screen
  between them; `--record` lists the gaps two judges AGREE on first.
- **Sources stage** in `kit ui plan`: every piece's provenance (render / trace / ink sidecar or a PROVENANCE row);
  a prompt-made hero piece is flagged.
- **`kit ui booth <Screen>`**: the screen in the REAL engine — through the shared capture bridge (no network): the
  run's Studio script shows the scratch place's own copy of the screen in StarterGui, plays its arrival through
  `src/kit/Ui.luau`, and `kit/vfx/capture.py studio` reads Studio's window at every moment
  (`--moments 0,0.15,0.4,1,2`) → `build/ui/booth/<Screen>_engine.png`. Needs the scratch place open and uploaded art.
- **`kit ui find "<words>"`**: the piece bank — proven pieces across identities and the starter library, with
  how each was made; look before any generator is asked.

## Recipe (one pass)
0. `lune run kit lessons ui` · `lune run kit examples screen` (RpgHud, RpgTitle, RpgBonfire, RpgLevelUp,
   RpgDeath = a dark-fantasy RPG-type pack; SimHud, SimShop, SimPets, SimEgg, SimRebirth, SimDaily, SimCodes,
   SimSettings = a complete pet/clicker simulator pack in the chunky `sim` identity; hud_example / main_menu =
   the skin-only starters).
1. **Identity.** `assets/ui/identities/<name>.json` — copy `souls.json` and change EVERYTHING to the game's world:
   `about` (one line), `world`, `tokens` (measure them from references, never from habit), `fonts` (an Enum.Font
   trio: Garamond for a souls-like, Oswald for military, Michroma for sci-fi…), `radius`, `roles` (the type
   scale), `shape` (line, line2, fill, width, corner flourish|bracket|chamfer|round|none, glow, grain),
   `motion` (the default enter + stagger), `bans`, `references` (what each real screen does, measured) and
   `minimums` (`{"text": 16, "button": 56, "icon": 40}` — the scale floors measured from those references; the
   compiler warns under them: every undersized pack's first judge verdict was "everything is tiny").
   `lune run kit ui identities` lists them. Ask the owner for the references; a hero screen without an identity
   is an ERROR.
2. **Art.** `lune run kit ui` draws the identity's plates in its shape language into `build/ui/art/<name>/`
   (frame, frame_line, slot, cap, track, divider, band, vignette, grain, highlight, keycap, glow, ornament,
   corner, arrow, ring, ribbon) plus the **chunky family** (bubble, pill, badge, card, banner, track_chunky,
   gloss, burst, close, plus) — and a finished colour variant of bubble / pill / badge / banner for every token
   in `shape.buttons` (`bubble_good`, `pill_danger`...) and a `card_<tier>` for every `rarity_<tier>` token.
   The shape's `width` (outline), `gloss`, `shade`, `shadow` (drop offset) and `card` colour decide the look:
   souls = thin gold flourish, sim = 5 px navy outline + white gloss. LOOK at them. Generated pieces (icons, emblems, logos): `lune run kit vision
   concept "<a <game> style item icon: ...>" --sheet single -o x.png` → `kit vision cutout x.png --rgba -o x_cut.png`
   → crop + square → `assets/ui/art/<identity>/<name>.png` (the next `kit ui` takes them in as `art:<identity>/<name>`).
   **Hero pieces are RENDERED, not typed:** a chrome logotype, a bevelled rank letter, a lit metal plate come from
   `lune run kit ui render <identity>/<name>` — Blender (Cycles, GPU) renders `assets/ui/art/<identity>/<name>.render.json`
   (text items in any Enum.Font from the font cache, scale / position / lean, `material` chrome_gold | chrome_silver |
   chrome_red | black_steel or {base, roughness, metallic}, `depth`, `bevel`, `outline`, `glow`, `horizon` (the
   reflected-horizon gradient that makes a flat face read as chrome — on by default), and `"ring": true` items =
   broken emissive energy strokes) to a transparent PNG in ~10 s; render-only faces (Pirata One blackletter,
   Kaushan Script brush italic) live in fonts.json beside the Enum.Font twins; the screen shows it as
   `art:<identity>/<name>` (an image node can be the `focus`). A font glyph with a flat tint is the AI-slop tell;
   the real game's HUD is painted art, so make art. Reference pictures: `assets/ui/refs/<identity>/` + REFS.md with
   the measurements and the crop origins (a contact sheet pasted at an offset once mis-measured by 180 px).
   **Ornate gauge HUDs** (character-action-style style gauges, segmented bars in a metal frame with an emblem): a `"gauge": true`
   render item makes the plate (leaning troughs of inset wells, bladed caps) and writes `<piece>.anchors.json` — every
   well's rectangle in pixels; `lune run kit ui gauge <Screen>` turns `assets/ui/<Screen>.gauge.json` (plate, fills
   per row, emblem art, pip art + lit count, a name) into the screen spec with live cells laid exactly over the
   wells (slant plates tinted with the fill token: scripts light `Hud.Plate.Row0.C3`). Emblems and pips come from
   `kit vision concept` cutouts. Edit the .gauge.json, never the generated screen.
   **A 1:1 replica is TRACED, not generated:** `lune run kit ui trace <identity>/<piece> --from <picture> --box x,y,w,h
   [--x 4] [--cutout] [--gauge] [--repaint "<instruction>"]` crops the reference at 1:1, upscales it (Real-ESRGAN x4),
   cuts it out (BiRefNet), and for `--gauge` finds the green cells, blanks them to empty wells and writes their
   anchors; `--repaint` runs Qwen-Image-Edit on the crop for a cleaner take. Each piece carries its box
   (`<piece>.trace.json`), so a `.gauge.json` with `"from_trace": true` lays the emblem, the pip strip
   (`"pips": {"strip": ...}`) and a traced name exactly where the picture has them. The reference's pixels are
   the art; text prompts give look-alikes (the judges called every prompted emblem "a different design").
   **The measured HUD sets the scale, never the picture:** a gallery sheet is zoomed, so give the gauge
   `"crest_width": 220` (the real crest's width in 1080p px, from REFS.md) and the plate is sized from the traced
   boxes to match; `"nudge": [dx, dy]` on the emblem or the name moves a piece off its traced place (a crop that
   put the name onto the crest). Two judge rounds disagreed by 1.4x on the same build's size — measure, don't ask.
   **An ORIGINAL gauge in a game's spirit (no picture to trace):** identity from two measured stills → the crest and
   the pips as FLUX concepts (`kit vision concept ... --sheet single`, cut out, squared) → the plate as a `gauge`
   render item with `"cap": "horn"` (a crescent curl) or `"spike"`, a custom `frame: {base, roughness, metallic}`
   (a mirror steel reflects the lights and reads pale: base ~0.1, roughness 0.4, exposure ~0.12 reads as steel),
   `wear_scale` ~50 for a brushed grain (18 is blotchy grime); a curling identity wants the SWEEP options, not the
   character-action trough: `"bow": 0.1` (the row rises at its middle), `"taper": 0.3` (thinner to the right), `"cap_left": "horn"`
   (both ends curl), `"frame_pad": 0.03` + a row's own `"cell": [w, h]` (thin bars, a thinner second row); size the
   screen from the anchors (plate_width so a cell is ~12 px tall at 1080p); the CELLS are rendered too — a `gem`
   item per state (`{"gem": true, "size": [2.4, 0.66], "color": [r, g, b], "lit": true, "glow": 0.9, "halo": 0.4}`,
   exposure ~0.03: a glass slab in a dark lip with a halo; unlit = smoked dark glass) and the gauge spec's
   `"cells": {"art_lit": "cell_lit", "art_unlit": "cell_unlit"}` stretches each picture until its slab covers the
   well (a tinted slant plate reads as a flat slab; a rendered gem reads as lit energy). A bowed holder's cells
   FOLLOW the bow: every well's anchor carries the bar's tangent (`rot`) and `kit ui gauge` places each cell by its
   centre and rotates it to it — level cells inside a curved bar are the wrong picture → the name as a text render → the gauge spec with
   `"pips": {"art_lit", "art_unlit", "count", "lit"}` (an unlit pip as its own painting, not a ghost) → look, judge.
   The Umbra gauge (WitchGauge, identity `witch`) is the example: ~45 min from stills to a judged screen.
   **A whole HUD in a borrowed GRAMMAR** (Arcade5Hud: a stylized anime RPG's cutouts in Heroine's world): the grammar is
   plates — `torn` / `torn_black` (a paper cutout over its ink layer, offset 8-14 px, both leaning -6..-12),
   `halftone` (tint with the accent, imageAlpha 0.35-0.5), `rays` (behind the one hero piece) — and the rule
   "nothing level, nothing alone": every block leans and overlaps a neighbour, paper white under every cutout,
   one accent does all the shouting. Reuse proven pieces across identities by copying the art folder entries
   (the Umbra gauge + its anchors went into arcade5 untouched) and graft a `kit ui gauge` subtree into a
   hand-authored screen. A role's font may be a face name (`"script": {"font": "Fondamento"}`). A title /
   menu in that grammar (Arcade5Title): the logotype as a chrome render on a torn paper + ink pile, the menu as a
   leaning stack of torn `button`s (the selected one in the accent with a `rays` marker), a seamless `dots` tile
   (`"fit": "tile", "tile": 120`) for a halftone sky (the gradient `halftone` stretched over a screen gives
   fist-sized dots), a FLUX black silhouette with a white rim as the character cutout. The owner rejected the
   first menu ("looks bad") — the fix was MORE references: Persona's actual title still (refs/arcade5/REFS.md)
   measures as a torn accent FIELD on 35 % of the frame, lower-left, its edge falling to the right (the `torn_field`
   plate, drawn large so its tears stay paper-scale), the logo over the field's upper edge, the character huge over
   the field, and the menu on the BLACK side as a diagonal staircase of torn black ribbons (same lean, stepping
   down-right), the selected one on an accent splash much bigger than its ribbon. Structure first, then the pile.
   Every text render passes a hairline guard (stray 1-2 px lines from a glyph's open contour are cleared).
   Live pips: trace one lit and one unlit pip (`t_pip_lit_x`, `t_pip_unlit_x`) and give the gauge
   `"pips": {"strip": "<traced strip>", "art_lit": ..., "art_unlit": ..., "count": 10, "lit": 3}` — `count` pips
   spread along the strip's box, the lit ones breathing. The generated screen uses `insets: core` (the cluster
   sits under Roblox's buttons) unless the .gauge.json says otherwise. Trace colour-matches each channel to the
   1:1 crop after the upscale (`--sharpen`, `--gap` for seams) — a traced crest keeps its reds and its chrome.
3. **Screens.** `lune run kit new screen <Pascal> --from RpgHud` (the identity + its art come along) → edit `assets/ui/<Name>.json`: `"identity"`,
   `"quality": "hero"`, `"kind": "hud"|"menu"`, `"insets"`, `"scale": {"phone": 0.72}`, **`"focus": "<Root>.<Node>"`**
   (the one text the eye lands on first — the compiler checks it is the biggest; a hero screen without one is
   warned), then nodes. `lune run kit ui plan <Name>` at any time: the stage board (identity → art → spec →
   build → preview → look → judge → promote) with the next command.
4. `lune run kit ui --motion` → fonts (fetched + pinned) → kit art → plates → compile (gates) → build → the
   built place checked → `build/ui/<Name>.png` (desktop + phone + tablet, REAL faces) + `<Name>.motion.png`
   (six frames of the enter choreography). Fix every `[error]`, then every warning it names. LOOK at both sheets
   — then say so: `lune run kit ui look <Name> "<what you saw, what you will change>" --as "<model>@<harness>"`
   (≥ 40 characters, tied to this build; the judge packet and `kit promote` refuse a build nobody looked at).
   A screenshot of the game saved as `assets/ui/art/<identity>/world.png` becomes the preview's backdrop, so the
   screen is judged over the world it ships in, not a painted gradient.
5. **Judge.** `lune run kit ui judge <Screens...>` → `build/judge/ui/<…>/PROMPT.md` + `sheet.png` (desktop at 50 %
   + phone, each tile with a ruler = 100 real px, so a judge reads sizes against the ruler and the identity's
   floors, not in sheet pixels) + `motion.png` + `references.md` (+ `refs.png`: the real screens, when a reference
   carries `"image": "<path>"`) and the designer's claims (focus + look note) for the judge to test. Give it to a
   FRESH model (another session / a subagent that saw nothing). Record its
   answer: `--record verdict.json` → `assets/ui/<Screen>.judge.json` (several judges: the median). Bars: prop
   55 / hero 75 / aaa 88. The score is reported, never chased — `--record`'s next_action is the lowest screen's
   located gaps; fix those, rebuild, new packet.
6. `lune run kit upload` (art goes up, screens recompile with the ids). Drive it with the **Ui** runtime (below).
   Good → `lune run kit promote screen <Name> --about "…"`.

## The spec (full shape: top of `kit/lib/ui.luau`)
```json
{ "name": "RpgHud", "identity": "souls", "quality": "hero", "kind": "hud", "insets": "device",
  "scale": { "phone": 0.72, "tablet": 0.88 }, "root": { … } }
```
Node types: `panel label button image imagebutton scroll bar input canvas viewport` + identity nodes `plate`
(`"plate": "frame"`), `divider`, `texture` (`"texture": "grain", "strength": 0.1`), `counter` (`"value": 14352`,
rolls in game), `stat` (`label`, `value`, `delta`, `plate`, `valueWidth`) + components `crosshair hitmarker ring
scope keyhint ammo feed vignette` (an identity's own keycap / vignette / ring / glow replaces the kit's).
- Text: `"role": title|heading|body|hint|number` (+ the identity's own, e.g. `death`), `"tracking": 0.3` (letters
  set apart — YOU DIED — one auto-sized label per glyph), `textOutline`, `textGradient`, `rich`, `autosize`.
- Buttons / labels / stats / panels / imagebuttons: `"plate": "highlight"` puts the identity's piece behind the
  text (the selected row); a panel or an imagebutton with a plate IS that piece (a bubble button with an icon and
  a label inside — the simulator idiom). `"shadow": true | px` adds the dark offset copy under the plate (the
  sticker look). `"rarity": "epic"` = the `card_epic` plate + a `Kit_Rarity` attribute. A plate's colour is the
  contrast ground of the text on it (dark text on a cream pill passes; white on green needs `textOutline`).
- Counters: `"format": "short"` rolls 1.2K / 3.4M (simulators); `"full"` (default) 14,352.
- Bars: `fill`, `fillGradient`, `"ghost": true` (the pale damage trail that drains late), `"caps": true`
  (ornamental end pieces, `capSize`), `stroke`; every fill carries a lit core hairline (`"core": false` to drop it);
  `"track": true` makes the identity's chunky track the bar's ground and `"fillGloss": true` lights the top half
  of the fill (the candy bar); a `plate` child at `pos: ["70%", "50%"]` is a slider knob.
- **Phone reflow:** `"at": {"phone": {"size": [...], "pos": [...], "anchor": "center", "textSize": 18, "visible": false},
  "tablet": {...}}` on any node — the preview's phone/tablet sheets and the runtime (`Ui.scale`) apply it; in Studio
  set `Ui.forceViewport = "phone"` then `Ui.scale(screen)` to see it at any window size. Hide a second column, widen
  the first, lift a toast. `"scale": {"phone": 0.72}` on the screen shrinks everything; `at` moves things.
- The selected row idiom: `"plate": "highlight"` on the row + a child `{"type": "plate", "plate": "arrow",
  "rotation": 90, "pos": [-22, "50%"], "anchor": "right", "motion": {"loop": "breathe"}}` as its gold marker.
- Layout: `{"dir", "gap", "align", "justify": start|center|end, "wrap"}` or `{"grid": [w, h]}`; slots for HUD
  conventions; `"100%-24"` edge positions; `aspect`; `clip`; `z`.
- Images: `"image": "art:<name>"` (kit) / `"art:<identity>/<name>"` (plates, your icons) / `rbxassetid://…`,
  `"fit": fit|crop|stretch|tile` (+ `tile` px), `imageColor`, `imageAlpha`, `slice`.
- `viewport`: `"model": "<ReplicatedStorage.KitModels name>", "spin": 0.4` — a kit 3D model inside the screen.

## Motion (v2: a choreography, not a hover)
```json
"motion": { "enter": { "type": "swell", "delay": 0.5, "duration": 2.4, "ease": "sine" },
            "loop": { "type": "flicker", "speed": 0.8 }, "hover": "brighten", "press": "sink" }
```
- enter: `fade rise drop sweep pop bounce slam swell wipe typewriter` (a word takes the identity's `motion.enter`
  timing; `bounce` = from 60 % with an elastic overshoot, the simulator pop); ease: `quad sine back expo linear
  elastic`; `"stagger": 0.08` on a container spaces its children.
- loop: `pulse breathe blink spin sweep flicker glint float wobble` (`float` = a pet / egg / icon hovering 6 px,
  `wobble` = a NEW! tag rocking 6°); hover: `lift grow glow brighten`; press: `sink flash`.
- The strip (`--motion`) and the runtime play the SAME plan: delays + container stagger in sibling order.

## In game — `ReplicatedStorage.kit.Ui` (client)
```lua
Ui.bar(Ui.find("RpgHud", "Hud.Vitals.Hp"), 0.42)      -- Fill snaps, the Ghost drains late
Ui.counter(Ui.find("RpgHud", "Hud.RpgBox.Souls"), 15210)   -- rolls with separators
Ui.text(Ui.find("RpgDeath", "Death.Died"), "YOU DIED")       -- tracked glyphs rebuilt
Ui.show(playerGui.RpgDeath, true)                            -- enters play in order; false fades out
Ui.transition(playerGui.RpgTitle, playerGui.RpgHud)
Ui.spring(frame, "Position", UDim2.fromScale(0.5, 0.5), 4, 0.6)  -- frequency Hz, damping (<1 pops)
Ui.flash · Ui.punch · Ui.ring · Ui.push · Ui.reveal · Ui.scale · Ui.model
```
The UiMotion client binds every kit screen: hover / press / loops per node, the per-viewport scale, the enter
choreography the moment a screen is enabled.

## Gates
Errors: unknown identity · hero/aaa without identity · contrast < 3 · text < 12 px · unknown type/font/colour/
style/slot/art/plate/ease · duplicate names · bad motion / insets / scale.
Warnings (the AI tells): the habit face (Gotham…) under an identity · an indigo / purple accent · one radius on
everything · a menu entering all at once · flat panel · no type hierarchy · centre blocked on a HUD · text over
the world without an outline · contrast < 4.5 · text < 14 px · touch target < 44 px · under the identity's
`minimums` (text / button / icon floors) · art not uploaded.
Preview: text overflow, off screen, under the Roblox top bar, phone touch targets, an empty panel (a grounded
panel whose content covers under half of it — a judge's "dead space"; desktop view only). `kit verify` fails if
`src/ui` drifts from the specs.

## Fonts
The preview draws Roblox's faces for real: `kit/ui/fonts.json` maps every Enum.Font to its Google Fonts family
(OFL / Apache / UFL only, sha256-pinned in `fonts.lock.json`, fetched by `kit ui fonts`). Roblox's proprietary
faces (Gotham, Builder Sans) and its renamed ones (Garamond = Guru, Bodoni = Accanthis…) draw with their
nearest free twin and say so. Roblox cannot take a custom TTF (2026): pick from Enum.Font.
